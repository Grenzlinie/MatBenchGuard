# Data, model, software, and environment readiness

For each category record `READY`, `NOT_REQUIRED`, `NOT_READY`, or
`NOT_ASSESSABLE`:

- `data`: datasets, structures, tables, annotations, splits, references;
- `model`: pretrained models, weights, potentials, tokenizers, checkpoints;
- `software`: indispensable software/service and permitted equivalents;
- `environment`: dependencies, versions, hardware/runtime assumptions;
- `license_access`: authentication, permission, license, privacy, consent.

Criterion 2.8 passes only when all five are `READY` or `NOT_REQUIRED`.

The solver-facing resource contract is the `assets` section of
`instruction.md`. The authoring/Playground side uses `resources.json` to locate
and deploy those assets; it is reviewer-side provenance, not a solver-visible
contract.

For every required instruction asset, record its role, identity/version,
declared locator or identifier, sufficiency of the declaration, and allowed
equivalent. Cross-check the package's `instruction.md` and `resources.json` for
internal consistency. Judge whether the asset is genuinely necessary for
solving the task.

Review/Repair must not call Playground, download resource bodies, inspect
resource contents, or verify deployment/mount results. When either declaration
contains an explicit HTTP(S) URL, perform a lightweight status probe and record
timestamp, final URL, and HTTP/status result. Distinguish:

- indispensable input absent from `instruction.md` `assets`, ambiguously
  identified, or internally inconsistent with `resources.json`: `NOT_READY`;
- confirmed `404/410` for the only declared URL of an indispensable input:
  `NOT_READY`;
- `401/403/405`, DNS/TLS/timeout/rate-limit, or audit-host failure:
  `NOT_ASSESSABLE`/`AUTOMATION_LIMITATION`;
- complete and internally consistent declaration under the platform-provided
  resource contract, with no confirmed dead URL: `READY`. A successful probe
  proves reachability only, not identity or content sufficiency.

Required external data, pretrained weights, potentials, tokenizers, and
checkpoints must be sufficiently declared when they are necessary for the task.
A model/structure/trajectory explicitly meant to be trained or generated by the
solver is an output, not a prerequisite. Routine solver parameters are governed
by `paper-grounded-audit.md`, not resource readiness.

An indispensable input that is absent from `instruction.md` `assets`,
ambiguously identified, or internally inconsistent with `resources.json`
without an allowed equivalent triggers
`INDISPENSABLE_DIRECT_INPUT_UNAVAILABLE`. A confirmed `404/410` at its sole
explicit URL triggers the same gate. Do not infer unavailability from
authentication/method errors, a transient audit-host/network failure, lack of a
downloaded copy, or inability to reproduce Playground deployment. Conversely,
do not declare `READY` solely because an asset name or URL string is present;
the declaration and mapping must be complete and self-consistent.
