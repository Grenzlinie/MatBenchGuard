**Keywords:** Band Structure, Electronic Properties, Optical Properties

**Abstract.** The electronic and optical properties of the Mg$_x$Zn$_{1-x}$S semiconductor ternary alloys crystallizing in the Zinc Blende structure are calculated using the empirical pseudopotential method (EPM) coupled with the virtual crystal approximation (VCA). The composition dependence of the direct and indirect band gap energies as well as the antisymmetric gap are investigated in the composition range 0 up to 1. Other quantities such as refractive index and coefficient of reflection are also obtained by means of different existing models. Our results are generally in good agreement with those available in the literature. The obtained informations could be useful for the feature design of blue wavelength optoelectronic devices.

## Introduction
These last years, much of theoretical and experimental efforts were made to understand and determine the fundamental properties of ternary alloys semiconductor II-VI crystallizing in zinc Blende (ZB) [1] because of their potential candidature in the development of the optoelectronic components (laser, diodes) emitting in the blue spectrum for ultra-violet [2], which is, a consequence of their broad forbidden band, by comparing them with semiconductors III-V, and their great band energy of simulated emission process [3,4]. The ternary alloy Mg$_x$Zn$_{1-x}$S semiconductors are one of the matters which are used like a layer as coating in the lasers diode [5]. The main parameter for determination of electronic properties of a solid is the valence energy distribution and the electronic conduction bands (*Band Structure*). The empirical pseudopotential method (EPM) [6] is one of the most reliable methods for calculation of semiconductors band structure. It is also considered as the most suitable method to study semiconductor alloys II-VI. For our ternary alloy semiconductor Mg$_x$Zn$_{1-x}$S we apply the (EPM) coupled with the virtual crystal approximation (VCA) for all $x$ molar fraction values. We have calculated the band structure; the energy gap, then we determined the various electronic and optical properties according to this energy gap.

## Calculation
In the (EPM) the band structure is calculated, by using only some forms factors V(G), obtained by theoretical adjustments with the observations on the reflection and the absorption of the crystals. The (EPM) resolve the problem of adjustment of the experimental factors form V(G) of the pseudopotential Vp(R), represented by a linear superposition of the atomic potentials.

$$\operatorname{Vp}(\mathrm{r})=\sum_{\mathrm{R}, \tau} \operatorname{Va}(\mathrm{r}-\mathrm{R}-\tau) \tag{1}$$

With R is the direct lattice vector and $\tau$ is the basic vector.

$$\operatorname{Vp}(\mathrm{r})=\sum \mathrm{V}(\mathrm{G}) . \mathrm{S}(\mathrm{G}) . \exp (\mathrm{iGr}) \tag{2}$$

The factor of structure S(G) = 1/Na $\sum \exp(\mathrm{iGr})$, Na is a number of basic atoms.
The method of optimization of these parameters used is the nonlinear method of least squares.

For ternary semiconductor alloys of the AₓB₁₋ₓC form, the factors of the symmetrical and antisymmetric forms are expressed by the relation:

$$
\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{G}}^{\mathrm{SA}}=\mathrm{x} \cdot \mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{AC}}^{\mathrm{SA}}+(1-\mathrm{x}) \cdot \mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{BC}}^{\mathrm{SA}} \tag{3}
$$

In the same way, the energy gap E<sub>g</sub> of the ternary alloy MgₓZn₁₋ₓS is:

$$
\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{g}}^{\mathrm{Mg}_{\mathrm{x}} \mathrm{Zn}_{1 \mathrm{x}} \mathrm{S}}=\mathrm{x} . \mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{g}}^{\mathrm{MgS}}+(1-\mathrm{x}) . \mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{g}}^{\mathrm{ZnS}} \tag{4}
$$

The wavelength is calculated by:

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{m}}(\mu \mathrm{m})=\frac{1,24}{\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{g}}(\mathrm{eV})} \tag{5}
$$

The index of refraction was calculated according the expressions of; Moss [7], Ravindra and al [8], Herve and Vandamme [9] Gosh and al. [10].

$$
n^{2}-1=\frac{A}{\left(E_{g}+B\right)^{2}}. \tag{6}
$$

With; A=25.Eg+212 and B=0.21.Eg+4.25

The static dielectric constant $(\varepsilon^{0})$ is calculated using the expression:
$$
\frac{\varepsilon 0-1}{\varepsilon \infty+1}=1+v \tag{7}
$$

The optical high-frequency dielectric constant ($\varepsilon_{\infty}$) is estimated using the expression:

$$
\varepsilon_{\infty}=\mathrm{n}^{2} \tag{8}
$$

and v is obtained as [11]
$$
v=\alpha_{\mathrm{P}}^{2}\left(1+2 . \alpha_{\mathrm{C}}^{2}\right) / 2 . \alpha_{\mathrm{C}}^{4} \tag{9}
$$

Where the polarity $\alpha_{P}$ is defined by [12]
$$
\alpha_{\mathrm{P}}=\frac{-\mathrm{V}^{\mathrm{A}}(3)}{\mathrm{V}^{\mathrm{S}}(3)} \tag{10}
$$

And $\alpha_{c}$ the covalence of the material under load defined as:
$$
\alpha_{\mathrm{C}}^{2}=1-\alpha_{\mathrm{P}}^{2} \tag{11}
$$

For a normal incidence, the coefficient of reflection is given by:
$$
\mathrm{R}=\frac{(\mathrm{n}-1)^{2}}{(\mathrm{n}+1)^{2}} \tag{12}
$$

## Conclusion
In this present work, we calculated the electronic and optical properties of the ternary alloy semiconductor $\text{Mg}_x\text{Zn}_{1\text{-}x}\text{S}$ for all the values of composition $x$; with $x$ varying from 0 to 1, by using the empirical pseudopotential method (EPM) coupled with the of the virtual crystal approximation (VCA). We correlated this theoretical study with the other experimental ones found in the literatures, we noted that; (i) the computed energy gaps values of the binary compounds MgS and ZnS are in perfect agreement with those of literature; (ii) our alloy semiconductor $\text{Mg}_x\text{Zn}_{1\text{-}x}\text{S}$ is with direct gap for $x$<0.4 and with indirect gap for $x \geq 0.4$, this is also checked by the study of the bands structure; (iii) the calculation of the antisymmetric gap informs us on the ionicity of the crystal; we found that MgS is more ionic than ZnS and that is completely true. (iv) the four expressions used to determine the index of refraction according to the energy gap, give us index of refraction which decreases not linearly when the composition $x$ increase; the values obtained by the formula of Ghosh are those which approach more of the values in the literature. Finally, we affirm that the (EPM) coupled with (VCA) enabled us to calculate and to determine the electronics and optics properties of the ternary alloy semiconductor $\text{Mg}_x\text{Zn}_{1\text{-}x}\text{S}$ and the results got are in concord with the experiment. What enables us to say that the (EPM) coupled with the (VCA) is besides being a simple method, not very expensive, it is highly reliable.
