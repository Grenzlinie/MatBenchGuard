# EFFECTIVE ION CHARGES AND SHORT-RANGE POTENTIALS IN THE SEMICONDUCTOR-FERROELECTRIC CRYSTALS WITH IONIC-COVALENT BOND

A. V. TURIK and A. G. KHASABOV

Department of Physics, Rostov State University, 344104 Rostov-on-Don, Russia

(Received August 31, 1994)

Parameters of short-range Born-Mayer potentials and effective ion charges in the semiconductor-ferroelectric $ABO_3$ perovskite-type crystals ($SrTiO_3$ and $BaTiO_3$) have been calculated on the base of a semiempirical approach using the experimental values of the elastic moduli of the crystals. It has been shown that the $Sr^{2+}$ and $Ba^{2+}$ ions have the complete and $Ti^{4+}$ ions reduced ion charge respectively i.e. the Ti—O bonds are in considerable degree covalent. The mean ionicity and stiffness factor of the Ti—O bond (less against $SrTiO_3$) facilitates ferroelectric transition in $BaTiO_3$ crystal. The $PbTiO_3$ crystals have still less ionicity because of a marked covalent contribution to the Pb—O bond.

Keywords: Ion-cobalent crystals; ion charges, potential, elastic moduli, bonds.

The theoretical investigation of the ion-covalent crystals encounters with marked difficulties connected with a correct definition of the effective ion charges and short-range potential parameters. $^{1-3}$ By the ordinary approach the potential energy of the unit cell of the crystal

$$
W=-\alpha_{M} e^{2} / a+U \tag{1}
$$

is considered as the sum of the Coulomb (Madelung) energy $-\alpha_{M} e^{2} / a$ ($a$ is the lattice parameter, $e$ is the elementary charge and $\alpha_{M}$ is Madelung constant) and short range interaction energy $U$. The mean ionicity $s=\alpha_{M}^{*} / \alpha_{M}$ is taken into account the effective Madelung constant $\alpha_{M}^{*}$ introduction and renormalization of the short-range potential parameters including in $U$.

The many-parameter (up to 11 variable parameters) fitting of the dispersion curves of the acoustic and optic phonons is used in most works for the definition of the short-range parameters and effective ion charges. $^{4-7}$ Then the dispersion curves may be approximate with high accuracy, but a numerous fitting parameters makes their numerical values strongly dependent to the choice of a theoretical model and, in a number of cases, physically meaningless. Besides by this approach clear sense of the effective ion charge notion, as well as the difference between a static (used in the lattice energy, elastic moduli and crystal equilibrium condition expressions) and dynamic (characterizing the properties of the oscillating ions) charges is lost.

This difference is manifested clearly, e.g., by the use of the shell model of the ions. $^{4-8}$

In the present paper the other approach based on the connection of the effective charges and the short-range potential parameters with the elastic module val-

ues $^{9-12}$ have been realized. For the perovskite type $ABO_{3}$ crystals in the cubic paraelectric phase (taking into account the short-range A-O, B-O and O-O interactions for the nearest ions) it is necessary to determine 9 unknown values: 6 dimensionlessKellermann force constants $^{10-12}$

$$
A_{i}=2 v / e^{2}\left(\frac{\partial^{2} U_{i}}{\partial r^{2}}\right)_{r=r_{i 0}} ; \quad B_{i}=2 v / e^{2}\left(\frac{1}{r} \frac{\partial U_{i}}{\partial r}\right)_{r=r_{i 0}}
\tag{2}
$$

(indices $i=1,2,3$ belongs to A, B and O ions interacting with the nearest (at the distance $r_{i 0}$ ) oxygene ions, $v$ is unit cell volume) and three effective charges $Z_{1}, Z_{2}$ and $Z_{3}$ of the A, B and O ions respectively. Since there is only 4 equations for their definition: expressions for $c_{11}$ and $c_{12}(c_{44}=c_{12}$ , which results from theuse of the Cauchy approximation), the lattice equilibrium condition $\partial W / \partial a=0$  and the requirement of electroneutrality of the unit cell $Z_{1}+Z_{2}+3 Z_{3}=0$ , it is necessary to decrease the number of the parameters to be defined. In this aim, we have used the short-range energy in terms of Born-Mayer potential $U=b$  $\exp (-r / \rho)^{9-11}$ (it is also possible to use the Born-Lande potential), $^{13}$ which permit to consider 4 constants instead of the $6: \rho, b_{1}=b^{A-O}, b_{2}=b^{B-O}$ and $b_{3}=b^{O-O}$ , three from which $(\rho, b_{1}$ and $b_{3})$ have been defined (using the algorithm $^{10}$ ) for the binary AO crystals of the NaCl structure with the same A ions as in the corre- sponding $ABO_{3}$ crystals. The value of $\rho$ is supposed the same for A-O, B-O and O-O interactions. The potential energy of the unit cell for the $ABO_{3}$ crystals is given as

$$
W=-\alpha_{M}^{*} e^{2} / a+6 b_{2} \exp (-a / 2 \rho)+12\left(b_{1}+b_{3}\right) \exp (-a / \sqrt{2} \rho)
\tag{3}
$$

the equilibrium condition for the crystal may be given as

$$
\partial W / \partial a=\alpha_{M}^{*} e^{2} / a^{2}-3 b_{2} / \rho \exp (-a / 2 \rho)-6 \sqrt{2} / \rho\left(b_{1}+b_{3}\right) \exp (-a / \sqrt{2} \rho)=0.
\tag{4}
$$

We have used the formulae of Cowley $^{4}$ for the relation of $c_{11}$ and $c_{12}$ to $A_{i}, B_{i}$ and Z,. In order to define the sensitiveness of the calculation results to the smallvariations of $\rho$ , we have calculated the dependences of $Z_{2}, Z_{3}, \alpha_{M}^{*}, b_{2},(b_{1}+b_{3})$  and $W$ from $Z_{1}$ by a few fixed values of $\rho$ (near by the values obtained for the AO crystals).

If for $SrTiO_{3}$ with $Z_{1}/e = 2$ and $s = 0.72$ it was success to satisfy, at the same time the results obtained for SrO crystal $\rho = 0.270\ \mathring{A}$ and $(b_{1}+b_{3})=10.74^{*}\ 10^{-16}\ \text{J}$ with an accuracy to $5\%$, then for $BaTiO_{3}$ by $\rho = 0.298\ \mathring{A}$, obtained for BaO, we were not allowed to obtain $(b_{1}+b_{3})=7.275^{*}\ 10^{-16}\ \text{J}$, calculated for BaO (the last value have been obtained for $BaTiO_{3}$ by $\rho = 0.294\ \mathring{A}$, also by $Z_{i}/e = 2$). Therefore the average (by $\rho = 0.298$ and $\rho = 0.294\ \mathring{A}$) values of $b_{i}$ and other quantities have been taken for $BaTiO_{3}$ ($s = 0.61$).

The analysis of the results obtained shows that effective Madelung constants, mean ionicities and lattice energies were feebly sensitive to the changes of the short-range interaction parameters and effective charges. They were defined, mainly, by the elastic moduli $c_{11}$ and $c_{12}$ and lattice parameter values, i.e. they have been sufficiently reliably defined in this work. In $ABO_{3}$ as well as in AO crystals $\alpha_{M}^{*},s$ and $|W|$ decrease with the increasing of the ion radii of A cations. The decreasing (in comparison with the formal) of the effective charges of the B and O ions indicates the remarkable covalency of the B-O binding in the $ABO_{3}$ crystals. $A^{2 +}$ ions have the complete ion charge and not participate in the covalent binding. This result confirms the literature data.¹⁵

$A_{2}$, $|B_{2}|$ force constants for B-O interactions exceed remarkably the correspond-

ing values of other force constants. For $SrTiO_{3}$ the quasielastic constant $k_{2}$ characterizing the thermal oscillations of Ti ion near the equilibrium position in paraelectric phase is considerably more than for $BaTiO_{3}$. It is possible to draw a conclusion that the small value of $k_{2}$ and the more covalency of the Ti-O bond in $BaTiO_{3}$ crystal (in comparison with $SrTiO_{3}$ ) favours on the ferroelectric transition.

It is evident interesting to carry out similar calculation for the $PbTiO_{3}$ crystal, all the more, that the properties of $PbTiO_{3}$ and solid solutions on its base are remarkably different from them for $SrTiO_{3}$ and $BaTiO_{3}$ and corresponding solid solutions. However, it was impossible to apply for $PbTiO_{3}$ the algorithm used above in view of the fact that the PbO binary oxide exists not in the form of the cubic crystal (it is known only rhombic and tetragonal phases of PbO crystal). Therefore were have been forced to limit one self by the consideration of the effective charges, effective Madelung constant and short range potential parameter dependences from $Z_{1}$ by the some $\rho$ fixed in an interval of the most probable values $0.27 \leq \rho \leq 0.30$.

From the obtained results it is possible to conclude with sufficient confidence that the situation when $Pb^{2+}$ ion has an effective charge equal to the complete one is unprobable as the value $Z_{2} / e=1.1 \div 1.4$ obtained by $Z_{1} / e=2$ is too small. Therefore $Z_{1} / e$ most be less than 2, and A ion in $PbTiO_{3}$ (as distinct from $SrTiO_{3}$ and $BaTiO_{3}$ ) participates in the covalent binding as well as B ion. However, it is impossible to estimate $Z_{i}$ without the additional data use. The comparison of the effective charge values obtained in present work with the corresponding charges calculated for AO crystals according to the known electronegativity values $^{2}$ has shown that the charge of the Pb ion in $PbTiO_{3}$ may be estimated approximately as 0.8 from the practically equal A ion charges calculated by us for $SrTiO_{3}$ and $BaTiO_{3}$, i.e. $Z_{1} / e=1.6$. The corresponding values of $\alpha_{M}^{*}$ and $s$ have been estimated as $14 \div 16$ and $0.53 \div 0.57$ respectively (by $0.27 \leq \rho \leq 0.30$ ), i.e. the mean ionicity in $SrTiO_{3}-BaTiO_{3}-PbTiO_{3}$ series demonstrates the monotone decrease.
