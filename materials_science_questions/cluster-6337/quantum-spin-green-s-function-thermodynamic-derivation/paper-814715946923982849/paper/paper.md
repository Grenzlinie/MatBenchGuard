## ABSTRACT
In this work, the magnetic properties of the ferromagnetic and antiferromagnetic two layer spin-1/2 Ising nanographene systems are investigated within the effective field theory. We find that the magnetizations and the hysteresis behaviors of the central graphene atoms are similar to those of the edge graphene atoms in the ferromagnetic case. But, they are quite different in the antiferromagnetic case. The antiferromagnetic central graphene atoms exhibit type II superconductivity and they have triple hysteresis loop. The peak effect (PE) region is observed on the hysteresis curves of the antiferromagnetic Ising nanographene system. Therefore, we suggest that there is a strong relationship between the antiferromagnetism and the peak effect. Our results are in agreement with some experimental works in recent literature.

## 1. Introduction
Graphene is a novel material that was successfully fabricated as a monolayer [1]. Graphene is a single atomic layer of graphite. Lattice structure of graphene is hexagonal. Carbon atoms in graphene forms a $sp^{2}$ hybridization which makes graphene a favorable material in terms of strength, thermal conductivity, electrical conductivity and optical transparency. Because of its unique characteristics, graphene has a wide range of aplications including field effect transistors (FET), nanoelectronics, power batteries [2-10]. Many theoretical and experimental studies have been carried out to understand the graphene structure. Experi- mentally, Gau et al. studied the face-to-face transfer of wafer-scale graphene films [11]. Santos studied the interplay between electric fields and magnetism in functionalized multilayer graphene [12]. Mitra et al. studied the spin-glass behavior in nickel adsorbed few layer graphene [13]. Panbo Liu et al. studied the superparamag- netic $Fe_{3}O_{4}$ nanoparticles on graphene polyaniline [14]. Hysteresis of graphene was studied by Sepioni et al. [15]. In theoretical side, ab-initio calculations along with density functional theory (DFT) is widely used to study the electronic band structure and magnetic properties of graphene such as antiferromagnetism, ferromagnet- ism, diamagnetism and paramagnetism in graphene studied by using DFT [16-20]. There are also different theoretical methods used to study the magnetic properties of graphene, such as Masrour and Bahmad et al. studied the magnetic properties of nanographene by using Monte Carlo simulations [21-24]. Milova- novic and Predin studied the coexistence of antiferromagnetism and d+id superconducting correlations in the graphene bilayer by using mean field approach [25].

On the other hand, effective field theory is a very successful method for investigations of the magnetic properties of the nanos- tructures. Such as, magnetic properties of the Ising nanotube, Ising nanowire, Ising nanoparticle and Ising thin film were firstly studied by Kaneyoshi [26], magnetic properties of the cubic nanowire were studied by Jiang et al. ## 2. Theoretical method

We investigate the magnetic properties of the ferromagnetic ($J_{\text{int}}>0$) and the antiferromagnetic ($J_{\text{int}}<0$) two layer spin-1/2 Ising nanographene system shown in Fig. 1 within the effective field theory. Each site in Fig. 1 is occupied by the spin-1/2 Ising particle. By using Kaneyoshi approach [26], the Hamiltonian of the Ising nanographene system is given by
$$
\begin{aligned}
H= & -J_{\text {Central }} \sum_{(\mathrm{c} 1 \mathrm{c} 2)} S_{\mathrm{c} 1}^{z} S_{\mathrm{c} 2}^{z}-J_{\text {Int }} \sum_{(\mathrm{c} 2 \mathrm{e} 1)} S_{\mathrm{c} 2}^{z} S_{\mathrm{e} 1}^{z}-J_{\text {Edge }} \sum_{(\mathrm{e} 1 \mathrm{e} 2)} S_{\mathrm{e} 1}^{z} S_{\mathrm{e} 2}^{z} \\
& -J_{\text {Layer }} \sum_{(1112)} S_{11}^{z} S_{12}^{z}-H\left(\sum_{\mathrm{c}} S_{\mathrm{c}}^{z}+\sum_{\mathrm{e}} S_{\mathrm{e}}^{z}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
where, $J_{\text {Central }}$ is the exchange interaction between two nearest- neighbor central graphene atoms ($m_{c 1}$ and $m_{c 2}$) at the graphene layers. $J_{\text {Int }}$ is the exchange interaction between two nearest- neighbor magnetic atoms, one of which is the central graphene atoms and the other is the edge graphene atoms. $J_{\text {Edge }}$ is the exchange interaction between two nearest-neighbor edge graphene atoms ($m_{\mathrm{e} 1}$ and $m_{\mathrm{e} 2}$) at the graphene layers. $J_{\text {Layer }}$ is the exchange interaction between two nearest-neighbor magnetic atoms, one of which is at the bottom graphene layer and the other is at the upper graphene layer. $S^{z}= \pm 1$ is the Pauli spin operator. $H$ is the external magnetic field. The Ising nanographene system shown in Fig. 1 has four different magnetizations; $m_{c 1}$ and $m_{c 2}$ are the magnetization of the central graphene atoms, $m_{\mathrm{e} 1}$ and $m_{\mathrm{e} 2}$ are the magnetization of the edge graphene atoms according to their nearest-neighbor atoms. Namely, $m_{c 1}$ has three nearest-neighbor central graphene atoms $(m_{c 2}), m_{c 2}$ has one nearest-neighbor central graphene atom $(m_{c 1})$ and two nearest-neighbor edge graphene atoms $(m_{e 1}), m_{e 1}$ has one nearest-neighbor central graphene atom $(m_{c 2})$ and one nearest-neighbor edge graphene atom $(m_{e 2}), m_{e 2}$ has two nearestneighbor edge graphene atoms $(m_{e 1})$. Additionally, $m_{c 1}$ and $m_{e 1}$  have one nearest-neighbor atom which is at the nearest-neighbor graphene layer. The magnetizations are given by
$$
\begin{aligned}
& m_{\mathrm{c} 1}=\left.\left[\cos h J_{1} \nabla\right)+m_{\mathrm{c} 1} \sin h J_{1} \nabla\right]\left[\cos h J_{c} \nabla\right)+m_{\mathrm{c} 2} \sin h J_{c} \nabla\right]\left.{ }^{3} F_{s-1 / 2}(x)\right|_{x=0}, \\
& m_{\mathrm{c} 2}=\left.\left[\cos h J_{c} \nabla\right)+m_{\mathrm{c} 1} \sin h J_{c} \nabla\right]\left[\cos h J_{\mathrm{Int}} \nabla\right)+m_{\mathrm{e} 1} \sin h J_{\mathrm{Int}} \nabla\right]\left.{ }^{2} F_{s-1 / 2}(x)\right|_{x=0}, \\
& m_{\mathrm{e} 1}=\left[\cos h J_{1} \nabla\right)+m_{\mathrm{e} 1} \sin h J_{1} \nabla\right]\left[\cos h J_{\mathrm{e}} \nabla\right)+m_{\mathrm{e} 2} \sin h J_{\mathrm{e}} \nabla\right] \\
& \quad \times\left[\cos h J_{\mathrm{Int}} \nabla\right)+m_{\mathrm{c} 2} \sin h J_{\mathrm{Int}} \nabla] F_{s-1 / 2}(x)\left.\right|_{x=0}, \\
& m_{\mathrm{e} 2}=\left.\left[\cos h J_{\mathrm{e}} \nabla\right)+m_{\mathrm{e} 1} \sin h J_{\mathrm{e}} \nabla\right] F_{s-1 / 2}(x)\left.\right|_{x=0},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\nabla=\partial / \partial x$ is the differential operator and the function of $F_{S-1 / 2}(x)$ is given as follows for the spin-1/2 Ising particles.
$$F_{s-1 / 2}(x)=\tan h[\beta(x+H)].\qquad(3)$$

The total magnetization of the Ising nanographene system is given by
$$M_{\mathrm{T}}=\frac{1}{13}\left[m_{\mathrm{c} 1}+3 m_{\mathrm{c} 2}+6 m_{\mathrm{e} 1}+3 m_{\mathrm{e} 2}\right].\qquad(4)$$

## 4. Conclusion

In summary, we investigated the magnetic properties of two layer spin-1/2 Ising nanographene system within the effective field theory. We find that (a) the magnetizations and the hysteresis behaviors were obtained for the ferromagnetic and antiferromagnetic cases, (b) in the ferromagnetic case, magnetic properties of the edge graphene atoms and the central graphene atoms exhibit similar behaviors, (c) the triple hysteresis loop behavior has been observed for the AFM Ising nanographene system, (d) the type II superconductivity hysteresis behavior has been observed for the central graphene atoms. Moreover, the peak effect (PE) regions have been observed on the hysteresis curves of the antiferromagnetic bilayer Ising nanographene system and (e) the results of the AFM bilayer Ising nanographene show that there is a strong relationship between the antiferromagnetism and the peak effect. Our results are in agreement with some experimental works in recent literature.
