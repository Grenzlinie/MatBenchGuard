# On the Codeposition of Silicon and Boron (I)

The influence of the conditions of deposition on the distribution of boron between gaseous and solid phase has been calculated thermodynamically. The results of the experimental investigations in a system for deposition on rods agree with the thermodynamically calculated dependences up to concentrations of boron in deposited silicon of nearly $5 \cdot 10^{16} \mathrm{~cm}^{-3}$. At lower concentrations of boron a significant decrease of the incorporation of boron is found which cannot be explained by the thermodynamic model.

Es wurde die Abhängigkeit der Verteilung des Bors zwischen Gasphase und fester Phase von den Abscheidungsbedingungen thermodynamisch berechnet. Die Ergebnisse experi- menteller Untersuchungen, die unter Benutzung einer Stababscheidungsanlage durch- geführt wurden, stehen mit den thermodynamisch berechneten Abhängigkeiten bis zu Borkonzentrationen im abgeschiedenen Silicium von ungefähr $5 \cdot 10^{16} \mathrm{~cm}^{-3}$ in guter Über einstimmung. Bei geringeren Borkonzentrationen wird eine starke Abnahme des Borein- baues gefunden, die mit dem thermodynamischen Modell nicht erklärt werden kann.

## 1. Introduction

The simultaneous deposition of semiconductor silicon and doping elements from gaseous phase is used especially in the epitaxial technique. For the deposition of donors and silicon numerous models exist, being based partly on kinetic, partly on thermodynamic considerations, by which the process of codeposition at the very least qualitatively can be understood. On the preparation of layers of silicon doped with boron is reported by SIRTL, CORRIGAN, MATUKURA et al., THEUERER, and by CHOUDHURY. Some of the experimental phenomena observed as e.g. the significant decrease of the incorporation of boron at low concentrations and the dependence of the incorporation of boron on the flow rate could not be explained satisfactorily till now. In this work it has been attempted to explain the codeposition behaviour of silicon and boron in the reduction process of chlorosilane by thermodynamic calcula- tions which were proved by experimental investigations.

## 2. Thermodynamic calculations

### 2.1. Fundamentals for calculation

As a quantitative measure of the incorporation of boron we use according to a previous work (BARTSCH, WOLF) the ratio $\alpha^{\circ}$, which characterises the distribution of boron between gaseous phase (index v) in the initial state (index o) and solid phase (index s)

22*

in the equilibrium state.

$$
\alpha^{\circ}=\frac{\left(N_{\mathrm{B}}^{0} / N_{\mathrm{Si}}^{0}\right)^{\mathrm{v}}}{\left(N_{\mathrm{B}} / N_{\mathrm{Si}}\right)^{\mathrm{s}}}
$$

$N$ means the mole fraction.

The calculation of $\alpha^{\circ}$ is based on the reaction equations(1)-(6), in which the brackets stand for the fact that Si and B are components of a solid mixed phase.

$$
[\mathrm{Si}]+3 \mathrm{HCl} \quad \rightarrow \mathrm{SiHCl}_{3}+\mathrm{H}_{2}, \tag{1}
$$

$$
[\mathrm{Si}]+4 \mathrm{HCl} \quad \rightarrow \mathrm{SiCl}_{4}+2 \mathrm{H}_{2}, \tag{2}
$$

$$
[\mathrm{Si}]+2 \mathrm{HCl} \quad \rightarrow \mathrm{SiCl}_{2}+\mathrm{H}_{2}, \tag{3}
$$

$$
[\mathrm{B}]+3 \mathrm{HCl} \quad \rightarrow \mathrm{BCl}_{3}+1.5 \mathrm{H}_{2}, \tag{4}
$$

$$
[\mathrm{B}]+2 \mathrm{HCl} \quad \rightarrow \mathrm{BHCl}_{2}+0.5 \mathrm{H}_{2}, \tag{5}
$$

$$
[\mathrm{B}]+\mathrm{HCl}+0.5 \mathrm{H}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{BH}_{2} \mathrm{Cl}. \tag{6}
$$

With the hypothetical partial pressures $p^{*}$ of Si, B, and Cl

$$
p_{\mathrm{Si}}^{*}=N_{\mathrm{Si}}^{\mathrm{f}} \cdot \gamma_{\mathrm{Si}} \sum_{n=1}^{n=3} K_{\mathrm{n}} p_{\mathrm{HCl}}^{l_{n}} p_{\mathrm{H}_{2}}^{m_{n}}
$$

$$
p_{\mathrm{B}}^{*}=N_{\mathrm{B}}^{\mathrm{f}} \gamma_{\mathrm{B}} \sum_{n=4}^{n=6} K_{n} p_{\mathrm{HCl}}^{l_{n}} p_{\mathrm{H}_{2}}^{m_{n}}
$$

$$
p_{\mathrm{Cl}}^{*} \approx p_{\mathrm{HCl}}+\sum_{n=1}^{n=3} z_{n} K_{n} p_{\mathrm{HCl}}^{l_{n}} p_{\mathrm{H}_{2}}^{m_{n}}
$$

the following term for $\alpha^{\circ}$ is obtained:

$$
\alpha^{\circ} \approx x+(1-x) \frac{\gamma_{\mathrm{B}} \sum_{n=4}^{n=6} K_{n} p_{\mathrm{HCl}}^{l_{n}} p_{\mathrm{H}_{2}}^{m_{n}}}{\gamma_{\mathrm{Si}} \sum_{n=1}^{n=3} K_{n} p_{\mathrm{HCl}}^{l_{n}} p_{\mathrm{H}_{2}}^{m_{n}}} \tag{7}
$$

### Symbols

| Symbol | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| $\gamma$ | activity coefficient referred to the pure phase |
| $K_{n}$ | equilibrium constant referred to the conversion of the pure phase |
| $p$ | partial pressure |
| $x$ | degree of conversion of trichlorosilane $x=n_{\mathrm{Si}} / n_{\mathrm{SiHCl}_{3}} \approx 1-3 p_{\mathrm{Si}}^{*} / p_{\mathrm{Cl}}^{*}$ |
| $n_{\mathrm{Si}}$ | number of molecules of deposited Si |
| $n_{\mathrm{SiHCl}_{3}}$ | number of molecules of $\mathrm{SiHCl}_{3}$ in the initial state |
| $l_{n}, m_{n}$ | stoichiometric coefficients |
| $z_{n}$ | number of Cl-atoms of chlorosilane of the $n^{\text {th }}$ reaction |

The thermodynamic data being necessary for the calculation of $\alpha^{\circ}$ are known with the ecxeption of the activity coefficients. The equilibrium constants $K_{4}$ to $K_{6}$ have been calculated from data of JANAF-Tables. For $K_{1}$ to $K_{3}$ we used values published by us in another work (BARTSCH, WOLF). Numerical values with sufficient accuracy for $x$, $p_{\mathrm{HCl}}$ and $p_{\mathrm{H}_{2}}$ are available by solution of the equation system (1) to (3), because the solid solutions of boron in silicon in the range of concentration being of interest in this case have to be considered to be highly diluted solutions. Therefore it is possible to set $\gamma_{\mathrm{Si}} \approx 1$ and to consider $\gamma_{\mathrm{B}}$ to be independent on concentration. Provided that substitutional solutions of boron in silicon up to the solubility limit of boron are like quasi-regular solutions, it is possible to estimate the activity coefficient from the

saturation solubility of boron ($N_{\mathrm{B}}^{\text{sat}}$) and the free energy of formation ($\Delta G_{\mathrm{f}}^{0}$) of the phase coexisting with the saturated solution (see e.g. SWALIN). Approximately we obtain the term:

$$
\gamma_{\mathrm{B}} \approx \frac{1}{N_{\mathrm{B}}^{\mathrm{sat}}} \exp \frac{\left(1-N_{\mathrm{B}}^{\mathrm{sat}}\right) \Delta G_{\mathrm{f}}^{0}}{\left(N_{\mathrm{B}}^{\mathrm{C}}-N_{\mathrm{B}}^{\mathrm{sat}}\right) R T}
\tag{8}
$$

$N_{\mathrm{B}}^{\mathrm{C}}$ represents the mole fraction of boron in the coexisting phase. At temperatures $<1620 \mathrm{~K}$ silicon boride $\mathrm{SiB}_{3-4}$ (SAMSONOV, SLEPTSOV; HORN, SCHWETZ; KIEFER et al.) coexists with silicon saturated with boron. Because the thermodynamic data of $\mathrm{SiB}_{3-4}$ are unknown, for all temperatures we use the estimated value $\Delta G_{\mathrm{f}, T}^{0} \mathrm{SiB}_{3-4} \approx 2 / 3$ $\Delta G_{\mathrm{f}, 1400}^{0} \mathrm{SiB}_{6} \approx 4.5 \mathrm{kcal} / \mathrm{mol}$ ($\Delta G_{\mathrm{f}, 294}^{0} \mathrm{SiB}_{6}=-7.4 \mathrm{kcal} / \mathrm{mol}$, KNARR).

From values by VICK and WHITTLE as well as HESSE we obtain the dependence on temperature for the saturation solubility of boron in silicon:

$$
\log N_{\mathrm{B}}^{\mathrm{sat}}=-2852 / \mathrm{T}-0.214.
$$

## 3. Experimental investigations

### 3.1. Methods of investigation

Trichlorosilane-hydrogen-mixtures containing determined amounts of $\text{BBr}_3$ have been reacted in a CVD system on $\langle 111 \rangle$-orientated heated silicon-rods (BARTSCH, WOLF). The gaseous stream was doped with $\text{BBr}_3$ by diffusing of $\text{BBr}_3$ into a $\text{H}_2$-partial stream (FORTUIN). For determination of the concentration of boron in the deposited silicon resistance measurements according to the four-point method have been carried out (calibration curves according to SZE, IRVIN). High concentrations have been determined comparatively also by the methylene blue method (PASTOR et al.). For getting knowledge of the homogeneity of the distribution of boron in the deposited silicon we investigated the distribution of boron across the rod-section autoradiographically (DÖGE; GARNISCH, HUGHES). On the other hand we have deter- mined the concentration of boron at various spots of the rod by chemical analysis.

Because we had reason to believe that in the gas mixtures used for deposition a rapid halogen replacement takes place between $\text{BBr}_3$ and $\text{SiHCl}_3$, some IR absorption spectra of $\text{SiHCl}_3$-$\text{BBr}_3$ gas mixtures have been measured.

### 3.2. Results

As expected it was possible to detect IR spectroscopically a rapid halogen replace- ment in the $\text{SiHCl}_3$-$\text{BBr}_3$ gas mixtures. In proportion to the excess of $\text{SiHCl}_3$ all compounds of the series $\text{BBr}_{3-x}\text{Cl}_x$ ($x=0-3$) have been formed. With increasing $\text{SiHCl}_3$-content the portion of $\text{BCl}_3$ is increasing. Under the conditions used for code- position a complete conversion of $\text{BBr}_3$ into $\text{BCl}_3$ before or in the reaction zone may be considered to be secure.

The concentration of boron in the deposited layer of silicon was constant along the axis of the rod with exep tion of short districts at the ends (relative standard deviation of the chemical analysis $6.5\%$) and across the rodsection respectively (relative stan- dard deviation of the autoradiographic analysis $4\%$). Differences in concentration between mono- and polycrystalline districts of the rods have not been determined.

## 4. Discussion
Because of the halogen replacement between $BBr_{3}$ and $SiHCl_{3}$ it should be possible to get the same rates of incorporation at the doping with $BBr_{3}$ as with $BCl_{3}$. Experi mental results confirming this fact, can be found in publications by SIRTL and by CORRIGAN.

![](./images/812024746022535168_4.jpg)

The distribution ratios $\alpha^{\circ}$ found experimentally in this work at high concentrations of boron and their dependence on the temperature of deposition and on the excess of hydrogen agree well with the values calculated thermodynamically. The continuous slope of $\alpha^{\circ}$-curve at concentrations of boron $\leqq 10^{-6}$ (Fig. 3) is not in keeping with the assumption that a thermodynamic equilibrium state at the Si surface is responsible for the incorporation of boron. In this case $\alpha^{\circ}$ should reach a constant value at de- creasing concentration of boron. A dependence of the incorporation of boron on concen- tration is also observed at the deposition of layers of silicon in epitaxial systems using boron halides as doping agents. In Figure 4 the results of several authors are shown.

Hitherto existing attempts for explanation of the described decrease of the in- corporation of boron like the assumption of critical concentrations of boron halides (SIRTL) or the supposition of the formation of Si-B compounds with two or more boron atoms (MATUKURA et al.) are not plausible. To be more probable we consider the possibility of influence of the incorporation of boron with traces of weater vapour.

This problem will be investigated in another work.
