Peculiarities of Thermoelectric Half-Heusler Phase Formation in Gd-Ni-Sb and
Lu-Ni-Sb Ternary Systems. — Phase equilibria in the Gd—Ni—Sb and Lu—Ni—Sb
systems are studied at 873 K by powder XRD and microstructure analyses. Samples
are prepared by arc melting of the elements with a 3—5 wt% excess of Sb to compen-
sate evaporative losses and subsequent annealing (evacuated quartz tubes, 873 K,
720 h). Five ternary compounds are found in the Gd—Ni—Sb system: $\text{Gd}_5\text{Ni}_2\text{Sb}$
($\text{Mo}_5\text{SiB}_2$-type, space group I4/mcm), $\text{Gd}_5\text{NiSb}_2$ ($\text{Yb}_5\text{Sb}_3$-type, space group Pnma),
$\text{GdNiSb}$ (MgAgAs-type, space group $\text{F}\overline{4}3\text{m}$), $\text{Gd}_3\text{Ni}_6\text{Sb}_5$ ($\text{Y}_3\text{Ni}_6\text{Sb}_5$-type, space group
$\text{P2}_1/\text{m}$), and $\text{GdNi}_{0.72}\text{Sb}_2$ ($\text{HfCuSi}_2$-type, space group P4/nmm). The Lu—Ni—Sb sys-
tem is characterized by the formation of $\text{LuNiSb}$ (MgAgAs-type, space group $\text{F}\overline{4}3\text{m}$),
$\text{Lu}_5\text{Ni}_2\text{Sb}$ ($\text{Mo}_5\text{SiB}_2$-type, space group I4/mcm), and $\text{Lu}_5\text{Ni}_{0.56}\text{Sb}_{2.44}$ ($\text{Yb}_5\text{Sb}_3$-type, space
group Pnma). Disordering in the crystal structure of half-Heusler GdNiSb and LuNiSb
is revealed by EPMA and studied by Rietveld refinement and DFT modeling. The elec-
tronic structure calculations are in good agreement with electrical transport property
studies at 80—400 K. Seebeck coefficients of both compounds are 12.8 and $70\ \mu\text{V/K}$
at 380 K, resp., indicating the dominance of p-type conductivity. —
