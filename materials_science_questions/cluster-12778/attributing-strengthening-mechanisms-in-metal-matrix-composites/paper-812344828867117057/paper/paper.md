# High strength and high ductility nano-Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 composites fabricated with nickel-plating and equal channel angle semi-solid extrusion (ECASE)

Tan Wang, Xiaoqing Zuo*, Yun Zhou, Jihua Tian, Songjiang Ran

Faculty of Material Science and Engineering, Kunming University of Science & Technology Kunming, Yunnan 650093

**Graphical Abstract:**

![](./images/812344828867117057_2.jpg)

**Abstract:** The nano-Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 nanocomposites prepared with nickel-plating, stir casting, and equal channel angle semi-solid extrusion (ECASE). The microstructure, tensile property, and fracture morphology of the fabricated nanocomposites have been investigated. The microstructure observation showed the nickel-plating could stimulate nucleation and break nanoparticle aggregation, resulting the matrix grains in a smaller size (63 μm) and the nanoparticles more uniform located on the grain boundary. The ECASE could further refine the grains size (23 μm) and improve the nanoparticle dispersion. Although 5 passes refine the grains to 16 μm, it would cause the particles to re-agglomerate, due to the nanoparticles exist in the semi-solid liquid phase for a long time. The fabricated nanocomposites exhibited an enhancement in both strength and ductility, about 1.2-2.5 times higher than those of the corresponding aluminum matrix composites. Due to the Orowan strengthening and fine grain strengthening, i.e., increased the number of grain boundaries and improved the resistance of particles to cooperative grain deformation. Meanwhile, the ECASE eliminated the intergranular defects and improved the grain boundary bonding. The enhanced mechanical properties of the nanocomposites were attributed to the grain boundary control increased by the nickel-plating and ECASE. The grain boundary control, such as increasing the grain boundary number, improving the particle-matrix adhesion and particle dispersion, can be applied to the development of high mechanical properties aluminum matrix composites.

**Keywords:** nano-Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 composites materials; equal channel angle semi-solid extrusion (ECASE); matrix grain size; particle dispersion; mechanical properties

2. A method was used to describe the nanoparticle dispersion in composites accurately.
3. Effected by the liquid phase existing time longer, the nanoparticles re-aggregated.
4. The mechanical properties changed is related to the dimple nucleation and growth.

### 1.Introduction

Characterized by medium strength, good fluidity, and corrosion resistance, A356 alloy is widely used in automobile and motorcycle wheels [1, 2]. However, the mechanical properties of A356 aluminum alloy have to be further improved if it is used for other structural parts, for example, car frames. Adding reinforced particles to the aluminum alloy to prepare aluminum matrix composites effectively improves the mechanical properties [3-5]. The micro-particles adding can achieve high strength but low ductility [6-8]. In contrast, the reinforcement with nanoparticles is expected to improve the ductility because of the small hindrance of nanoparticles to plastic deformation [9, 10].

The low cost and the simple process madecasting the conventional method for preparing particle-reinforced aluminum-based composites [11-13]. When preparing nano-Al₂O₃/Al composites by casting, there are two problems: 1) Interface wettability: At 750 °C, the wetting angle between aluminum and Al₂O₃ particles is 94 ° [14], which bring the bonding between the particles and the matrix down; 2) The particle agglomeration: In addition to interface wettability, the reason also involves the high surface energy of nano particles [15]. It is challenging to make the nano-Al₂O₃ particles uniform dispersed through regular mechanical stirring [16, 17].

Surface modification is used to improve the wettability between melt and particles [18], which increase the interface bonding strength. It is necessary to preparing composites materials [19]. Yu [20] used a liquid-phase reaction to modify the surface of sub-micron Al₂O₃ particles with Y₂O₃. The results showed that the wettability of Al₂O₃ particles with the aluminum matrix was improved significantly after surface modification. The reason is that an interface reaction occurred between Y₂O₃ and the aluminum matrix, which produced a reaction wetting. Behnamfard [21] prepared Al₂O₃/A356 composites by Fe-plating on the surface of alumina and rolling, which significantly enhanced the interface bonding, and improved the mechanical properties of Al₂O₃/Al composites.

There are few reports on the dispersion of Al₂O₃ particles in the aluminum matrix, especially the quantitative description [22, 23]. Eskin [24] studied the effect of ultrasound on the dispersion of nano-Al2O3 particles. Furthermore, the results show that the high pressure of the ultrasonic cavitation effect impacts Al₂O₃ particles. It can be seen that the dispersion of particles in the matrix is improved on the microstructure. Guo[25] prepared nano-Al₂O₃/Al composites materials by friction stirring, and the agglomeration of nanoparticles was eliminated.

The metal semi-solid processing is applied to particle-reinforced aluminum matrix composites, which can effectively improve the wettability and dispersion of particles in melt [26, 27]. Agarwal [28] prepared Al₂O₃p/6061 composites by ball milling and semi-solid mixing. It showed that Al₂O₃ particles and aluminum powder are segregated near the grain boundaries. It is possible to provide an effective connection between the matrix and the reinforcing particles. Behnamfard [21] obtained a nano-SiCp/A356 composites with good particle dispersion through surface modification, secondary semi-solid stirring, and hot rolling. The SiC particles are evenly dispersed and well combined with the aluminum alloy matrix.

Equal Channel Angular Extrusion (ECAE) can not only realize equal-diameter processing of materials but also has improve the particle dispersion in the composites, and homogenize the structure [29], as well as the overall mechanical properties of composites materials. Hassani [30] performed 8 rounds of ECAE processing on the Al-4.5%Mg/SiCₚ composites materials, of which the matrix grains were refined and provided with super ductility. However, the article did not mention the improvement of particle dispersion.

Increasing the nanoparticles dispersion and grain boundary bonding is beneficial to improving the mechanical properties of composites. Martin Balog [31] revealed that the presence of Al2O3 nanoparticles located on grain boundaries, promoted remarkable stabilization of the Al grain structure, and GBs played a significant role in the overall strength. I.A. Ovid'ko [32] describe the effects of stress-driven migration of grain boundaries (GBs) on both the formation of nanoscale cracks (nanocracks) and the growth of comparatively large cracks in deformed nanocrystalline ceramics and metals with theoretical models. It is necessary to study the effect of the behavior of nanoparticles at the grain boundary on the mechanical properties by experiments.

In this study, based on nano- Al₂O₃ nickel-plating, a new type of equal channel angle semi-solid extrusion (ECASE) was used to prepare nano-Ni- Al₂O₃/A356 composites. The effect of nickel-plating on the surface of nano- Al₂O₃ particles and ECASE on the

grain size of composites matrix, particle dispersion, and mechanical properties are suitable. The focus on the refinement of the A356 matrix grains and the dispersion of nano-Ni- $Al_2O_3$ particles. Combined with the fracture morphology and mechanical properties under different processing, the grain boundary strengthening mechanism of the composite was proposed. It provided a useful reference for the research and development of high-strength aluminum-based composites.

## 2. Experimental

### 2.1 Material preparation
A series of tests were conducted used A356 alloy as the original material, and its chemical composition is provided in Table 1. A356 alloy, as the most commonly used semi-solid aluminum alloy, has a wide range of semi-solid temperature, and is also a common composite matrix, 1.5 wt.% nano-$\alpha$-$Al_2O_3$ particles with a particle size of 40-120 nm were selected as the reinforcing phase. This amount of nano particles has better strengthening effect and is easier to disperse. The nano-$Al_2O_3$ particle electroless Nickel-plating process consisted of several steps: degreasing$\rightarrow$ water washing$\rightarrow$ coarsening$\rightarrow$ water washing$\rightarrow$ sensitization, and activation$\rightarrow$ water washing$\rightarrow$ reduction$\rightarrow$ water washing$\rightarrow$ $Al_2O_3$ Nickel-plating $\rightarrow$water washing$\rightarrow$ drying. This process has detailed process parameters in our previous studies [33], and it is similar to the Leon and Drew[8]. Nickel-plating is the most commonly used surface modification technology for ceramic particles, which can improve the wettability between particles and melt. The composites materials blank was prepared by stir casting. The preparation device is shown in Figure 1(a). The stirring temperature of the composites materials was $750\ ^{\circ}C$, and the stirring speed was 300 r/min. The composites materials were obtained by air cooling. The composite materials obtained by stir casting was put into an equal channel angular extrusion die and then heated to semi-solid temperature, and ECASE prepared the nano-Ni- Al2O3/A356 composites materials, as shown in Figure 1(b). The ECASE temperature was $585\ ^{\circ}C$, and the extrusion force was 100 MPa, and the extrusion passes were 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5, respectively. The composites were obtained by air cooling.

### 2.2 Sample characterization

#### 2.2.1 Average grain size calculation


The average grain size calculation is determined by intercepting $1\ \text{mm}^2$ on the metallographic diagram to calculate the number of grains (Figure 1(d)). The grains number on $1\ \text{mm}^2$ counted (on four sides counted as 1/2, on four corners counted as 1/4). Then, the grain shape is normalized as a circle in calculating the average grain size. Finally, the three calculations' average is taken to get the average grain size of the matrix.

### 2.2.2 Characterization of the dispersion of nanoparticles
Karbalaei Akbari[34] indicated the particles in the composites materials are distributed along the grain boundary. The dispersion of the particles at the grain boundary reflects their situation in the matrix - the particle dispersion index U=U₀·K. Among them, $U_0$ refers to the uniformity of particle distribution with a maximum value of 100. The closer approaches to 100, the more uniform the particle distributes. Moreover, the particle distance factor K represents the average distance between adjacent particles and the particle diameter. The larger the K value is, the smaller the particle agglomeration will be.

(1) Calculation of uniformity of particle distribution $U_0$

According to the grid counting method, there is a particle distribution uniformity $U_0$:
$$
U_{0}=100 ×\left[1-\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left|P_{i}-\overline{\mathrm{P}}\right|}{\mathrm{N} \overline{\mathrm{P}}}\right] \tag{1}
$$

Where Pi refers to the number of particles in the i grid; $\overline{\mathrm{P}}$ represents the average number of particles in each grid, and N is the number of grids. The detection surface is divided into 4×4 grids equally, and the number of grids N refers to the number of grids overlapped with grain boundaries (as shown in Figure 1(e), C-K is included in the number of grids, N=9).

(2) Calculation of distance factor K

Using line segments to connect adjacent particles, as shown in Figure 3(f), measuring the length Di of each line segment, then the particle distance factor K is calculated as follows:
$$
\mathrm{K}=\left(\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} D_{i}}{N}-\overline{D}\right) / \overline{D} \tag{2}
$$

Where Di refers to the distance between any two adjacent particles, and $\overline{\text{Di}}$ is the average diameter of the particles. While Di≤$\overline{\text{D}}$, then Di=$\overline{\text{D}}$.

(3) Calculation of particle dispersion index U
$$
\mathrm{U}=\mathrm{K} \cdot \mathrm{U}_{0}=\left[\left(\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} D_{i}}{\mathrm{~N}}-\overline{\mathrm{D}}\right) / \overline{\mathrm{D}}\right] ×\left[1-\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left|P_{i}-\overline{\mathrm{P}}\right|}{\mathrm{N} \overline{\mathrm{P}}}\right] × 100 \tag{3}
$$

### 3.3 Microstructure and room temperature properties by ECASE

It shows a graph of the dispersion effect of nano-Ni-$\ce{Al_{2}O_{3}}$ particles in the nano-Ni-$\ce{Al_{2}O_{3}}$/A356 composites materials prepared by ECASE with 1, 3, and 5 passes in Figure 6. Figure 7 shows the metallographic structure of the nano-Ni-$\ce{Al_{2}O_{3}}$/A356 composites with the ECASE, passes being 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5, respectively. Table 4 illustrates the variation of the average grain size of the matrix and the particle dispersion index U with different ECASE passes. Nano-Ni-$\ce{Al_{2}O_{3}}$ particles were distributed along the grain boundaries, and the dispersion was significantly improved ( Figure 6 and Table 4). As illustrated in Table 4, with the increase of ECASE passes from 1 to 5, the average grain size became 60 $\mu$m, 35 $\mu$m, 23 $\mu$m, 18 $\mu$m and 16 $\mu$m, respectively. When the ECASE passes increased from 0 to 3, the dispersion index U of nano-Ni-$\ce{Al_{2}O_{3}}$ increased from 53 to 73. When the ECASE passes reached 5, it agglomerated again, and the dispersion index U dropped to 70. Compared with the non-extruded sample shown in Figure 7(a), the nano-Ni-$\ce{Al_{2}O_{3}}$/A356 composites prepared by ECASE have a dense microstructure, and the grains changed from dendrites to equiaxed. Nano-Ni-$\ce{Al_{2}O_{3}}$ particles were distributed along the grain boundaries, and the dispersion was significantly improved ( Figure 6 and Table 3).

![](./images/812344828867117057_8.jpg)

Figure 6 The dispersion state of nano-Ni-Al₂O₃ particles in nano-Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 composites prepared by different passes ECASE. (a) (b) : 1 pass; (c) (d) : 3passes; (e) (f) : 5 passes

The reason for the improvement of dispersion of nano-Ni-Al₂O₃ particles is that the grains are broken, refined, and spheroidized under extrusion, which increases the number of grain boundaries and improves the dispersion of nano-Ni-Al₂O₃ particles. When the ECASE passes are 5, the number of grains and grain boundaries is further increased. However, with an increase in the ECASE passes number, the particles stay in the liquid phase for a longer time. The aggregation tendency of nano-Ni-Al₂O₃ particles in the liquid phase is enhanced. The dispersion effect caused by the increasing of grain boundaries is weaker than the tendency of agglomeration in the liquid phase for a long time, resulting in a slight decrease of the dispersion index U.

It illustrates the stress-strain curves of nano-Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 composites with different ECASE passes in Figure 8 and Table 5. It can be seen that the number of ECASE passes increased from 1 to 3, the yield strength of the composites increased from 163 MPa to 219 MPa, and the tensile strength increased from 259 MPa to 298 MPa. When the passes reached 5, the yield strength and the tensile strength dropped to 171 MPa and 278 MPa, respectively, and the elongation increased from 8.7% to 10.8%. When the pass is 4, the nano-Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 composites materials with the best comprehensive mechanical properties are obtained, with the yield strength of 212 MPa, the tensile strength of 293 MPa, and the elongation of 10.8%.

Compared with the mechanical properties of aluminum matrix composites prepared by other methods. High-energy ball milling was utilized by Behnamfard[36] to conducted Ni and Al₂O₃ particles and prepared a 70 nm 1wt.% Ni-Al₂O₃ particle with Al₂O₃/A356 composites by a semi-solid method with the yield strength of 148 MPa, the tensile strength of 228 MPa, and the elongation rate of 4.2%; Kheirifard[37] used electroless Nickel-plating, casting and rolling to prepared the 3wt.% 170nm Al₂O₃ particles with Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 composites which were characterized with the yield strength of 148 MPa, the tensile strength of 215 MPa, and the elongation of 9.5%. It can be seen that base on the nano-Al₂O₃ electroless nickel-plating and ECASE, the nano-Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 composites materials of 1.5wt% shows a higher tensile property.

\frac{\Delta G_{\mathrm{K}}^{\prime}}{\Delta G_{\mathrm{K}}}=\frac{2-3 \cos \theta+\cos ^{3} \theta}{4} \tag{5}

### 4.2 Improvement of grain boundary cohesion by ECASE and its effect on fracture

ECASE is different from traditional ECAE; semi-solid processing is the rheological process. The grain refinement is mainly due to dendrite breakage and grain roundness [42]. The existence of the liquid phase can reduce the difficulty of deformation and eliminate casting defects [43]. The liquid phase can also react with Ni on the nanoparticles' surface to improve the adhesion between the nanoparticles and the matrix. Its disadvantage is that the grain refinement effect is limited, and the nanoparticles tend to agglomerate again in the liquid phase for a long time, as Figures 6 and 7 shown.

![](./images/812344828867117057_12.jpg)

Figure 10 The tensile fracture morphology of nano-Ni-Al2O3/A356 composites with different passes of ECASE: (a) 1 pass; (b) 3passes; (c) 5passes

It showed the room temperature tensile fracture morphology of the nano-Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 composites when the ECASE passes were 1, 3, and 5, respectively, in Figure 10. The compactness and grain boundary adhesion of ECASE are improved. The fracture morphology of ECASE is quite different from that of stir casting. It can be seen from Figure 10(a) that in the tensile fracture of the nano-Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 composite in 1 pass, it appeared as a transgranular fracture and formed noticeable dimples. Nano-Ni-Al₂O₃ particles were visible in some dimples. The tearing edge is longer and the dimple size is larger. When extruding 3 passes (as shown in Figure 10(b)), the dimple size was more uniform, and the average size was small. This phenomenon also appears in the Ding, Zhao[44]. The nano-Ni-Al₂O₃ particles were entirely dispersed, which was difficult to observe in this field.

Figure 10(c) shows the tensile fracture of nano-Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 composites materials with an ECASE of 5 passes. The uniformity of the dimples was reduced, and large dimples appeared. This is the result of the increase of toughness and the decrease of the blocking effect of nanoparticles.

The ECASE processing eliminates the casting defects of nano-Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 composites, the grain boundary adhesion and the yield strength of the materials were significantly improved [45]. The grain embedded in the grain surface improves the deformation resistance of the grain boundaries. The elimination of defects also improves the cooperative deformation ability between grains. The refinement and homogenization of dimples indicate that the increase of dimple nucleation sites and the dispersion of nanoparticles increase the hindrance to dimple growth, which is the main reason for the increase of tensile strength. When the ECASE passes are 5, the dispersion of nano particles drops again, the hindering effect of the particles on the growth of dimples decrease, and the strength also decreases[34]. The continuous increase of elongation mainly comes from the increase of grain boundaries, which was conducive to the nucleation of dimples. The hindering effect of re-aggregated particles on the growth of dimples is reduced, leading to the occurrence of large-size dimples.

### 4.3 The grain boundary control mechanism of mechanical properties of composites
Nickel-plating and ECASE were used to refine the grain size, improve the dispersion of particles at grain boundaries and enhance the grain boundary adhesion. Because these changes occur at the grain boundary, it belongs to a grain boundary regulation mechanism. When the properties of the matrix remain unchanged, grain boundary control is the main factor affecting the mechanical properties of the composites. Figure 11 describes the number of grain boundaries, particle distribution, grain boundary adhesion and the influence mechanism on mechanical properties under different processing conditions.

![](./images/812344828867117057_13.jpg)

As shown in Figure 11, the non-nickel-plating Al₂O₃ particles are distributed in clusters on the grain boundaries, and it is difficult for the particles to be wetted with the melt, resulting in a large number of casting defects at the grain boundaries. The properties of the composites are inferior, even lower than that of the matrix. After nickel-plating, the adhesion between the particles and the matrix is improved by reactive wetting. However, the vast surface energy of nanoparticles still causes some particles to agglomerate and cause defects. The performance improvement of composites is limited. At the same time, due to the existence of casting defects, the grain cooperative deformation ability is poor, resulting in poor yield strength. After ECASE 3 passes, the defects are eliminated, the grain boundary area increases with grain refinement, and the particle dispersion reaches the limit. The strength of the composites reaches the maximum due to the blocking effect of particles on the dimple growth, but the blocking also makes it difficult to improve the elongation. It also shows that nanoparticles have two sides in the grain boundary regulation mechanism. This mechanism indicates that high strength and high ductility composites can be obtained by the grain boundary control such as nickel-plating and ECASE.

## 5. Conclusion
In this study, nickel-plating and ECASE was employed to fabricate nano-Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 composites. Through the microstructure, tensile property, and fracture morphology analysis of the fabricated composites, and the grain boundary

regulation mechanism was analyzed, the following conclusions can be drawn:

(1) The nano-Ni-Al₂O₃/A356 composites, with 40-120 nm 1.5wt.% nano-Ni-Al₂O₃ particles were successfully fabricated with the nickel-plating and ECASE. Where the processing is beneficial to improve grain boundary adhesion, particles dispersion, meanwhile, the grain size is refined. The nanoparticles are located on grain boundaries of matrix and exhibit wholly embedded in the grain surface finally.

(2) The fabricated composites exhibited an enhancement in both strength and ductility due to the grain boundary regulation ability, i.e., improved the number of grain boundaries and the second phase's role in strengthening grain boundaries. It promotes the nucleation of dimples and hinders the growth of dimples.

(3) The composites exhibited good mechanical properties, about 1.2-2.5 times higher than those of the corresponding composites. The transformation process of fracture morphology indicated that the fracture mechanism of the composites is related to grain boundary adhesion and dimple movement. Moreover, the enhanced mechanical properties of the composites were attributed to the Orowan strengthening and fine grain strengthening.

Thus, nickel-plating and ECASE is a promising strategy to fabricate high strength and high ductility aluminum matrix composites with low cost and industrial scale.
