## ABSTRACT
$Mg_2TiO_4$ is a spinel material, however it is electrochemically inactive. We have simulated Ni doping in its tetrahedral network by partially replacing $Mg^{+2}$ ($0.5\ Mg^{+2}$) ions with $Ni^{+2}$ to form $Mg_{1.5}Ni_{0.5}TiO_4$. $Mg_2TiO_4$, $Mg_{1.5}Ni_{0.5}TiO_4$ and its de-intercalated end product $MgNi_{0.5}TiO_4$ are studied through analysis of structural parameters and density of states. All the three materials produce almost similar x-ray diffraction pattern ensuring structural stability during charge-discharge. After doping of Ni, $Mg_{1.5}Ni_{0.5}TiO_4$ becomes electrochemically active, as, only Ni states are found to be contributing charges (electrons) to the conduction band of $MgNi_{0.5}TiO_4$ after de-intercalation. This redox activity is also supported from the magnetic moments of Ni indicating change in valance from +2 to +3 on de-intercalation. Calculation of de-intercalation voltage for de-intercalation of tetrahedral Mg from $Mg_{1.5}Ni_{0.5}TiO_4$ indicates a value of 4.22 V. This high voltage with electrochemical capacity 151 mAh $g^{-1}$ would generate energy density of 637.2 W-h $kg^{-1}$. Simulation of formation energy ($E_f$) of $Mg_{1.5}Ni_{0.5}TiO_4$ indicates that with rutile $TiO_2$ the phase formation is probable with slightly negative value of formation energy, however, with anatase $TiO_2$ the phase formation is highly probable with high negative value of formation energy ($E_f$).

## Introduction
Researches on Li ion battery are going on for decades [1] as a potential replacement of fossil fuels in cars. However, high cost is one of the prime barriers against this replacement. Nevertheless, this problem can be solved if Mg ion battery is used as a replacement of Li ion battery. This advantage appears due to the high abundance of Mg on earth's crust. ## Computational details

Spinel $Mg_2TiO_4$ with Fd3 m [9] space group, has its atoms arranged at tetrahedral 8a positions (8 Mg), at octahedral 16d positions (8 Ti and 8 Mg) and at position 32e (32 O atoms). 56 atomic $Mg_2TiO_4$ cell was further used to simulate $Mg_{1.5}Ni_{0.5}TiO_4$ cell by replacing 4 Mg atoms from tetrahedral atomic positions with 4 Ni atoms. To simulate charged condition of this cell, rest of the 4 Mg atoms at tetrahedral 8a was extracted to form a 52 atom cell for formula unit $MgNi_{0.5}TiO_4$. We have used Wien 2 k [15] code for this simulation. $R_{mt} \times K_{max}$ [16] was set to 7 for all the cells. 14 reciprocal lattice (k) [16] points were set to map the Brillouin zone for all the cases. GGA + U [17] method was adopted for Ni doped samples and an $U_{eff}$ (U + J, where U = Hubbard interaction parameter and J = exchange paarmeter [17]) = 6 eV was applied for Ni d orbital in each cases.

## De-intercalation voltage

De-intercalation voltage of $\text{Mg}_{1.5}\text{Ni}_{0.5}\text{TiO}_4$ was calculated by extracting 0.5 mol Mg from $\text{Mg}_{1.5}\text{Ni}_{0.5}\text{TiO}_4$ in accordance with the following equation (described by Aydinol et in [20]);

$$
V = \frac{E_{tot}(\text{Mg}_{1.5}\text{Ni}_{0.5}\text{TiO}_4) - E_{tot}(\text{MgNi}_{0.5}\text{TiO}_4) - 0.5\text{Mg}}{0.5\text{Mg}}
$$

The voltage was come out to be 4.22 V for complete de-intercalation of tetrahedral Mg from the novel material $\text{Mg}_{1.5}\text{Ni}_{0.5}\text{TiO}_4$.

## Redox couple and electrochemical capacity

Redox couple can be identified by the oxidation states of Ni, which can be known from the calculated magnetic moments of Ni. The magnetic moment of Ni in $\text{Mg}_{1.5}\text{Ni}_{0.5}\text{TiO}_4$ and in $\text{MgNi}_{0.5}\text{TiO}_4$ are respectively found to be 1.7 and 2.1 $\mu_B$ which correspond to the respective oxidation state of $\text{Ni}^{+2}$ and $\text{Ni}^{+3}$. Hence, the electrochemical capacity for this novel electrode material would be ($C = \frac{nF}{M}$ with $n = 1$ and $F$ is Faraday constant [1] and $M$ is molecular mass of $\text{Mg}_{1.5}\text{Ni}_{0.5}\text{TiO}_4$) 151 mAh g$^{-1}$. Hence, energy density [1] of 637.2 W-h kg$^{-1}$ per cycle would be possible to extract from this material.

## Conclusions
A novel material $Mg_{1.5}Ni_{0.5}TiO_{4}$ was formed by partial replacement of Ni from tetrahedral position of $Mg_{2}TiO_{4}$. Structurally it is found that $Mg_{2}TiO_{4}$, $Mg_{1.5}Ni_{0.5}TiO_{4}$ and its de-intercalated end product $MgNi_{0.5}TiO_{4}$ shows almost similar x-ray diffraction pattern indicating robust structural stability of this material. From density of states analysis, $Ni^{+2}$ has been identified as a redox active center in $Mg_{1.5}$ $Ni_{0.5}TiO_{4}$, as $Ni^{+2}$ ion was seen to be contributing charges (electrons) in $MgNi_{0.5}TiO_{4}$. The voltage for de-intercalation for $0.5\ Mg^{+2}$ ion from $Mg_{1.5}Ni_{0.5}TiO_{4}$ was come out to be 4.22 V. This voltage along with its capacity $151\ mAh\ g^{-1}$ will produce an energy density of $637.2\ W-h\ kg^{-1}$. The formation enthalpy with *ru- tile* $TiO_{2}$ was come out to be slightly negative, while with *anatase* $TiO_{2}$ the formation enthalpy was come out to be highly negative and hence the formation of $Mg_{1.5}Ni_{0.5}TiO_{4}$ is probable.
