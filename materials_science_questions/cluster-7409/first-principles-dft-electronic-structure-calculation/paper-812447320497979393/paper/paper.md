# Electronic structure of 3d transition metal perovskites, $\text{LaMO}_3$ from band structure calculations

D.D. Sarmaa*, N. Shanthi $^{\text{a}}$ and Priya Mahadevan $^{\text{b}}$

$^{\text{a}}$Solid State and Structural Chemistry Unit

$^{\text{b}}$Department of Physics,
Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore 560 012, INDIA

We have performed band structure calculations for $\text{LaMO}_3$ with M=Ti-Ni within the linearized Muffin-tin orbital (LMTO) method using the atomic sphere approximation (ASA). We have estimated the charge transfer excitation energy ($\Delta$) and the hopping interaction strengths ($t$) by fitting LMTO-ASA results with that from a parametrized tight binding (TB) approach and compared the results with experiments

## 1. INTRODUCTION

$3d$ transition metal perovskite oxides, $\text{LaMO}_3$ with M=Ti-Ni exhibit interesting trends in electronic structures. $\text{LaTiO}_3$ is a small band-gap insulator; the compounds $\text{LaVO}_3$-$\text{LaCoO}_3$ are ground state insulators and $\text{LaNiO}_3$ is metallic. The electronic structure of $\text{LaMO}_3$ compounds can be described within the modified ZSA [1] phase diagram There have been many experimental investigations of electronic structures of these systems using various techniques of high-energy spectroscopies [2]. However, there is hardly any band structure calculation of these compounds in the literature. Thus we have carried out a systematic study of the electronic structure of the $\text{LaMO}_3$ series using LMTO-ASA method.

## 2. METHOD

Scalar-relativistic calculations were carried out for each of the compounds in the real crystal structure within the LMTO-ASA method The space filling criterion could be satisfied with reasonable overlaps of the various spheres without using any empty sphere The calculations were carried out using $s$, $p$, $d$ and $f$ basis orbitals. The density of states (DOS) and partial DOS (PDOS) were calculated with 150 $k$-points in the irreducible part of the Brillouin zone

## 3. RESULTS AND DISCUSSION

In Fig.1(a) we show the DOS and the Ni $3d$ and O $2p$ PDOS of $\text{LaNiO}_3$ and in Fig.1(b), the corresponding bands along four symmetry directions. There are four prominent features in the DOS [marked A-D in Fig.1(a), the corresponding bands can be observed in Fig 1(b) There are four bands approximately between -0 5 and 2 5 eV responsible for the DOS feature A The PDOS in Fig 1(a) shows that these bands are formed by the covalent mixing of Ni $3d$ and O $2p$ states. Approximately one-fourth of these bands are occupied Considering the electronic configuration of the low-spin $\text{Ni}^{3+}$ ions in an octahedral field ($t_{2g}^6e_g^1$), the feature A can be associated with the antibonding $e_g^*$ bands, while the feature, B, are due to primarily Ni $3d$ derived $t_{2g}^*$ bands The latter arises from six bands with small dispersions between -0 5 and -1 5 eV. While the $e_g^*$ bands are derived from Ni $3d$- O $2p$ $\sigma$ interactions, the $t_{2g}^*$ bands arise due to Ni $3d$- O $2p$ $\pi$ interactions The bonding $e_g$ and $t_{2g}$ bands are also identifiable in Fig 1(b), four strongly dispersing bands between -4 and -7 eV are due to the bonding $e_g$ bands, while the bonding $t_{2g}$ bands are between -3 8 and -4 8 eV, both of which overlap giving rise to the feature $\Gamma$ The remaining eight bands with considerable dispersions between -1 5 and -3.5 eV are essentially nonbonding O $p$ bands, marked C. The various DOS features are marked A-D, as in the case of $LaNiO_3$ The main distinction between these two extreme cases is that in $LaTiO_3$, the antibond- ing bands (A and B) are at a much higher energy compared to the bonding feature (D) or the non- bonding feature (C). This is obviously due to an increased O $2p$ - M $3d$ energy separation in the case of M=Ti compared to Ni. Our results for the other compounds in the series show that this energy separation systematically decreases across the series, as expected from the decrease of $\Delta$ in a related series of compounds, whereas, $t$ between the M $3d$ and O $2p$ are comparatively less sensi- tive

By fitting the results of a parametrized TB cal- culation which included only the nearest neigh- bour interaction with M $3d$ and O $2p$ in the real crystal structure, onto the results of LMTO calculation we have estimated various $t$ values as well the bare energies of M $3d$ and O $2p$ states. The $t$ values were parametrized according to Slater-Koster scheme [3]. The best descrip- tion for $LaNiO_3$ were obtained with $pp\sigma$, $pp\pi$, $pd\sigma$ and $pd\pi$ to be 0 40, -0 13, -1.73 and 0.78, re- spectively. Since $t$ between Ni $3d$ $e_g$ and symme- try adapted O $2p$ derived $e_g$ orbitals is $\sqrt{3}$ times the Slater-Koster strength, $(pd\sigma)$, the estimate for this $t$ turns out to be close to -3 eV, which is in very good agreement with the estimate (about -3.2 eV), from experimental core-level photoemis- sion spectrum [2]. The bare energy difference of about 0 4 eV between the Ni $3d$ and O $2p$ is also in good agreement with the experimentally esti- mated value of the charge transfer energy.
