# Electronic Structure and Chemical Bonding of Zr Substitution of Ti Site on $\text{Pb(Zr}_{1-\text{x}}\text{Ti}_x\text{O}_3$ Using DV-X$\alpha$ Method

YUN SEOK KIM, YANG SOO KIM, SUNGKWAN KIM,
and KWANGSOO NO

Electronic and Optical Materials Lab., Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Korea; Advanced Institute of Science and Technology, 373-1, Guseoung-dong, Yuseoung-gu, Daejeon, 305-701, Republic of Korea

(Received May 1, 2004; In final form July 16, 2004)

Electronic structures were investigated on $\text{Pb(Zr}_{1-\text{x}}\text{Ti}_x\text{O}_3$ (PZT) using the discrete variational X$\alpha$ cluster method for obtaining the information of dependence on the amount and position of the substituted Zr atoms. Also, we investigated the effect of them on fatigue. The net charge and the overlap population were discussed together with the energy gap, which is related to fatigue. As a result, the net charge was independent on the amount and position of the substituted Zr atoms, and the overlap population wasn't. Furthermore, from the calculated energy gap, we conclude that Zr-rich PZT is less susceptible to fatigue than Ti-rich PZT.

Keywords: PZT; electronic state; DV-X$\alpha$; chemical bonding; fatigue

## 1. INTRODUCTION

Ferroelectric thin films have attracted our attention to various device applications, such as FeRAM and Probe based data storage. Especially, $\text{Pb(Zr}_{1-\text{x}}\text{Ti}_x\text{O}_3$ (PZT) has been the most promising candidate, because of its ferroelectricity, high dielectric constant, piezoelectricity, pyroelectricity and electro-optical properties. Recently, there are few studies of electronic structures of oxide ceramics, which are calculated by molecular orbital methods. However, a few questions relevant to the origin and interpretation of the electronic states of ferroelectric materials remain answered. This acts as obstacles for complete understanding of observed results. Cohen discussed the origin of ferroelectricity in the perovskite oxides such as $\text{SrTiO}_3$, where the calculation was performed using the band theory [1]. Miura *et al.* reported

that the origin of the fatigue in PZT is the occupancy of the remaining electrons in the Ti 3d states, because electrons remaining from oxygen va- cancies occupy the Ti 3d states and weaken the $\pi$ bonds between the Ti 3d and O 2p states [2]. Sugihara *et al.* reported on the relationship between the electronic structures and dielectric properties of substituted-BaTiO₃ [3].

In this article, we investigate the electronic structures of PZT using the discrete variational X$\alpha$ (DV-X$\alpha$) cluster method in order to explain the in- fluence of the electronic structures of PZT on the amount and position of the substituted Zr atoms. Also, we discuss the effect of them on fatigue in PZT based on the calculated results.

## 2. CALCULATIONAL METHOD

The DV-X$\alpha$ method, proposed by Adachi *et al.*, is a molecular orbital method [5, 6]. It is one of the molecular orbital methods based on the density func- tional theory through Slater's X$\alpha$ potential:

$$
V_{xc}=-3\alpha\left[\frac{3}{4\pi}\rho(r)\right]^{1/3} \tag{1}
$$

where $\rho(r)$ is the charge density at $r$ and $\alpha$ is a parameter fixed at 0.7. This method makes use of the local density approximation, where the exchange- correlation potential is that introduced by Slater.

## 3. CLUSTER MODEL

Using the DV-X$\alpha$ cluster method, we calculated the electronic structures of $\text{Pb}(\text{Zr}_{1-\text{x}}\text{Ti}_x)\text{O}_3$ and investigated the effect of Zr substitution on fatigue in PZT. In this calculation, we chose the cluster model of $(\text{Pb}_8\text{B}_3\text{O}_{48})^{68-}$ (B = Ti and Zr), which is shown in Fig. 1. Because we investigated the electronic structures using as a simple model and the electronic structures of this cluster model were not qualitatively different from those of larger cluster models [6]. To study the effect of Zr substitution, Ti atom was substituted by Zr ($x=1/3, 2/3$ and 1) at the B site in the $\text{Pb}(\text{Zr}_{1-\text{x}}\text{Ti}_x)\text{O}_3$ system. In this process, we calculated different 4 substitution cases: no substitution, Zr substitution on upper Ti atom, Zr substitution on lower Ti atom and Zr substitution upper and lower Ti atoms. Initial charges are $\text{Ti}^{4+}$, $\text{O}^{2-}$, $\text{Pb}^{2+}$ and $\text{Zr}^{4+}$. The lattice parameters were fixed by the experimental values for $\text{PbTiO}_3$: $a=3.904$ Å and $c=4.152$ Å (P4MM symmetry).

## 4. RESULTS AND DISCUSSION

In order to discuss the effect of the amount and position of the substituted Zr atoms, we calculated using 4 different cluster models. Figure 2 shows the density of states (DOS) of each case. In this figure, the DOS is also shown by analyzing the molecular orbitals in terms of the individual atomic orbital components following the Eq. (1). $O_X$ means the oxygen ion around the central Ti atom at x-axis and $O_Z$ means that at z-axis.

Figure 3 shows the net charge for each cluster. In each cluster, the net charges of each atom are different from the fully ionized states: $Ti^{4+}$, $Pb^{4+}$, and $O^{2-}$. These results coincide with the Cohen's calculation [1]. Cohen concluded that these are originated from the existence of hybridization between Ti 3d orbitals and O 2p orbitals. As the substituted Zr atoms increased, the charges increased except for the Ti atom. It indicates that, as the substituted Zr atoms increased, atoms except for the Ti atom were surrounded by more electrons. However, when the Zr atom was only substituted at the upper or lower Ti site, the Ti atom was surrounded by more electrons than any cases. And each atom was surrounded by more electrons in the substitution of the lower Ti atom than that of the upper Ti atom except for the $O_Z$ atoms. However, the modification of the net charge is significantly small. Therefore, the amount and position of the substituted Zr atoms didn't affect significantly the net charge.

We evaluated the overlap populations of the $Ti-O_Z$, $Ti-O_X$ and $Ti-Pb$ bonds as a function of amount of the substituted Zr atoms, which is shown in Fig. 4. The magnitude of the overlap population corresponds to the strength of the covalent interactions. As the substituted Zr atoms increased, the overlap populations of $Ti-O_Z$ and $Ti-O_X$ bonds decreased. Therefore, as the substituted Zr atoms increased, the strength of the covalent interactions of $Ti-O_Z$ and $Ti-O_X$ bonds decreased. However, the overlap population of $Ti-Pb$ bond was independent of both the amount and position of the substituted Zr atoms. In the $Ti-O_Z$ bond, the substitution of upper Ti atom had larger overlap population than that of lower Ti atom. Otherwise, the substitution of upper Ti atom had smaller overlap population than that of lower Ti atom in the $Ti-O_X$ bond. Thus, we think that bonds between titanium and oxygen of x-axis have larger covalent interactions than that between titanium and oxygen of z-axis in the substitution of lower Ti atom. In other words, covalent behaviors of bonds between titanium and oxygen are dependent on the position of substituted Zr atoms.

## 5. CONCLUSION

We calculated the electronic structures of 4 different cases of $(Pb_{8}B_{3}O_{48})^{68-}$ (B = Ti and Zr) using the DV-X$\alpha$ cluster method and found that the electronic structures dependence on the amount and position of the substituted Zr atoms. The net charge isn't significantly affected by the amount and position of the substituted Zr atoms. In the covalency, they were found that Ti—O$_Z$ and Ti—O$_X$ bonds are dependent on both the amount and position of the substituted Zr atoms, and Ti—Pb bond is independent on them.

We also found that the remaining electrons prefer to occupy the Ti 3d states of the Ti-rich PZT than that of the Zr-rich PZT. Since it was concluded that the origin of fatigue in PZT is the occupancy of Ti 3d states by remaining electrons from oxygen vacancies, we conclude that Zr-rich PZT is less susceptible to fatigue than Ti-rich PZT. Also, we conclude that oxygen of x-axis (the substitution of lower Ti atom) affects mainly the fatigue than that of z-axis (that of upper Ti atom).
