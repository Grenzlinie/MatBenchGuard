# First principle studies on the structures and properties of Ti/C-doped ZrN compounds

Shaocun Liu⁵, Dongxu Liᵇ, Shaofeng Huangᵇ, Yingjiang Qianᵇ, and Zhimin Yangᶜ

⁵College of Chemistry and Materials Science, Longyan University, Longyan, Fujian, PR China; ᵇCollege of Materials Science and Engineering, Huaqiao University, Xiamen, Fujian, PR China; ᶜAnalysis and Testing Center, Huaqiao University, Xiamen, Fujian, PR China

## ABSTRACT
Metallic nitride have excellent electronic and mechanical properties, but often exist in various crystal structures simultaneously. Here, after doping Ti/C atoms in ZrN, different stoichiometry and structures of metal-carbon nitride were studied by first principle calculations. Base on the results, electrons in d-orbital take the lead in their con- ductivities. With increasing Ti/C-doped atoms, densities of states at fermi level raised, while Ti atoms could enhance better their electron transport properties. All of the structures remain high mechanical properties because of similar electronic configurations of Ti and Zr or C and N atoms, indicating their various applications.

## 1. Introduction
Zirconium nitride (ZrN) is one kind of useful and important compounds due to its special properties, such as high modulus and hardness, superconducting and corrosion resistance [1–3]. Titanium nitride (TiN) and titanium carbide (TiC) have similar structures and properties [4–6] with ZrN. As ceramic materials, both of them could be vary applications by coating or doping technology, especially on carbide or steel for improv- ing their surface properties. Gowrishankar et al. found that p-ZnO thin films were syn- thesized with different concentrations of ZrN doped [7]. Aouadi et al. studied the effects of Ag, Au and Pd doped on microstructures and Young’s modulus of ZrN films [8]. Both of TiN and ZrN coatings got gold color and high wear resistance, which made them widely studied and used [9–11]. Kim et al. studied on optical properties of TiN and ZrN by first principle method [12]. The calculated colors and screened plasma fre- quency are good agreement with those of experiments. They also found the color vari- ation due to chemical doping interestingly.

Furthermore, metallic carbide and metallic nitride often presents similar physical and chemical properties. Strong bonding energy of C-N bonds also make metal-carbon nitride showing high mechanical properties as well as metallic carbide and metallic nitride. Although carbon with various forms might coexist in metal-carbon nitride, con- tent and bonding conditions of carbon elements are very important for their stabilities

## 2. Methods

ZrN crystal structure was built with symmetry of $Fm$-$3m$ and $a=4.6176$ Å. There are four Zr and N atoms in unit cell respectively. Four Zr atoms was replaced by Ti atoms in unit cell one by one. Geometry optimization and properties calculations of all struc- tures were proceeded sequentially. The data were compared with those of ZrN calcu- lated under same preference. Structure relaxations were performed by first principle method using density functional theory (DFT) within the generalized gradient approxi- mation (GGA) using Perdew-Burke-Enzerhof (PBE) functional [17]. Ultrasoft pseudopo- tentials was used for electrons simulation [18]. The plane-wave energy cutoff was set to 350 eV, and k-point set was $4\times4\times4$ for geometry optimization and $6\times6\times6$ for elec- tronic properties calculation. Similar operations were perform for simulation of C- doped ZrN.

## 3. Results and discussions

Based on their stoichiometry, the crystal structures were named as ZrN, $TiZr_{3}N_{4}$, $TiZrN_{2}$, $Ti_{3}ZrN_{4}$ and TiN. After optimization, cubic and tetragonal crystal structures were obtained, as shown in Figure 1. The models of ZrN and TiN are similar. Bond length of Zr-N and Ti-N are 2.31 and 2.17 Å respectively.

The lattice parameters and space group were listed in Table 1. The symmetries of unit cell were changed with substitution of Ti atoms, but high crystal symmetries were kept. Due to little smaller atomic radium of Ti, volumes of unit cell (eight atoms calcu- lated) decreased slightly, which might helpful to analyze experimental data, such as X- ray diffraction or selected area electron diffraction.

Ti and Zr are congeners and have similar physical and chemical properties. In order to further investigating the difference of electronic properties, band structures and den- sities of states of all structures were calculated and analyzed. Base on the simulation

The calculated bulk moduli of ZrN, TiZr₃N₄, TiZrN₂, Ti₃ZrN₄ and TiN are 230, 244, 249, 251 and 239 GPa, which are not very different. The calculated hardness of them are 12, 12, 12, 15 and 18 GPa. In agreement with experimental results, all compounds kept high mechanical properties. Similar bonding configurations provided them high bulk moduli. Shorter bond length of Ti-N might produce stronger bonding energy, leading to harder performance.

In order to investigate influence of carbon elements, C-doped ZrN models were built and optimized by DFT-GGA, as shown in Figure 3. Lattice parameters of optimized models were listed in Table 2. Whether Ti-doped or C-doped in ZrN, the same change of space group were observed. Similar structures might result from similar electronic configurations of C and N atoms, like those of Ti and Zr atoms, which also could be seen in their densities of states showed in Figure 4. All structures are conducting. The densities of states at fermi level of Zr₄CN₃, Zr₂CN, Zr₄C₃N and ZrC were 2.9, 0.7, 2.7 and 1.9 electrons/eV respectively. Compared with those of Ti-doped ZrN, electron transport properties of C-doped ZrN were enhanced but not high, due to less outer electrons of C atoms.

Bulk moduli of Zr₄CN₃, Zr₂CN, Zr₄C₃N and ZrC are 228, 225, 216 and 219 GPa, respectively, which are lower than those of ZrN and Ti-doped ZrN. The same results were obtained in hardness calculation. The calculated hardness of Zr₄CN₃, Zr₂CN, Zr₄C₃N and ZrC are 10, 11, 9 and 11 GPa, lower than that of ZrN. The bond length of Zr-N, Zr-C and Ti-N are 2.31, 2.35 and 2.17 Å, resulting in different lattice parameters and physical properties. As previous discussions, C and N or Ti and Zr has similar electronic configurations, which could not make them expressing very different properties.

## 4. Conclusion

ZrN is golden color and conducting and very useful as hard ceramic materials, which was found coexisting with other carbide or nitride in experiments. By DFT simulation,

ZrN by Ti/C-doped could make structures and conductivities changed lightly, due to similarity of Ti and Zr or C and N atoms. Electrons of $d$-orbital played important role for electronic transport. Color and electronic properties of Ti/Zr-C/N compounds might be varied when Ti-N and Zr-C bonds are in coexistence with Zr-N bonds. Whether metal or light elements was doped, all compounds kept relatively high bulk moduli and similar hardness, indicating relative stabilities.
