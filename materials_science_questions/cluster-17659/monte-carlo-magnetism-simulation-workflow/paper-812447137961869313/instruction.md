# Monte Carlo Simulation of Hysteresis and Quench Magnetization in Random Anisotropy Models

## Problem background
Amorphous rare-earth alloys exhibit complex magnetic behavior due to random local magnetocrystalline anisotropy. The Harris‑Plischke‑Zuckermann (HPZ) model describes such systems with a ferromagnetic exchange \(J\), a random uniaxial anisotropy of strength \(D\), and an applied field \(H\). The Hamiltonian is \(\mathcal{H} = -J \sum_{\langle i,j\rangle} \mathbf{S}_i \cdot \mathbf{S}_j - D \sum_i (\mathbf{S}_i \cdot \mathbf{n}_i)^2 - \sum_i \mathbf{S}_i \cdot \mathbf{H}\), where \(\mathbf{n}_i\) are random unit vectors. Computer simulations of the two‑dimensional, two‑component version of this model reveal a rich temperature‑field phase diagram with hysteretic and memory effects. This task reproduces Monte Carlo simulations of the 2D HPZ model to compute (i) the full hysteresis loop \(M(H)\) at \(D/J=0.4\) and \(T/J=0.1\), (ii) the coercive field \(H_\text{co}\) as a function of temperature at \(D/J=0.4\), and (iii) the zero‑field magnetization \(M(T)\) following a quench‑warming protocol.

## Approach
The core method is single‑spin‑flip Metropolis Monte Carlo simulation on a \(64\times 64\) square lattice with periodic boundary conditions. Each site is assigned a random unit easy‑axis direction. The simulation updates spins using the Metropolis acceptance criterion at temperature \(T\), with energy given by the HPZ Hamiltonian. For the hysteresis loop, the external field \(H\) is swept from \(-2\) to \(+2\) (in units of \(J\)) and back, recording the average magnetization per spin at each field. For the coercive field, similar sweeps are performed at each temperature, and \(H_\text{co}\) is extracted as the field at which the magnetization changes sign. The quench‑warming protocol starts from a random spin configuration at high temperature, instantly quenches to \(T=0\), then warms up in steps, equilibrating and measuring the magnetization at zero field. The output consists of three CSV files: `hysteresis_loop.csv`, `coercive_field_vs_temperature.csv`, and `magnetization_quench.csv`.

- Output file: `/app/outputs/coercive_field_vs_temperature.csv`

- Output file: `/app/outputs/magnetization_quench.csv`
- Format: csv
- Contract: Columns: temperature (T/J, float), magnetization (M, float). One row per temperature step from 0 to 1.5.
- Scoring: scored by hidden verifier
