# Valence band photoemission study of $Co_xPd_{100-x}$ alloy films

J.-S. Kanga,*, S.K. Kwon$^{\text{b}}$, B.I. Min$^{\text{b}}$

$^{\text{a}}$ Department of Physics, The Catholic University of Korea, Puchon 422-743, South Korea
$^{\text{b}}$ Department of Physics, Pohang University of Science and Technology, Pohang 790-784, South Korea

## Abstract

Electronic structures of $Co_xPd_{100-x}$ alloy films have been investigated using photoemission spectroscopy. The extracted Co 3d partial spectral weight (PSW) distribution exhibits interesting features at $x=25\%$ reflecting large Co 3d/Pd 4d hybridization, and the Pd 4d PSW becomes broader as $x$ increases. Calculated partial densities of states of $Co_xPd_{100-x}$ compounds are found to be qualitatively consistent with experiment. © 1998 Elsevier Science B.V. All rights reserved.

**Keywords:** Electronic structure; Photoemission spectroscopy; Thin films; Hybridization

Transition-metal films have attracted much attention due to their novel magnetic properties, such as antiferromag- netic coupling and perpendicular magnetic anisotropy (PMA) [1-3]. PMA has been observed in $[Co_n/T_m]$ multi- layer films with $n\sim1$ and $m\geqslant\sim3$ atomic layers [2] and also in $Co_xT_{100-x}$ alloy films with $x<20$-$30\%$ (T = Pd, Pt) [3]. It is interesting that Co concentration in $Co_xT_{100-x}$ alloy films is similar to that in $[Co_1/T_3]$ multilayer films, suggesting that the interactions between Co and Pd(Pt) atoms would be similar both in alloy and multilayer films, and so the origin of PMA might be the same in both cases.

To understand the origin of these physical properties, it is essential to study their electronic structures. Since both the wave functions and energy distributions of Co 3d and Pd 4d states overlap each other in $Co_xPd_{100-x}$ alloys as well as in $[Co_n/Pd_m]$ multilayers, a strong hy- bridization interaction between Co 3d and Pd 4d valence electrons is expected. This will cause their electronic structures to be quite different from a simple summation of those of constituent materials. The purpose of this paper is to provide systematic photoemission spectro- scopy (PES) data for $Co_xPd_{100-x}$ alloy films and to understand the origin of PMA.

PES measurements were performed at PLS on the 2B1 beamline. $Co_xPd_{100-x}$ alloy films [4] were sputtered using Ne-ions of 2 keV to remove surface contamination [5] and then measured at room temperature in a vacuum better than $3\times10^{-10}$ Torr. Cleanliness of sample sur- faces was monitored by 2p emissions of O or CO in valence band spectra at a low photon energy ($h\nu$). The overall instrumental resolution is $\sim0.4$ eV at $h\nu=70$ eV and $\sim0.6$ eV at $h\nu=150$ eV.

Using the Cooper minimum in Pd 4d photoionization cross section at $h\nu\sim130$ eV, we have extracted Co 3d and Pd 4d PSWs (partial spectral weight distributions) of $Co_xPd_{100-x}$ [6]. Fig. 1 compares the extracted Co 3d and Pd 4d PSWs. For comparison, Co 3d and Pd 4d PES spectra of pure Co and Pd films are included. Co 3d PSWs are essentially same for $x\geqslant40\%$, but they are different from that at $x=25\%$ which exhibits two interesting fea- tures: (i) the main peak position located very close to $E_{\text{F}}$ and (ii) the shoulder structure at about $-2$ eV. These features seem to reflect large hybridization interaction between near-neighbor Co and Pd atoms, supported by the existence of structures at about $-2$ eV in Pd 4d PSWs (Fig. 1b). Note that these two features are similar to those observed in very thin Fe overlayer on Cr [7] and in dilute alloys of PdCo and PdNi [8], which were attributed to Fe 3d/Cr 3d and Co(Ni) 3d/Pd 4d hybridization, respectively.

Pd 4d PSWs in Fig. 1b show that the FWHM (full- width at half-maximum) becomes larger with increasing $x$ (decreasing Pd concentration). This result seems in contrast to the ordinary expectation that electrons be- come localized as the number of sites are diluted. Several possible mechanisms for the broadening of Pd 4d PSWs in $Co_xPd_{100-x}$ will be examined below. A first possible mechanism is increasing disorder in Pd sites in the alloy formation with increasing $x$. Greater disorder will cause a higher probability of electron-electron scattering, yielding a broader spectral function. Since the hybridization broadening depends on the magnitude of hybridization matrix elements and the DOS at the energy positions of bare Pd 4d states, its effect on quasi-particle DOS is rather complicated. Nevertheless, this will provide a con- sistent description for the Co 3d PSW at $x=25\%$, even though it cannot explain the uniqueness at $x=25\%$, as compared to $x\geqslant40\%$. As a third mechanism, charge transfer among valence electrons can be considered. But a simple rigid band-filling model is ruled out as the origin of the broadening of Pd 4d PSWs since the average number of 3d electrons per Co atom $(n_{3d}\sim7.3)$ is smaller than that of 4d electrons per Pd atom $(n_{4d}\sim8.5)$. There- fore, the most likely origin of the broadening in the Pd 4d PSWs seems to be the Co 3d/Pd 4d hybridization.

Fig. 2 compares the calculated angular momentum projected local density of states (PDOS) of Co 3d and Pd4d states of $Co_xPd_{100-x}$. The FCC structures and ferro- magnetic ground states are assumed for $x=25,50,75$, and a paramagnetic ground state is considered for $x=0$. The self-consistent LMTO band method is used in the local density functional approximation. Co 3d PDOS near $E_{F}$ is much larger at $x=25$ than at other $x$ values. In the Pd 4d PDOS, the intensity near $E_{F}$ decreases and the bandwidth increases as $x$ increases. This reduction in the intensity near $E_{F}$ is caused by the 4d band splitting, with an induced magnetic moment of $\sim0.33\mu_{B}$ per Pd atom at $x=25$, and accordingly the bandwidth in- creases. These features indicate that the Co 3d/Pd 4d hybridization is important in the occurrence of an in- duced magnetic moment in $Co_xPd_{100-x}$. Note that the trends in the calculated PDOSs are qualitatively same as those in experiment, even though there are quantitative differences. Therefore, theoretical calculations support the experimental evidence of large Co 3d/Pd 4d hybrid- ization in $Co_xPd_{100-x}$.
