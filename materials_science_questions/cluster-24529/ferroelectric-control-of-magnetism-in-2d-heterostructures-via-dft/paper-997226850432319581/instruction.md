# DFT Calculations of Magnetoelectric Coupling in Bilayer CuCrP2S6

## Problem background
Atomically thin van der Waals multiferroic materials can host coupled magnetic and ferroelectric orders. In the layered compound CuCrP₂S₆ (CCPS), antiferroelectric ordering of Cu⁺ ions coexists with interlayer antiferromagnetism. Applying an external electric field triggers layer-dependent rearrangements of the Cu⁺ ions, forming a heterogenous ferrielectric (FiE) state. This FiE transition is predicted to modulate the interlayer magnetic superexchange coupling, giving rise to a widely-tunable magnetoelectric effect. First-principles density functional theory (DFT) calculations are the key tool to quantify how Cu⁺ displacements within the CrS₆ and P₂S₆ cages change the interlayer coupling energy and the net spin polarization on the bridging sulfur atoms. The goal of this task is to reproduce the DFT‑computed magnetoelectric coupling energy curve and the accompanying spin analysis for bilayer CCPS, which together reveal the mechanism of electric‑field control of magnetism in this system.

## Approach
The central idea is to model a bilayer of CCPS with the stripe‑antiferroelectric (AFE) ordering of Cu⁺ ions and interlayer antiferromagnetic (AFM) Cr spin alignment as the reference ground state. Starting from the published bulk crystal structure, a bilayer supercell is constructed and fully relaxed with the spin‑polarized DFT+U method including van der Waals corrections. This relaxed AFE+AFM ground state serves as the reference geometry. A metastable state (denoted 2L‑mAFE‑I) is then generated by displacing one anti‑parallel Cu⁺ stripe in the bottom monolayer towards the van der Waals interface. The pathway connecting the ground state and this metastable state is mapped using the climbing‑image nudged elastic band (CINEB) method, which yields a series of intermediate geometries characterized by the displacement Δd of the mobile Cu⁺ ions from their ground‑state positions. At each of the selected displacement points Δd = 0, 0.29, 0.44, 1.0, and 1.69 Å, two total‑energy calculations are performed: one with interlayer spins constrained to the field‑aligned ferromagnetic (FM′) configuration and one with the interlayer antiferromagnetic (AFM) configuration. From these energies the magnetoelectric coupling energy change, defined as ΔE_MEC(Δd) = (E_FM′(Δd) − E_AFM(Δd)) − (E_FM′(0) − E_AFM(0)), is computed and collected into a table. In addition, for the ground‑state (Δd = 0) and one intermediate displacement (Δd = 0.44 Å) the net majority spin on the bridging sulfur atoms is extracted from the DFT spin density. These two quantities — the ΔE_MEC curve and the sulfur spin polarization — constitute the reproduction targets.
