**Abstract**—The relation among electronic structure, chemical bond and thermoelectric property of $Ca_3Co_2O_6$ and Cu-doped is studied by first-principle theory. The results indicate that the highest valence band(HVB) and the lowest conduction band(LCB) are mainly contributed from Co3d, Cu3d and O2p atomic orbitals and that 1D Co-O chains along c axis play important role in electric property of $Ca_3Co_2O_6$ and Cu-doped. The property of semiconductor is shown from the gap between HVB and LCB and impurity energy level comes out after Cu-doped and the gap becomes narrower which will increase the mobility of carriers and then decrease the resistivity. The metal characteristics of Cu-doped one are stronger than those of $Ca_3Co_2O_6$. The thermoelectric property should be improved from adding Cu element into the system of $Ca_3Co_2O_6$.

**Keywords-** cobaltite; Cu-doped; electronic structure; chemical bond; thermoelectric property

## I. INTRODUCTION
Thermoelectric materials exhibit the performance of energy transfer between heat and electricity in solid state. The most advantage of these materials is no pollution during the transfer work, and can be used in no oil and coal place, at the same time; solar, nuclear, geothermal, waste heat or other energies can be used as heat source of them. These materials can relieve the fuel and environment crisis at present. However, most of these materials have poor thermoelectric efficiency and can't be used widely. Especially, it has been generally accepted that the property of cobaltite oxide is lower than that of the conventional thermoelectric material, such as $Bi_2Te_3$, PbTe and SiGe. But $Na_xCo_2O_4$ with excellent thermoelectric power changes the idea [1], which can overcome inevitable shortages of conventional materials, such as poor stability in air at high temperature, toxic to environment and difficult to manufacture. So oxides become new stars in thermoelectric materials field and have more attention.

$Ca_3Co_2O_6$ with 1D Co-O chains is one kind of stable one in the Ca-Co-O system. Some reports show that it has good thermoelectric property $^{[2-4]}$ and that lower conductivity has bad influence in its transfer performance. Understanding the electronic structure of these cobaltite oxides is key and important to solve the present problem. In the paper we calculate electronic structure of $Ca_3Co_2O_6$ and the Cu-doped one by first-principle theory. The relation among composition, structure, chemical bond and property of $Ca_3Co_2O_6$ and Cu-doped one has been discussed.

## II. CALCULATE MODEL
The crystal structure of $Ca_3Co_2O_6$ with Co-O chains belongs to space group $R\overline{3}C$. Its crystal cell is shown in Fig.1. Lattice constants are $a = 9.0793\ \mathrm{\mathring{A}}$, $c = 10.3810\ \mathrm{\mathring{A}}$. The cobalt atoms are divided into two types, Co1 and Co2. Face-sharing [Co1O6] octahedral and [Co2O6] triangular prisms appear alternately along c axis and form the 1D Co(1)-Co(2)-Co(1)-Co(2)-Co(1) chain structure(Fig.1b). Each chain is surrounded by 6 chains, separated by Ca atoms and each Co is coordinated by 14 Co atoms, that is 2+(6+6). The minimum distance of Co-Co is $2.59\mathrm{\mathring{A}}$ and distance between chains is $5.24\mathrm{\mathring{A}}^{[5]}$。The calculated model of $Ca_3Co_2O_6$ is expanded into 66 atoms, or $Ca_{18}Co_{12}O_{36}$. In the Cu-doped model of $Ca_{18}Cu_3Co_9O_{36}$, the center Co1 and other two Co1 in the diagonal are replaced by Cu, and others, such as point group and geometric parameters, are the same as those of $Ca_{18}Co_{12}O_{36}$.

### B. Density of state

The total density of state (DOS), Co1 3d4s, Co2 3d4s, O 2s2p partial DOS of $Ca_{18}Co_{12}O_{36}$ are in fig.3. Fermi level is at the zero point in the cross axis. The maximum of total DOS is 100%, and the contribution of partial DOS to the total can be seen from the vertical axis. It is indicated from comparing the partial DOS, such as Co 4s, Ca 4s or O 2s, is too small and far way the Fermi level in this energy field and Co 3d and O 2p make mainly contribution to the nearer Fermi level, and that the contribution to LCB of Co2 3d is larger than that of Co1 3d and O2p mainly contribution to HVB. The contributions to HVB and LCB of Co3d are larger and nearer to Fermi level than those of O2p. So the Co-O chains along c axis play important role in electric conductivity of $Ca_{18}Co_{12}O_{36}$, which is in. agreement with Whangbo et al.'s conclusion in the calculation of $Ca_{3}Co_{2}O_{6}$ and $Ca_{3}CoRhO_{6}$ $^{[6]}$.

The total density of state (DOS) and PDOS of $Ca_{18}Cu_{3}Co_{9}O_{36}$ are in fig. 4. In comparison with the results of $Ca_{18}Co_{12}O_{36}$, it can be found that other partial DOS of $Ca_{18}Cu_{3}Co_{9}O_{36}$, beside Cu 3d, is much same as that of $Ca_{18}Co_{12}O_{36}$. Cu atoms doped $Ca_{18}Co_{12}O_{36}$ introduce the impurity energy level and Cu 3d orbits contribute to Fermi level. Therefore, the metal characteristics of $Ca_{18}Cu_{3}Co_{9}O_{36}$ is stronger that of $Ca_{18}Co_{12}O_{36}$. The contributions to HVB and LCB of Cu 3d are larger and nearer to Fermi level than those of Co 3d, which has the influence on the gap between HVB and LCB.

## IV. CONCLUSIONS

Ca₃Co₂O₆ shows the semiconductor property, which can be found from the gap between the highest valence band (HVB) and the lowest conduction band (LCB) of band structure. The contributions to HVB and LCB near Fermi level in Ca₃Co₂O₆ are only mainly from Co 3d and O 2p in Co-O chains, which shows the electrical conductivity of c axis is better than that of ab-plane. Ca plays the role to connect interaction between Co-O chains, decrease ionic and covalent bond strength and improve the thermoelectric property.

Impurity energy level comes out after Cu atoms replace part of Co in the model of Ca₃Co₂O₆ and the gap becomes narrower. The metal characteristics of Cu-doped one is stronger that of Ca₃Co₂O₆, which can be confirmed by the net charge and covalent bonds. The thermoelectric property should be improved from adding Cu element into the system of Ca₃Co₂O₆.
