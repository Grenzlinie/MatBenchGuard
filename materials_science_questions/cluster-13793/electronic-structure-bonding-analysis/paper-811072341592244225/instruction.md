# Lithium Net Charges in Spinel Li1.33Mn1.67O4 from Cluster MO/DFT Calculations

## Problem background
Spinel-type lithium manganese oxides can selectively exchange Li⁺ ions with protons. The exchange occurs preferentially at tetrahedral 8a sites over octahedral 16d sites of the cubic close-packed oxygen framework. Understanding the electronic origin of this selectivity requires quantifying the degree of ionization and the charge distribution at the two distinct lithium sites. This task focuses on computing net atomic charges on lithium in the tetrahedral and octahedral coordination environments of the spinel phase Li₁.₃₃Mn₁.₆₇O₄. The goal is to determine whether the tetrahedral lithium carries a higher net positive charge than the octahedral lithium, and to measure the magnitude of these charges through first-principles cluster calculations.

## Approach
The approach uses first-principles electronic structure calculations on embedded cluster models cut from the spinel crystal lattice (space group Fd 3̅m, a = 8.19 Å). Two clusters are constructed: one centered on a tetrahedral lithium site and one centered on an octahedral lithium site. Each cluster is embedded in a Madelung potential generated by point charges at the external atomic positions to mimic the crystalline environment. A self-consistent field (SCF) calculation is performed on each cluster using quantum chemistry or density functional theory software with an appropriate basis set and exchange-correlation functional. After convergence, Mulliken (or Löwdin) population analysis is carried out to obtain net atomic charges. The average charge on lithium atoms at tetrahedral sites is compared with the average charge at octahedral sites. The key comparison is between the two site-specific net charges.

## Reproduction target
The objective is to compute the average net Mulliken (or Löwdin) charge on lithium at tetrahedral sites and the average net charge on lithium at octahedral sites in Li₁.₃₃Mn₁.₆₇O₄, and to verify the relative ordering of the two charges. The result must be written to the file “net_charges.json” containing exactly two numerical values: "tetrahedral_Li_charge" and "octahedral_Li_charge".
