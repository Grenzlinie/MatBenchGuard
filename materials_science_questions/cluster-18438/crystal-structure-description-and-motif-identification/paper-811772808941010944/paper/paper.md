Macropolyhedral Boron-Containing Cluster Chemistry. Triple Cluster Fusion and the Molecular Structure of $[(PMe_3)_2IrB_{26}H_{24}Ir(CO)(PMe_3)_2]$. A 28-Vertex Metallaborane Cluster with a Polyboron Core.

— The title compound is obtained by thermolysis of $[(CO)(PMe_3)_2HIrB_8H_{12}]$ in molten $B_{10}H_{14}$ ($N_2$, $134\ ^{\circ}C$). It is a triple cluster species having a closo twelve-vertex $[IrB_{11}]$ and a closo ten-vertex $[IrB_9]$ as units fused by an $[IrB_2]$ triangular face and wedge interboron linkage that also binds a nido nine-vertex $[IrB_8]$ subcluster. The resulting $[IrB_{26}]$ cluster has a novel five B atom core. The single crystal XRD shows a monoclinic structure (space group $P2_1/n$, $Z=4$). — (BOULD, J.; KENNEDY, J. D.; BARTON, L.; RATH, N. P.; Chem. Commun. (Cambridge) (1997) 24, 2405-2406; Sch. Chem., Univ. Leeds, Leeds LS2 9JT, UK; EN)
