Crystal Structure, Vibrational Spectrum and Thermal Behavior of the Ammonium Hexathiohypodiphosphate Dihydrate $(NH_{4})_{4}P_{2}S_{6}\cdot 2H_{2}O$. — The title compound crystallizes in the monoclinic space group $P2_{1}/c$ with $Z = 4$ (single crystal XRD). The structure contains isolated, ethane-like $P_{2}S_{6}^{4-}$ anions with staggered conformation, $NH_{4}^{+}$ cation, and water molecules, all involved in an extended hydrogen network. — (GJIKAJ*, M.; BROCKNER, W.; Z. Naturforsch., B: Chem. Sci. 62 (2007) 8, 1102-1106; Inst. Anorg. Anal. Chem., TU Clausthal, D-38678 Clausthal-Zellerfeld, Germany; Eng.) — Schramke
