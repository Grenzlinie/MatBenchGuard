The manufacturing of nanoscale devices with sizes smaller than 100 nm is founded on the quantum physical phenomena. We proposed the new nanoscale device with photoacoustic switching. The memory cell can be made by means of two thin silicon surface layers one of which contains oxygen in Si-O-Si bonding. The charge storage is caused by inserting on clean silicon layer the oxygen incorporated with silicon. The mechanical deformation of upper oxidized silicon layer results in shift of atomic positions. The oxygen appearance on the silicon surface is reflected on electronic structure as new defect level inside band gap. The electrons are stored on this oxygen related level with energy position Ec-0.18 eV. By applied bias voltage we realize the erase procedure by removing the stored electron. The silicon surface should be prepared because the oxygen incorporation depends on the chemical properties. The electronic structure of oxidized silicon surfaces with (111) and (100) orientation was tested by using second harmonic generation response. The characteristic time of storage, 1 ns, was measured by using the laser time-resolved short pulse spectroscopy. We used modelocked mode of laser system with pulse duration 120 ps. The speed of switching was approximately $10^{13}$ Hz.

Keywords: oxygen incorporation, Si-O-Si bridge, molecular tautomeria, dipole moment.

## 1. INTRODUCTION

During last two decades the silicon surface was intensively studied. The oxygen incorporation on silicon surface was studied by other researchers with optical second-harmonic generation (SHG) spectroscopy method [1,2]. The strain was seen to cause redshift of 40 meV compared with the bulk silicon. The various electronic transitions at Si(111)/SiO2 compose into broaden spectral peaks around 3.37 eV and 3.26 eV. It is assigned that the peak position energy 3.26 eV corresponds to the optical response of strained silicon monolayers [1]. The resonance band at 3.37 eV in the linear susceptibility of bulk silicon is resulted due to the E1 direct band gap transitions [2]. The bond length distortion causes the surface strain. This is because of oxygen atoms incorporation and structural relaxation of surface Si atoms.

Shklyaev and coworkers [3] proposed the model of oxygen nucleation on the silicon surface. There are several critical conditions in oxygen interaction with silicon. The kinetic of Si surface oxidation was studied by SHG method. However, there are no evident information about earlier lateral growth and oxygen adatoms. But the oxygen adsorption on silicon surface for different kind of crystal orientation is varied. Therefore, the oxygen incorporation with silicon surface layer during adsorption is very important for future nucleation process. Because, in this work we study the chemical structure of oxidized silicon surfaces with orientation (111) and (100), the electronic structure of surface layers by SHG method, and hyperfine splitting of orbitals by time-resolved laser picosecond spectroscopy.

### III.MODELS OF A CENTER

#### 3.1 A-center on the Si(111) surface

The model of A-center consisted of one oxygen atom between four coupled silicon atoms is shown on Fig. 1. The pump-probe laser picosecond spectroscopy data with phonon stimulated stress pulses [9] confirm the existance of the quantum interference of closed neighbor levels resulted in quantum beats phenomena (see Fig. 5.). The estimation of the energy difference of levels position is around $5\ \mu$eV. Picosecond acoustic longitudinal stress pulses cause the small displacement of interface media surraunding the silicon nanocrystals. Because, the Si-O-Si bridges were shakened and oxygen atom oscillates between two stable excess states of four coupled pairs of silicon atoms. The main principle of qubit desing bases on switching between two silicon coupled atoms with oxygen from one pair of silicon atoms to another.

### 3.2 Model of Quantum Oscillator
The energy of Si-O-Si bridge interaction can be written in form of Morse function [10]:

$$
U=U_{0}(1-\exp (-\alpha(x-d)))^{2}+U_{0}(1-\exp (-\alpha(x+d)))^{2} \tag{5}
$$

where $U_0$ is the energy of Si-O (4.5 eV), $\alpha$ is coefficient. The oxygen atom in A-center oscillates between two points of stable positions. The change of the spatial position of oxygen atom can be described by using simple model of oscillator

$$
\ddot{x}+\gamma \dot{x}+\omega_{0}^{2} x=f_{0} \cos (\Omega t) \tag{6}
$$

where the eigen frequency of oscillating oxygen atom can be expressed as $\omega_{0}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{d^{2} U}{d x^{2}}\right)} ; \gamma=\frac{k T}{h}$, k is Boltzman constant.

The solution of equation by the resonance conditions (by the $\omega_{0 / \gamma}>>1$ ) can be written as

$$
x(t)=e^{-\gamma t}\left(\frac{f_{0}}{2 \omega_{0}^{2}} \sin \left(\omega_{0} t\right)+\frac{f_{0}}{2 \gamma \omega_{0}} \cos \left(\omega_{0} t\right)\right)-\frac{f_{0}}{2 \gamma \omega_{0}} \cos \left(\omega_{0} t\right) \approx-\frac{f_{0}}{2 \gamma \omega_{0}}\left(1-e^{-\gamma t}\right) \cos \left(\omega_{0} t\right). \tag{7}
$$

The eigenfrequency value for Si-O bond was estimated by means of FTIR spectroscopy technique. It equals approximately to $3^{*} 10^{13} \mathrm{~Hz}$. The resonance will be by the equality between the phonon frequency $\Omega$ and $\omega_{0}$ value.

### 3.3 Two coupled oscillators
For A defect center with oxygen atom we proposed the model of two coupled oscillators:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \ddot{x}_{1}+\gamma \dot{x}_{1}+\omega_{0}^{2} x_{1}+a x_{2}=f_{0} \cos (\Omega t) \\
& \ddot{x}_{2}+\gamma \dot{x}_{2}+\omega_{0}^{2} x_{2}+b x_{1}=f_{0} \cos (\Omega t)
\end{aligned} \tag{8}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& U=U_{0}\left(1-\exp \left(-\alpha\left(x_{1}-d\right)\right)\right)^{2}+U_{0}\left(1-\exp \left(-\alpha\left(x_{1}+d\right)\right)\right)^{2}+ \\
& U_{0}\left(1-\exp \left(-\alpha\left(x_{2}-d\right)\right)\right)^{2}+U_{0}\left(1-\exp \left(-\alpha\left(x_{2}+d\right)\right)\right)^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

where $x_1$ and $x_2$ are the displacement values for oxygen atom position relatively Si-Si$_\text{A}$ and Si-Si$_\text{B}$ pairs.
The solution of the system of two two-order differential equations contains two eigenfrequencies $\text{w}_1$ and $\text{w}_2$. The oxygen atom oscillates in A center with two different frequencies corresponded two energy levels for oxygen atom.
The quantum beats observed in picosecond laser spectroscopy experiments shown on Fig. 5 caused by quantum interference of two energetic states of oxygen atom.

According to the criteria proposed by Lakhno [12] based on solution of Shredinger equation for electron-dipole interaction the localized bonded states for electron can exist only if the value of dipole moment of molecular dipole is more than critical value μ<sub>critical</sub>=0.318 D. In the case of μ<μ<sub>critical</sub> there is no localized bonded states. It is clear that for μ<sup>0</sup><sub>Si-O</sub>>μ<sub>critical</sub> there is localized bonded state for electron, but for μ<sup>0</sup><sub>Si-O-Si</sub><μ<sub>critical</sub> such state can not exist. Because the Si-O bonding by zero displacement generates localized electron states on Si(111) surface which can cause the troubles for writing and storing procedures of memory cell. By the annealing of Si(111) substrate to the temperature more than 250°C we can eliminate the Si-O bonds and produce the strong siloxane bonding. Therefore, it is possible to prepare the oxidized Si(111) surface with strict siloxane Si-O-Si bridges without localized electron states on surface. Applying the electrical (or even acoustical [9]) field stimulates A center production which cause the appearance of localized electron states.

## V.MEMORY CELL

### 5.1 Memory switching

The polarization wave generated by laser radiation is given by

$$
P=\tilde{\tilde{P}}(z) \exp (i(\Omega t-k z)) \exp (-\gamma t)
\tag{13}
$$

We can estimate the yield of interference of two polarization waves with shift of phases around π.

$$
P_{\text {sum }}=P_{+}+P_{-}=\tilde{\tilde{P}}_{\text {sum }} \cos (\Omega t+\varphi)
\tag{14}
$$

$$
\tilde{\tilde{P}} \exp (i \varphi)=\tilde{\tilde{P}}_{-} \exp \left(i \varphi_{1}\right)+\tilde{\tilde{P}}_{+} \exp \left(i \varphi_{2}\right)
$$

$$
\tilde{\tilde{P}}^{2}=\tilde{\tilde{P}}_{-}^{2}+\tilde{\tilde{P}}_{+}^{2}+2 \tilde{\tilde{P}}_{+} \tilde{\tilde{P}}_{-} \cos \left(\varphi_{2}-\varphi_{1}\right)
$$

The spatial distribution of charges for polarized molecular bridges Si-O-Si on the clean Si(111) surface is used for electrical charge trapping. The potential energy barrier for electron trapping can be estimated as

$$
W \approx 2 \tilde{\tilde{P}}_{+} \tilde{\tilde{P}}_{-} \sin \left(\varphi_{2}-\varphi_{1}\right) \tilde{\tilde{\Delta \varphi}}
\tag{15}
$$

$$
\tilde{\tilde{\Delta \varphi}}=\frac{\pi \Delta d}{\lambda}
$$

where $\Delta d$ is the cell size, $\lambda$ is the wavelegth of polarization wave. By applied electrical field $10^{7}$ V/m W≈100 μeV; and by the value of field $10^{8}$ V/cm is 1 meV.

### 5.3 Deformation by photoacoustic pulse

We can use photoacoustic pulse for sure storing and erasing. The energy of interaction of acoustic phonon with Si crystal lattice can be written into the form:

$$
H_{e-p h}=\Xi \Delta
\tag{16}
$$

where $\Xi$ is the deformation potential, $\Delta$ is deformation of the Si(111) structure. The necessary value for erasing procedure should be around 0.1-0.2 eV [14]. The ultrashort acoustic pulses generated by incident picosecond pulsed laser beam is 5 ps.
