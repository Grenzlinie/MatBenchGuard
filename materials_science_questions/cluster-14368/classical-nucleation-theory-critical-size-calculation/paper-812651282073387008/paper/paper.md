### Condition 1
G=0
G<0xE1><0xB5><0xA2>=0, G<0xE1><0xB5><0xAC>=0
r=0
S=S<0xE2><0x82><0x90>>1
No crystals are formed yet, and thus no energy is associated to the new phase.

### Condition 2
G=0
G<0xE1><0xB5><0xA2>=G<0xE2><0x82><0x82>, G<0xE1><0xB5><0xAC>=-G<0xE2><0x82><0x82>
r=r<0xE2><0x82><0x82>
S=S<0xE2><0x82><0x82>>1
During crystal growth, the system is approaching the solubility limit and a net increase of energy is observed. Before this occurrence, the system stabilizes itself at the equilibrium size (minimum).

### Condition 3
G=G<0xE1><0xB5><0xA2>
G<0xE1><0xB5><0xAC>=0
r=r<0xE1><0xB5><0xB9><0xE1><0xB5><0xB2><0xE1><0xB5><0xAF>
S=1
Here the system reached the solubility limit: there is no more driving force in term of volume and the only valid contribution is the surface term, which is positive. Reaching this point is very unlikely, even if the system stabilizes itself in a condition close to this point in terms of size.

### Condition 4
G>G<0xE1><0xB5><0xA2>
G<0xE1><0xB5><0xAC>>0
r=r<0xE1><0xB5><0xB9><0xE1><0xB5><0xB8><0xE1><0xB5><0xB9>
S=0
At this point, all monomer has been transferred to the crystals and none is left in solution: reaching this condition is impossible (this is the reason it is not a definite point in the plot). The system requires +∞ amount of energy (asymptote) and thus the probability is zero.
