# Density Functional Theory Study of $\mathbf{Gd}_n\mathbf{O}_3$ ($\mathbf{n=1-5}$) Clusters

Lin Xu$^\text{a}$, ZongLin Liu$^\text{b}$, HongKuan Yuan$^\text{c\dagger}$

School of Southwest University,
Chongqing, 400715, People's Republic of China

$\dagger$ Corresponding author.

Keywords: DFT, $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ cluster, geometries, electronic and magnetic properties

Abstract. The geometries, stabilities, electronic and magnetic properties of small $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ ($n$=1-5) clusters have been systematically studied by using density functional theory with the generalized gradient approximation. We found that the Gd atoms and O atoms in $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ clusters prefer three and two coordination, respectively, which origin from the electronic configurations of Gd and O atoms. The results show that $\text{Gd}_2\text{O}_3$ cluster is more stable than its respective neighbors, which is reflected from its high average binding energy and high HOMO-LUMO gap. In addition, we calculate the magnetic properties of $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ clusters. The local magnetic moments of the Gd atom in the $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ clusters exhibit a weak dependence on the O atoms, which are slightly enhanced with the increasing of the number of Gd atom.

## Introduction

During the past few years, gadolinium oxide clusters have attracted intensive attentions from experimental and theoretical aspects, due to their potential applications in spintronic, optic, biomedicine, and as storage media. Recently, Pedersen et al. [9] and Söderlind et al. [11] have predicted that gadolinium oxide clusters may function as the contrast agents for medical magnetic resonance imaging. By using citric acid as a capping agent, Rahman et al. have successfully synthesized the small $\text{Gd}_2\text{O}_3$ nanoparticles (1-3nm) and found to be of a narrow size distribution [3]. Interestingly, Huang et al. have revealed that that the $\text{Gd}_2\text{O}_3$ nanospheres are hollow or porous structures, so that these structures are promising of encapsulating drugs for controlled release during magnetic delivery. They have also predicted that $\text{Gd}_2\text{O}_3$ nanoclusters have the capable of providing high-contrast MR images [2]. From the theoretical sides, the structures and magnetic properties of $\text{Gd}_2\text{O}_3$ and $\text{Gd}_8\text{O}_{12}$ clusters have been investigated [1,6,7], where they found that the antiferromagnetic couplings between adjacent Gd ($4f^7$) spins are preferred in their ground state structures. Although there are intensive investigations on gadolinium oxide clusters, the systemic studies on small gadolinium oxide cluster is absence. In order to further understand the geometrical evolution and magnetic variations of gadolinium oxide clusters, and to underline their intrinsic characters that are electronic dependence, a comprehensive theoretical calculation on the trioxide species is necessary.

## Computational Details

All spin-polarized first-principle calculations are performed on the structural candidates of $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ clusters based on the density functional theory (DFT) within the framework of generalized-gradient approximation (GGA) in the form of Perdew, Wang, and Becke (PW91) [5] exchange-correlation potential. In the electronic structure calculations, we have chosen a basis set composed of double numerical basis with polarized function (DNP). For accurate evaluation of the charge density, we have chosen the octuple scheme for the multipolar fitting procedure and a fine grid scheme for numerical integration. The convergence criterion of optimization is set to $1\times10^{-5}$ eV/Å for the energy gradient and $5\times10^{-4}$Å for the atomic displacement. The charge density is converged to $1\times$

$10^{-6}$, which allow a total energy convergence of $1\times10^{-5}\text{eV}$. The direct inversion in an iterative subspace (DIIS) approach and the thermal smearing scheme with a value of 0.005 Ha, have been chosen. The geometrical relaxations are done without symmetry constraint. The reliability of the current computational scheme has been verified in our previous work on $\text{Gd}_{13}$ [8]. In addition, our obtained bond length of $\text{Gd}_2\text{O}_3$ is 0.202nm, which also is comparable to available theoretical value of 0.211nm [6]. This indicates that our method is reasonably to describe the properties of $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ clusters.

## Results and Discussions

### Geometrical Structures
Fig.1 shows the structures, symmetries, and energy difference on each of the low-lying isomeric $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ clusters. The isomers are labeled with two indices: the first (number) represents the number of Gd atoms in cluster and the second (letter) ranks the isomers in decreasing order of binding energy.
The ground state structure of $\text{GdO}_3$ is a planar structure ($D_{3h}$) with three equal Gd-O bond lengths of 0.202 nm. It is a spin quintet state with twofold HOMO fully filled and has a binding energy of 3.25eV/atom. The three-dimensional tetrahedron structure ($C_{3v}$) and planar butterfly-like structure ($C_{2v}$) is proved to be the second and third low-lying structure, with energy 0.001eV and 0.065eV above the ground-state structure, respectively.
For $\text{Gd}_2\text{O}_3$, we have optimized the geometries subject to the square pyramid of $C_{2v}$, $C_{4v}$, $C_1$, and the triangular bipyramid of $C_s$, $C_{2v}$, $C_{3v}$, and $D_{3h}$ symmetries. It is shown that the lowest energy structure of $\text{Gd}_2\text{O}_3$ cluster is a standard trigonal bipyramid ($D_{3h}$) with a Gd-O average bond length of 0.217nm and a binding energy of 4.44eV/atom. It is a spin fifteen state with a singlet HOMO. The results are in agreement with previous theoretical studies [6]. The tetrahedron-like ($C_1$) and planar ($C_s$) are 0.159eV/atom and 0.349eV/atom above the lowest energy structure, respectively. The significant energy differences between the three-coordinated tetrahedron and the low-coordinated structures indicate strong tendency of forming three single covalent bonds for each Gd atom.
In the case of $\text{Gd}_3\text{O}_3$, all possible structural configurations are tried to identify the ground state. The octahedron, pentagonal pyramid, capped trigonal bipyramid, two planar structures, linear chain as well as triangular prism with different symmetries are optimized. Among those isomers, the planar hexagon structure $D_{3h}$ with an average bond length of 0.214nm and a binding energy of 4.39 eV/atom is found to be the lowest-energy structure. It is a spin twenty-five state with twofold HOMO. The next low-lying isomer 3b is higher in energy than the ground state by 0.386eV/atom, it is a $C_{2v}$ three-dimensional structure. A $C_s$ configuration is identified and shown in this figure as the third stable isomer of $\text{Gd}_3\text{O}_3$ cluster, and its energy lies about 0.432eV/atom above the ground state.
The $C_{3v}$ edge-capped tetrahedron (4a) with a spin thirty-two state and a singlet HOMO is the lowest energy configuration for $\text{Gd}_4\text{O}_3$. It has a binding energy of 3.87 eV/atom and average Gd-O bond length of 0.213nm. In addition, the planar structures $C_{2v}$ and $C_s$ (4b, 4c), capped octahedron, bicapped hexahedron, and capped prism are considered. However, these isomers are higher in energy than ground state.
$\text{Gd}_5\text{O}_3$ is the largest trioxides studied here. The results show that the lowest energy structure and the two low-lying isomers of $\text{Gd}_5\text{O}_3$ clusters can be seen as three O atoms absorbed on the different sites of the trigonal biyramid $\text{Gd}_5$ cluster, forming different edge-capped configurations, as shown in Fig.1. The lowest energy structure 5a $C_s$ with an average Gd-O bond length 0.2111nm, and binding energy 3.5873eV/atom. The next low-lying isomer 5b ($C_1$) and 5c ($C_{3v}$) are 0.008eV/atom and 0.027eV/atom above the lowest energy structure.
According to the above-noted analysis, the Gd atoms and O atoms prefer three and two coordination, and the Gd-Gd and O-O bonds are broken in the lowest energy clusters. For the $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ clusters, the interactions between the $2p$ electrons of O and the $5d$ electrons of Gd result in a strong Gd-O bond. The high average binding energies, indicating that the $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ clusters are very stable, especially for $\text{Gd}_2\text{O}_3$ and $\text{Gd}_3\text{O}_3$ clusters.

## Relative stabilities

To understand the relative stability of $Gd_nO_3$ clusters, we carefully estimated the average binding energies for each low-lying isomer. The $E_b$ is defined as $E_b=(E_{cluster}-nE_{Gd}-3E_O)/(n+3)$, where the $E_{cluster}$ is the energy of the $Gd_nO_3$ cluster, and $E_{Gd}$ and $E_O$ are the energies of Gd and O atom. The calculated average binding energies (E_b/atom) of the ground state structures have been shown in Fig.2. It is clear from Fig. 2 that the $Gd_2O_3$ and $Gd_3O_3$ clusters possess the relative higher stability. The atomic electronic configuration of the gadolinium is $4f^75d^16s^2$, in which seven $4f$ electrons are deeply embedded under the $5d$ $6s$ orbital and can be regarded as the core electrons. Thus, there are only one $5d$ and two $6s$ valence electrons being ready to form chemical bonds. The valence electronic configuration of oxygen is $2s^22p^4$, and thus it needs another two electrons to fulfill the $2p$ electronic orbital. This also explains the preferences of three coordination numbers for each Gd atom, and two coordination numbers for each O atom. In $Gd_2O_3$ cluster, the coordination numbers of Gd and O atoms are exactly three and two, respectively. We found that the bond lengths of Gd-O atoms are ranged from 0.202nm to 0.217nm, with small variation, which is attributed to the nearly constants of coordination numbers of Gd and O atoms.

## Electronic Properties

Fig.3 presents the HOMO-LUMO gaps of $Gd_nO_3$ clusters, which is considered to be a good indicator of the electronic stability for small clusters. From the Fig.3, we can see that the gaps exhibit an even-odd oscillating variations. There are two local peaks at the sizes of $n$=2 and 4, where the $Gd_2O_3$ shows the largest gap values of 1.724 eV in the investigated range, indicating that it is relatively the most chemically stable than other size clusters. In addition, we have calculated the HOMO-LUMO gaps of single Gd and O atoms, the values are 2.474eV and 3.048eV, respectively. Compared with free Gd and O atoms, the $Gd_nO_3$ HOMO-LUMO gaps decrease as the hybridization between Gd ($5d$ and $6s$) and O ($2p$) atoms occurs in the clusters, indicating an enhancement of metallicity.

### Magnetic Properties
Finally, we comment on the magnetic properties of $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ ($n$=1-5) clusters. In the calculations, $7\mu_\text{B}$ with different arrangements (ferromagnetic ordering and antiferromagnetic ordering) have been imposed on each Gd atoms. Generally, we found that the antiferromagnetic orderings are higher than corresponding ferromagnetic ordering in each structure (usually more than 0.076eV/atom). According to Fig.4 one can observe that the total magnetic moments increase monotonically with the increasing of clusters size and the local magnetic moment of O atoms are about $0.101$~$0.132\mu_\text{B}$ (except for $\text{GdO}_3$, in which the average local magnetic moment the O atom is $0.906\mu_\text{B}$). The average local magnetic moments of Gd atom increase with the increase of the Gd atom from 1 to 3, then drop at $n$=4, 5. The magnetic values ranged from $6.719\mu_\text{B}$ to $8.047\mu_\text{B}$. The results demonstrate that in the $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ ($n$=1-5) clusters the magnetic moments exhibit a weak dependence on the O atoms, and the local magnetic moments between Gd and O atoms are antiferromagnetic. The magnetic properties are mainly decided by Gd atoms. The further analysis implies the total magnetic moments are mostly dominated by the Gd $4f$ orbital.

### Conclusions
We have investigated the structures, stabilities, electronic and magnetic properties of the $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ ($n$=1-5) clusters by using the DFT method. The geometry optimizations indicate that the gadolinium oxide prefers to keep three and two coordinations on Gd and O atom, respectively. By analyzing the stabilities and HOMO-LUMO gaps of $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ clusters, we found that the $\text{Gd}_2\text{O}_3$ is the most stable than its neighboring clusters. The magnetic calculations demonstrate that the total magnetic moments of $\text{Gd}_n\text{O}_3$ clusters are mostly dominated by the Gd atoms.
