# Electronic structure and magnetism of $Y_2Fe_3Mn$ with cubic Laves-phase structure

## Kiyosi Terao*, Hideji Yamada
The Faculty of Science, Shinshu University, Matsumoto 390, Japan

---

### Abstract
The electronic structure of $Y_2Fe_3Mn$ is calculated in LMTO-ASA method, by assuming a hypothetically ordered structure. It is found that high- and low-spin states of the Mn atom are stabilized at larger and smaller lattice parameter $a$, respectively, when the Mn moment is parallel to the Fe moment in the compound. When the Mn moment is antiparallel to the Fe moment, only high-spin state of the Mn atom is stabilized at larger $a$, with the energy very close to that in the parallel case. © 1998 Elsevier Science B.V. All rights reserved.

**Keywords**: Laves-phase compounds; Band structure – energy; Band magnetism; Magnetic moments calculated

---

The pseudobinary intermetallics $Y(Fe_{1-x}Mn_x)_2$ with the cubic Laves-phase (C15) structure show very complicated magnetic behaviors: the Mn atom takes high- or low-spin states and its moment is parallel or antiparallel to Fe moment [1-5]. In this paper, we calculate the electronic structure of $Y_2Fe_3Mn$, by assuming a hypothetically ordered structure and discuss the local moments of Fe and Mn. Numerical calculations are carried out by a self-consistent LMTO-ASA method in the framework of local density functional formalism. The self-consistent calculation is carried out at 344 inequivalent $k$-points in the irreducible $\frac{1}{12}$ Brillouin zone. The basis set with angular momenta up to $l=3$ is used for all atoms. A similar calculation was recently carried out for $Y_2Co_3Mn$ and it was revealed that the Mn moment is rather stable and the spin state of Co atom changes from low to high with increasing lattice parameter $a$ [6].

Electronic structures in the nonmagnetic, ferromagnetic and ferrimagnetic states are calculated as a function of $a$. The calculated total energies are minimum at $a \sim 7.0$ Å for both nonmagnetic and ferromagnetic states, as shown in Fig. 1. The value of $a$ is smaller by about 5% than the observed value [4]. In the ferromagnetic state with Mn moment parallel to Fe moment, the Mn atom has about $2\ \mu_\text{B}$/atom in a high spin state at $a > 7.1$ Å and about $0.5$–$1\ \mu_\text{B}$/atom in a low-spin state at $a < 7.0$ Å, as shown in Fig. 2. The total moment at the energy minimum is $4.52\ \mu_\text{B}$/unit-cell, being almost the same as the observed bulk moment about $4\ \mu_\text{B}$/unit-cell [3,4].

A ferrimagnetic solution with the Mn moment more than $1.7\ \mu_{\text{B}}$/atom antiparallel to the Fe moment is obtained at $a > 7.17\ \text{\AA}$. By NMR measurements, the Mn atom with antiparallel moment was observed to be in the low-spin state in $\text{Y(Fe}_{1-x}\text{Mn}_{x}\text{)}_{2}$ for dilute $x$ [4,5]. However, in our $\text{Y}_{2}\text{Fe}_{3}\text{Mn}$ with hypothetical ordered structure, no solution in the low-spin state is obtained when the Mn moment is antiparallel to the Fe moment. Neutron diffraction measurements are desired to estimate the Mn moment in $\text{Y(Fe}_{0.75}\text{Mn}_{0.25}\text{)}_{2}$.

As shown in Fig. 1, the total energy of ferrimagnetic state is a little higher than that of the ferromagnetic state at $a = 7.17\ \text{\AA}$. With increasing $a$, the energy difference decreases and, subsequently, the antiparallel Mn moment is stabilized at $a = 7.5\ \text{\AA}$. The calculated Mn moment takes low- and high-spin states, depending on $a$. This is different from the stable Mn moment in $\text{Y}_{2}\text{Co}_{3}\text{Mn}$ [6]. It is interesting that the Mn atom takes many magnetic states in $\text{Y(Fe, Mn)}_{2}$ and does not obey a simple rigid band model, while the Mn moment is stable in $\text{Y(Co, Mn)}_{2}$.

In this paper, we have obtained the ferromagnetic and ferrimagnetic solutions for $\text{Y}_{2}\text{Fe}_{3}\text{Mn}$. Moreover, the Mn atom has been found to be both in high- and low-spin states, depending on the lattice parameter and the direction of the Mn moment. It is pointed out that the energy difference between parallel and antiparallel spin states of Mn to the Fe moment is very small near the observed lattice parameter. Then we can expect certain complicated behaviors of Mn moment in the present pseudobinary intermetallics, as observed [4,5].
