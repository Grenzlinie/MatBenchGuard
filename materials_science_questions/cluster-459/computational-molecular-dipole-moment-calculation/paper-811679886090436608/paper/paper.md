# THEORETICAL CHARACTERS OF
$MgX$ ($X = Te$, $Se$, $S$ AND $O$) CLUSTERS

SHUHONG XU, CHUNLEI WANG and YIPING CUI*

Advanced Photonics Center,
School of Electronic Science and Engineering,
Southeast University, Nanjing, 210096, China
*cyp@seu.edu.cn

Received 12 October 2010

At DFT/B3LYP/LANL2DZ theoretical level, a series of MgX clusters with $Mg_3X_3$ hexagon, $Mg_4X_4$-t tetrahedron and $Mg_4X_4$-o octagon conformations are investigated. Their wavelengths of absorption spectra are calculated with time-dependent Density Functional Theory (TDDFT) using water as solvent. Firstly, the lowest vibrational frequencies, bond lengths, Dipole Moment and Raman spectra of them present regular change. Dipole Moment result shows that different clusters have different charges. From Raman spectra, we have discovered that they correlate with the conformation and variety of clusters. Conformations of MgO clusters have different optimizing characters with MgS, MgSe and MgTe molecules. Secondly, through Molecular Orbital and Nature Bond Orbital (NBO) analysis, we have found that all these clusters are materials with wider gaps. MgTe structures have stronger bonding. Besides, MgO have different transition from the other clusters. The order of wavelengths of absorption spectra is $MgTe > MgSe > MgS$. In a word, except MgO molecules, these clusters have similar or regular characters. These results are significant for experimental study of MgX nanocrystals.

Keywords: MgX; DFT; bonding characters; spectra.

## 1. Introduction

II–VI nanocrystals (CdTe, CdSe, CdS, ZnTe, ZnSe, ZnS) have attracted much attention during the past two decades for its variety of new intriguing physical, chemical properties and extensive applications, resulting from quantum confinement effects. $^{1–6}$ Mg, as a IIA element, can form II–VI nanocrystals with O, S, Se and Te. Recently, the importance of Mg-based semiconductor material for wide band-gap light emitters has been demonstrated. These kinds of materials and their doping structures have been synthesized and investigated in experiment. $^{7–15}$ Of course, there are many reports in theory. In 2004, with first principles calculations, structural, electronic, elastic and optical properties of Mg-based compounds in zinc

Moreover, the contrast of their characters has not been achieved. In this paper, we get MgTe, MgSe, MgS and MgO clusters according to wurtzite and zinc blende. Then, their bonding characters, spectra, molecule orbitals are investigated.

## 2. Calculation Method

The conformations and characters of MgX clusters are studied at B3LYP/ LANL2DZ level with Gaussian03 package. $^{30}$ Under Lanl2dz basis set, the external electrons of Mg, Te, Se, S and O atoms were described. With this basis set, the core electrons were not taken into account, which can save much time for calculation of these kinds of clusters. The original structures were based on the experimental zinc blende and wurtzite structure of MgTe, MgSe, MgS and MgO nanocrystals. Raman spectra were calculated with Freq = Raman since they could not be created automatically under DFT method. Molecular Orbital (MO) figures were generated using GaussView program. Absorption spectra of these clusters in solvent were obtained using time-dependent DFT (TDDFT). Moreover, ten electro-states are chosen with td = (nstates = 10). As to this kind of TDDFT calculation, Scrf = (solvent = water) must be added to get absorption spectra in solvent. The dielectric constant of water in our paper is 78.39 ($\varepsilon = 78.39$). WBI values, which are used to estimate the bonding between two atoms, were calculated in Nature Bond Orbital (NBO) with Pop = Nboread.

## 3. Result and Discussion

### 3.1. Conformations and optimizing characters

At DFT/B3LYP/LANL2DZ theoretical level, a series of MgX (X = O, S, Se and Te) clusters are investigated. In this paper, three kinds of conformation of MgX clusters ($\text{Mg}_3\text{X}_3$ hexagon, $\text{Mg}_4\text{X}_4$-t tetrahedron and $\text{Mg}_4\text{X}_4$-o octagon) are depicted in Fig. 1. Quadrangle, which has strong tensility, is not stable with many imaginary frequencies. Thus, in this paper, we do not study this conformation. As for hexagon $\text{Mg}_3\text{Te}_3$, $\text{Mg}_3\text{Se}_3$, $\text{Mg}_3\text{S}_3$ and $\text{Mg}_3\text{O}_3$ structures, their conformations are

Seeing from the lowest vibrational frequencies, we have found that MgO clusters have high frequencies, and the order is MgO > MgS > MgSe > MgTe. As for different conformation, the order of low- est vibrational frequencies is Mg₄X₄-t > Mg₃X₃ > Mg₄X₄-o, which illuminates that octagon has worse stability. Obviously, the stability of the clusters relates with the kinds of molecules and the conformations of clusters. Bond lengths of three conformations increasing in order Mg₄X₄-o, Mg₃X₃ and Mg₄X₄-t. Namely, Mg₄X₄-o clusters have the shortest bond length (Mg₄O₄-o < Mg₃O₃ < Mg₄O₄-t, Mg₄S₄-o < Mg₃S₃ < Mg₄S₄-t, Mg₄Se₄-o < Mg₃Se₃ < Mg₄Se₄-t, Mg₄Te₄-o < Mg₃Te₃ < Mg₄Te₄-t). According to the rule of bond length, Dipole Moment (DM) should have the same rule as it. In fact, the rule of DM is not identical with that of bond lengths. The rule of DM is Mg₃X₃ < Mg₄X₄-o < Mg₄X₄-t, which shows that Mg and X atoms have different charge in different conformation for $\mu = q \times r$ ($\mu$ is DM; $q$ is charge; $r$ is the distance of positive and negative charges). Therefore, we believe that there are different charges for experimental MgX nanocrystals with dif- ferent size and conformations. Besides, Raman spectra are pictured in Fig. 2. The frequencies of main Raman spectra shift to low frequencies in order MgO, MgS, MgSe and MgTe. Moreover, as for the same MgX molecules, their Raman spectra are different. We presume that the change of conformations has influence the vibra- tion of bonding between Mg and Te, Se, S and O atoms. It can be concluded that

The lower gaps of $Mg_4O_4$ structure maybe correlate with their special conformations. The HOMO-LUMO gaps are the useful criterion to judge the variety of materials. However, the HOMO-LUMO gaps do not relate the stability of cluster. In order to estimate the stability, we have obtained WBI values of these clusters. WBI values of NBO show that MgTe clusters have the strongest bonding and MgO molecules have the feeblest bonding. The bonding order is MgO < MgS < MgSe < MgTe. As for the same MgX clusters, bonding order is $Mg_4X_{4-t} < Mg_4X_{4-o} < Mg_3X_3$. The calculating bonding orders of our paper are important for experimental investigation. Besides, we have calculated the wavelength of absorption spectra of clusters. MgS, MgSe and MgTe have similar characters: their transition orbitals are the same. For there is solvent for experimental nanocrystals during measuring the wavelengths of absorption spectra, we have added water as solvent to simulate the condition of experiment. And the wavelengths of absorption spectra have the order: MgS < MgSe < MgTe (Table 1). However, MgO clusters have completely different characters. Their wavelengths of absorption spectra are longer than those of MgS clusters. In $Mg_4X_{4-t}$ clusters, $Mg_4O_4$ molecule has longer wavelength than $Mg_4Te_4$ structure. Moreover, the transition orbitals of MgO clusters are different from those of MgS, MgSe and MgTe structures. We have thought that there are two factors inducing the special characters of MgO clusters. The first one is the different conformations of MgO with MgTe, MgSe and MgS molecules. The other one is molecule orbital. Transition orbital of MgO clusters are completely different with corresponding MgTe, MgSe and MgS molecules (Table 1).

## 4. Conclusion
With DFT/B3LYP/LANL2DZ method, conformations, bonding characters, MO, NBO, Raman and absorption spectra of $Mg_3X_3$ hexagon, $Mg_4X_{4-t}$ tetrahedron and $Mg_4X_{4-o}$ octagon conformations are studies. We have found that MgO structures have different characters with other molecules and characters of MgS, MgSe, MgTe present rule. MgTe clusters have the strongest bonding, which proves that MgTe nanocrystals are the most stable materials. Raman spectra, which can offer guidance for the future experiment, shift to low frequencies in order MgO, MgS, MgSe and MgTe. The wavelengths of absorption spectra shift to red gradually with MgS, MgSe and MgTe structures. MgO do not meet this rule for they have different transition, molecule orbitals and conformations. Finally, we believe that out characters are important for experimental investigation of MgTe, MgSe, MgS and MgO clusters.
