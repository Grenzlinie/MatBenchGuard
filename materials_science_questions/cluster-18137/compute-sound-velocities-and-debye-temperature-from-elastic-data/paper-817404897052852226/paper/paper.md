# First-Principles Study on Electronic Structure, Elasticity, Debye Temperature and Anisotropy of Cubic $\mathbf{KCaF_3}$

Xing Liu$^{1,2,a}$, Jia Fu$^{2,b*}$, Manman Han$^{2,c}$, Kaixin Sun$^{2,d}$, Shengli Wei$^{2,e}$

**Keywords:** $\mathbf{KCaF_3}$; Electronic structure; Elastic properties; Debye temperature; Acoustic wave velocity.

**Abstract.** As a potential functional material in the perovskite family, the $\mathrm{KCaF_3}$ on electronic structure, elasticity, Debye temperature and anisotropy are studied based on density functional theory (DFT). Above all, the structural parameters of $\mathrm{KCaF_3}$ crystal are optimized. Then the elastic constants and Debye temperature are calculated. The results show that: (1) $\mathrm{KCaF_3}$ is composed of covalent bonds, in which the Ca-F bond is stronger than K-F. (2) Ca atom mainly contributes for the electronic properties of $\mathrm{KCaF_3}$. (3) The structural parameters of $\mathrm{KCaF_3}$ is in fair agreement with the experimental data. (4) The anisotropy of $\mathrm{KCaF_3}$ was analyzed from the pure and quasi waves, of which the longitudinal wave velocity in the direction of [$100$] is the larger than the others two directions ([$110$] and [$111$]). Finally, The homogenized elastic moduli, Pugh and Poisson ratio are obtained. This research is meaningful and thus to provides a good theoretical guidance for the design the new $\mathrm{ABX_3}$-type material with better performance.

## Introduction

The fluoro-perovskite $\mathrm{ABF_3}$ has aroused wide attention in both theoretical and experimental studies. As an important branch of the perovskite family, it has strong ionic, low phonon energy, wide band-gap and so on, which has important application in the field of energy conversion, information transport and storage materials, display materials, tunable laser materials$^{[1-2]}$. Besides, first-principles calculations are also commonly-used to calculate mechanical properties of perovskite materials$^{[3]}$, building materials$^{[4]}$ and new alloy phases$^{[5]}$.

To understand the physical properties of $\mathrm{ABF_3}$, several researchers have done a lot of studies both in experiment and theory. Wood et al. $^{[6]}$ studied the structure of $\mathrm{KCaF_3}$ in different temperature range by high resolution neutron powder diffraction method, which found that the $\mathrm{KCaF_3}$ can keep cubic structure. B. Huang et al. $^{[7]}$ prepared $\mathrm{KZnF_3}$ and $\mathrm{KCdF_3}$ nanocrystals used a mild solvothermal route, and their properties were characterized by TEM and XPS. The reaction temperature, time and molar ratio of the initial materials were studied, and the results showed that $\mathrm{KZnF_3}$ and $\mathrm{KCdF_3}$ nanocrystals could be controllably synthesized. Knight K.S. et al. $^{[8]}$ using high resolution powder neutron diffraction showed that, at 300 K, there is also a phase structure of $\mathrm{KCaF_3}$ crystal, and the crystal structure was determined by the lattice constant. Demetriou et al. $^{[2]}$ studied the thermal conductivity of cubic perovskite $\mathrm{KCaF_3}$ by single crystal neutron diffraction, and the results showed that thermal conductivity was caused by the high migration hole inside the crystal. In terms of theoretical research, T. Seddik et al. $^{[9]}$ studied the elastic, electronic and

thermodynamic properties of $KZnF_3$ by using the full potential linearized augmented plane wave(FP-LAPW) method and quasi harmonic Debye theory. Sahnoun et al. $^{[10]}$ studied the relationship between electronic properties and pressure of $KCaF_3$ via the same method as Seddik, the results indicated that the electronic structure shown linear variation with pressure.

The $ABF_3$ have made great achievements in experiment and theory, however, as an important member of fluoro-perovskite family, $KCaF_3$, systematic study on electronic structure, elasticity, Debye temperature and other properties are relatively lacking. In this paper, based on DFT method, the properties of $KCaF_3$ are studied and analyzed in detail.

## Calculation Detail

Our initial step to obtain a stable structure is to optimize the atomic structure to its minimum energy. Thus, DFT calculations were performed using the CASTEP $^{[11]}$ code of Materials Studio to optimize cubic perovskite $KCaF_3$ structures. The Perdew-Burke-Ernzerh (PBE) of generalized gradient approximation (GGA) were used for exchange correlation. The Brillouin zone was sampled with $\Gamma$-centered k-point grids of 10×10×10 for all unit cells. The cutoff energy for plane-wave basis was set to 380 eV for the optimization calculations, and the BFGS method was adopted to optimize the cubic perovskite $KCaF_3$ structure. The electronic structure and optical properties of $KCaF_3$ were investigated. In calculation, the crystal wave function was expanded from the plane wave. The interaction between electrons and ions were described by the ultrasoft pseudopotential, which can reduce the number of base planes in the convergence calculation to save the computing resources.

The space group of cubic $KCaF_3$ is $Pm\overline{3}m$, which contains 5 atoms in the primitive cell, and the atomic coordinates are K (0.5, 0.5, 0.5), Ca (0, 0, 0), F (0, 0.5, 0.5), respectively. Fig. 1 shows the crystal of a perfect cubic perovskite $KCaF_3$. The K cations are represented by the purple spheres at the corners of the unit cell, the Ca cation is represented by the brown sphere at the body center, and the F anions are represented by silver balls at the face centers.

Electronic structure. By studying the band structure and density of states of KCaF₃, not only can a good understanding of the electronic structure, but also to explain the physical and chemical properties of these compounds from the micro level of electron. The results on the band gaps are shown in Fig.2(a), the Fermi level is located at 0eV. The maximum band gap is located between the M and G(M→G), therefore, KCaF₃ presents an indirect band gap ($E_\mathrm{g}$=6.15$eV$). Generally, using density functional theory, the researched band gaps are less than the experimental value $^{[13-14]}$, because of the well-known underestimation in DFT calculation. Density of states for KCaF₃ are shown in Fig. 2(b), we can see that the top of the valence bands and the bottom of conduction bands are all involved by F-2p and Ca-3d, and a small amount of K-3p is involved in the hybridization. In the low valence bands zone (<-10 eV), K-3p, F-2s, Ca-3p K-4s and Ca-4s are the main contributors, and F-2s and Ca-3p are hybrid. Therefore, it can be inferred that CaF₆ octahedron affects the performance of KCaF₃.

Debye temperature. The expression of the Debye temperature described by the average wave velocity are as follows⁽³²⁾:

$$
v_{s}=\sqrt{\frac{G_{x}}{\rho}} ; v_{p}=\sqrt{\frac{B_{x}+\frac{4 G_{x}}{3}}{\rho}} ; v_{m}=\left(\frac{\frac{2}{v_{s}^{3}}+\frac{1}{v_{p}^{3}}}{3}\right)^{-\frac{1}{3}} \tag{1}
$$

$$
\Theta=\frac{h}{k}\left(\frac{3 n N_{A} \rho}{4 \pi M}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}} v_{m} \tag{2}
$$

Where, \(v_{\text{s}}\) is the shear wave velocity, \(v_{\text{p}}\) is the longitudinal wave velocity, \(v_{\text{m}}\) is the average wave velocity, \(h\) is Planck constant, \(k\) is Boltzmann constant, \(N_{A}\) is the Avogadro constant, \(M\) is the molecular weight, \(\rho\) is the density.

**Anisotropy of elastic wave velocity.** For cubic KCaF₃, Young's moduli in the direction of $[100]$, $[110]$ and $[111]$ by elastic constants $C_{11}$, $C_{12}$ and $C_{44}$ can be calculated as follows$^{[34]}$:

$$
\left.E\right|_{[100]}=\frac{c_{11}^{2}+c_{11} c_{12}-2 c_{12}^{2}}{c_{11}+c_{12}} \tag{3}
$$

$$
\left.E\right|_{[110]}=\frac{4\left(c_{11}^{2}+c_{11} c_{12}-2 c_{12}^{2}\right) c_{44}}{c_{11}^{2}+c_{11} c_{12}+2 c_{11} c_{44}-2 c_{12}^{2}} \tag{4}
$$

$$
\left.E\right|_{[111]}=\frac{3\left(c_{11}+2 c_{12}\right) c_{44}}{c_{11}+2 c_{12}+c_{44}} \tag{5}
$$

## Conclusion

The band structure, DOS, elastic constants, Debye temperature of cubic KCaF₃ are investigated from first-principles calculation. Results show that:

(1) The calculated structural parameters are consistent with previous works. The study of the electronic structure indicates that KCaF₃ is indirect band gap material, and band gap are determined by F-2p and Ca-3d, and a few number of K-3p contributions, they all have the characteristics of covalent bonds, which Ca-F covalent bond is stronger than K-F.

(2) From the band structure and the density of states, that the electronic properties of KCaF₃ are determined by CaF₆ octahedron. Therefore, it is possible to design the KCaF₃ material with desired electron performance.

(3) Based on $C_{ij}$, the bulk, shear and Young's moduli are calculated, which can be used to determine the stability, Vickers' hardness and Poisson' ratio.

(4) The elastic properties of KCaF₃ are studied by calculation and analysis of the elastic modulus $\boldsymbol{B}$, $\boldsymbol{G}$, $\boldsymbol{E}$, Pugh and Poisson ratio, which show that KCaF₃ has a certain ductility. Young's modulus in directions of [100], [110] and [111] are separately 99.26 GPa, 53.47 GPa and 46.34 GPa.

(5) Under the concept of pure and quasi waves, the acoustic waves of the cubic KCaF₃ have great anisotropy in the [110], [110] and [111] directions and the calculated Debye temperature is 431 K at 0 GPa.
