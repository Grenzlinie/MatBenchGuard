# Theoretical study of the adsorption of gas molecules on Mg-embedded boron carbide ($\text{C}_3\text{B}$) nanosheets: Implications for gas sensors

Kangping Zhao $^{1,* \#}$, Yannan Zhang $^{1\#}$, Yuting Ma $^{1}$, Guoli Li $^{1}$, Chun-Ming Yu $^{2}$

$^{1.}$ Gansu Guoxinrunda Analysis and Testing Center, Lanzhou, Gansu, 730000, China

## Abstract
In this paper, the adsorption behaviors of nitrogen based gas molecules (NO, $\text{N}_2\text{O}$, $\text{NH}_3$ and $\text{NO}_2$) on the Mg-adsorbed $\text{C}_3\text{B}$ monolayers were investigated using the density functional theory computations. Firstly, the adsorption of Mg metal on the $\text{C}_3\text{B}$ nanosheet was considered. Our results suggested that the adsorption of Mg atom on the surface of $\text{C}_3\text{B}$ monolayer is a suitable strategy to enhance the sensitivity of $\text{C}_3\text{B}$ based sensors towards aforementioned gas molecules. The semiconductor to metal phase change occurred upon the adsorption of metal dopant on the $\text{C}_3\text{B}$ monolayer. The considered gas molecules were adsorbed on the Mg site of Mg-$\text{C}_3\text{B}$ sheets via chemical bonds, which indicates the superior media for gas sensing and adsorption. Besides, all the gas adsorbed Mg-$\text{C}_3\text{B}$ nanosheets exhibited metallic behavior. Thus, our findings indicated that Mg-adsorbed $\text{C}_3\text{B}$ nanosheet is an outstanding candidate for sensing nitrogen based gas molecules.

Keywords: Density functional theory; Adsorption; $\text{C}_3\text{B}$ monolayer; Gas sensor

### 1. Introduction

Motivated by the exploration of graphene, 2D nanostructures have attracted marvelous research interests in the field of nanoelectronic devices and nanosensor technologies [1-6]. The potential importance of 2D nanomaterials can be attributed to their excellent chemical, physical and surface properties. In recent years, atmospheric pollution has become a critical problem all over the world with the increasing progress in the industry and technology. The importance of a safe and workable environment has never been ignored, but a trace concentration of each harmful gas in the environment can cause serious damage to the climate. Thus, the environment is facing significant risks due to the harmful gas emissions from factory wastes, industries and vehicles [7].

Gas sensors fabricated from semiconductor metal oxides are usually applied for environmental protection; nevertheless, their applicability is greatly limited because they can exhibit optimum performance at high temperatures [8-11]. These types of sensing materials also show limited selectivity towards different air pollutants. Thus, we need to search for highly sensitive and selective sensors with improved operability and selectivity. 2D nanomaterials based on carbon nanostructures, especially graphene, are significantly efficient for applications as gas sensors owing to their excellent charge transfer and large surface-to-volume ratio [12-14]. Recently, the remarkable sensitivity of graphene monolayers for detection of $\text{NH}_3$ and $\text{NO}_2$ gas molecules has been extensively addressed [15].

The interaction of a gas molecule with pure graphene is normally dominated by weak physical adsorption, which is not appropriate for working principle of sensors at ambient conditions. Thus, several methods have been proposed to enhance the sensing performance of gas sensors. For example, in the case of graphene monolayer, Cr and Mn doping have greatly enhanced the sensing capability of substrate towards $\text{SO}_2$ molecules [16]. Moreover, Fe-doped graphene monolayers

have shown efficient adsorption performance for sensing $H_2S$ molecule [17]. Therefore, substitutional doping has been considered as an effectual strategy to modify the electronic, adsorption and sensing properties of materials [18-22].

More recently, hexagonal structure of boron and carbon atoms, namely $C_3B$ monolayer, has been experimentally synthesized [23-26]. The pristine $C_3B$ monolayer shows a relatively low sensitivity towards gas molecules due to the weak interaction of gas molecules with pristine surface. Mahabel et al. [27] examined the sensing and adsorption behaviors of $NO$, $CO$, $NO_2$, $CO_2$ and $NH_3$ gas molecules over the $C_3B$ nanosheets using DFT computations. The enhanced sensitivity of Al-doped and Si-doped $C_3B$ nanosheets to formaldehyde and acetone detection has been also addressed in the previous works [28, 29]. The metal adsorption has been demonstrated to be an efficient method for modulating the electronic and structural properties of 2D nanomaterials [27]. Thus, in this work, we studied the adsorption of Mg atom on the surface of $C_3B$ monolayer to probe its ability for gas sensing and adsorption.

Choi et al. [30] reported that the K, Ca and Mg doping could act as a promising strategy for enhancing the adsorption properties of sensors. Also, Naqvi group examined the adsorption of gas molecules including $CO$, $CO_2$ and $CH_4$ on the alkali metal adsorbed $C_3B$ monolayers. They suggested that the Mg-embedded $C_3B$ monolayers is a promising candidate for the effective sensing of harmful gas molecules [31]. Zhang et al. [32] also examined the gas adsorption on the Mg/Ca decorated hexagonal boron nitride nanosheets using DFT computations. They suggested that with the Mg/Ca dopant, the binding interaction between gas molecules and the BN sheet could be strongly enhanced. In this work, we examined the electronic and adsorption properties of the Mg-adsorbed $C_3B$ monolayers. We firstly analyzed the electronic and geometrical properties of metal adatom adsorbed $C_3B$ systems and found that metal adsorbed $C_3B$ sheets exhibited superior

performance for sensing gas molecules. The main importance of this work is to suggest an appropriate gas sensor based on Mg-adsorbed $C_3B$ nanosheets for gas detection in the environment.

## 2. Calculation methods and structures

Our theoretical calculations were conducted on the basis of density functional theory (DFT) [33, 34] as implemented in the Siesta package [35]. The Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE) functional of generalized gradient approximation (GGA) was employed to describe the effects of exchange-correlation energy potential [36]. The convergence criteria for the total energy and forces in the system were set to $10^{-5}$ Hartree and $0.005\ \text{eV}/\text{\AA}$, respectively. The electronic structure calculations were performed with the Monkhorst-Pack k-point sampling of $8\times8\times1$ [37]. The vacuum region between the neighboring repeated images was set to be $20\ \text{\AA}$, which is necessary to avoid extra interactions. Due to the underestimation of the adsorption energies by GGA functionals, we have employed a semi-empirical Grimme D2 contribution (DFT-D2) to correctly take the effects of van der Waals (vdW) interaction into account [38]. The considered unit cell of $C_3B$ includes a $2\times2\times1$ supercell of graphene with two carbon atoms substituted by boron atoms. For Mg adsorption on the $C_3B$ monolayer, the calculations were spin-polarized. We have placed the Mg atoms at the center of the hollow position of $C_3B$ monolayers and evaluated the stability of the systems according to the following equation:

$$
E_{ad}=E_{(\text{C3B} + M)}-E_{\text{C3B}}-E_{M} \tag{1}
$$

where $E_{(\text{C3B} + M)}$ shows the total energy of the $C_3B$ nanosheet with adsorbed metals, and $E_{\text{C3B}}$ is the energy of the free $C_3B$ monolayer, $E_{M}$ also signifies the energy of the adsorbed metals.

Subsequently, we investigated the adsorption of $\text{NO}$, $\text{N}_2\text{O}$, $\text{NH}_3$ and $\text{NO}_2$ gas molecules on the

Metal-adsorbed $C_3B$ monolayers. The adsorption energy for gas adsorption on the metal functionalized systems is also evaluated using the following equation:

$$E_{ad}=E\ (_{M\text{-C3B }+Gas)}-E\ _{M\text{-C3B}}-E\ _{Gas} \tag{2}$$

where, E $_{(M\text{-C3B }+Gas)}$ and E $_{M\text{-C3B}}$ denote the energies of the gas adsorbed and non-adsorbed $C_3B$ monolayers.

## 3. Results and Discussion
### 3.1. Structure and electronic properties of Mg-adsorbed $C_3B$ monolayers

To examine the adsorption of Mg atoms on the pristine $C_3B$ monolayers, it is important to inspect the electronic properties and optimized structure of pristine $C_3B$ monolayer. The structure of $C_3B$ monolayer is similar to that of graphene. The electron deficient behavior of $C_3B$ sheet results from the substituted boron atoms. The optimized structure of the considered $C_3B$ monolayer in two different views was shown in Figure 1a-b. To inspect the electronic behavior of the $C_3B$ monolayer, we have calculated the electronic band structure diagram for pristine $C_3B$. Figure 1c describes the band structure plot of the $C_3B$ monolayer. As can be seen from this figure, in the pristine $C_3B$ monolayer, we can see an indirect band gap around the Fermi level, indicating the semiconductor property of the system. The calculated band gap for the bare $C_3B$ monolayer is about 0.66 eV, which is in good agreement with the previously reported results [39, 40].

To examine the impacts of the metal adsorption, we have calculated some structural and electronic parameters for the adsorption of Mg atom on the $C_3B$ monolayers. In the case of Mg adsorption on the $C_3B$ monolayer, the optimized atomic configuration was displayed in Figure 2 in two different views. In this configuration, we can see that the Mg atom was coordinated to the neighboring atoms at the hollow position. The adsorption energy of this configuration is -3.20 eV, which indicates the higher stability of the adsorption process. Thus, Mg atom strongly interacts

with $C_3B$ monolayer. The electronic band structure graph of the Mg-adsorbed $C_3B$ monolayer was also plotted in Figure 2c. As it is seen from this figure, the Mg-$C_3B$ system represents metallic behavior. The charge transfer from the Mg atom to the $C_3B$ monolayer is calculated to be +1.284 e, indicating that Mg atom acts as a charge donor. This can be verified by the electron density difference (CDD) plots. The CDD plots for Mg adsorption on the $C_3B$ monolayer were also illustrated in Figure 3. According to the charge density isosurfaces, the depletion and accumulation of electronic charges near metal dopants are shown as positive (blue) and negative (yellow) regions. From Figure 3, we can see a large electron depletion on the adsorbed Mg atoms, indicating the considerable charge transfer from Mg adatom to the $C_3B$ monolayer. We have also performed the magnetic moment analysis for the Mg-adsorbed $C_3B$ system. We found that the Mg adsorption on the $C_3B$ monolayer results in the total spin magnetic moment of 0.1 $\mu$B. Thus, Mg-adsorbed $C_3B$ system exhibits magnetic behavior. Therefore, it can be concluded that the magnetic moments and therefore magnetization are largely localized on the adsorbed Mg atom.

### 3.2. Adsorption of NO, $N_2O$, $NO_2$ and $NH_3$ on Mg-adsorbed $C_3B$ nanosheets

In this section, the interaction between nitrogen based gas molecules (NO, $N_2O$, $NO_2$ and $NH_3$) and Mg-adsorbed $C_3B$ monolayers was described. Several parameters have been analyzed including adsorption energies/distances, band structures, and charge density differences for the adsorption of gas molecules on the Mg-adsorbed $C_3B$ systems. The most stable structures of the adsorption configurations for NO, $N_2O$, $NO_2$ and $NH_3$ molecules on the Mg-$C_3B$ monolayers were displayed in Figures 4 and 5. It can be seen from Figure 4 that the nitrogen and oxygen centers in NO and $N_2O$ molecules tend to be adsorbed on the metal site of Mg-$C_3B$ system. Figure 5 also describes the optimized structures for $NO_2$ and $NH_3$ adsorption to the Mg-adsorbed $C_3B$

monolayer. The oxygen and nitrogen atoms of the $NO_2$ molecule are adsorbed on the Mg site. For NO and $NO_2$ molecules, we have studied two adsorption configurations.

To further probe the stability and adsorption strength of these gas molecules on Mg-$C_3$B systems, we have calculated the adsorption energies and distances (Table 1). NO adsorption configurations on the Mg-$C_3$B monolayer were presented in Figure 4. The first configuration (I) represents the adsorption of NO molecule through the N center, while the second one (II) refers to the adsorption through the O atom of NO on the Mg-$C_3$B system. The adsorption energy of NO through its N center on the Mg-$C_3$B monolayer is calculated to be -1.11 eV, which is higher than the adsorption energy through the O center (-1.02 eV). Thus, NO molecule shows a stronger chemisorption through the N atom on the surface compared to the adsorption via the O atom. By including DFT-D2 method of Grimme [38], we found that NO molecule was adsorbed on the pristine $C_3$B monolayer with a smaller adsorption energy of -0.31 eV. This indicates a weak physisorption (vdW interaction) between NO molecule and pristine $C_3$B system. In the case of polyatomic $N_2O$ gas, the adsorption energy by the N atom is -1.10 eV. Therefore, $N_2O$ molecule is strongly adsorbed on the Mg-$C_3$B system [27]. The calculated adsorption energy of $N_2O$ molecule on the pristine $C_3$B sheet (-0.32 eV) is lower than that of Mg-$C_3$B system, indicating that $N_2O$ molecule adsorbs on pristine $C_3$B monolayer through weak vdW interaction. In the case of $NH_3$ adsorption, the N atom of the $NH_3$ molecule is seized by the Mg atom with the adsorption energy of -1.15 eV. On the pristine $C_3$B sheet, $NH_3$ molecule shows a relatively higher adsorption energy of -0.72 eV. Therefore, these gas molecules represent one binding point on the Mg-$C_3$B system [27].

Adsorption orientations of $NO_2$ molecule on the Mg-$C_3$B monolayer were also depicted in Figure 5. As can be seen, the first configuration shows the adsorption of $NO_2$ molecule on the Mg site through the O atoms, and the second configuration displays the adsorption through the N atom.

The adsorption energy of configuration II (-1.62 eV) is higher than that of configuration II (-1.35 eV). This means the adsorption of $NO_2$ molecule via the side oxygen atoms is stronger that that via the N atom. The O atoms of $NO_2$ molecule strongly bind to the Mg site based on a double binding point. Furthermore, the adsorption energy of $NO_2$/Mg-$C_3$B configuration is the highest among all the studied gas molecules adsorption. As mentioned above, the DFT-D2 method is very important for gas molecules when adsorbed on the pure $C_3$B monolayer. The calculated adsorption energy of $NO_2$ molecule on the pristine $C_3$B monolayer is -0.34 eV, which shows a weak vdW interaction between these systems. Hence, the adsorption of all gas molecules on the Mg-adsorbed $C_3$B monolayer is stronger than the adsorption on the pristine one. Figures 6 and 7 represent the electron density profiles for the adsorption of NO and $NH_3$ molecules on the Mg-$C_3$B monolayers. The accumulation of charge density between the interacting atoms of $NO/NH_3$ molecules and Mg atoms indicates the chemisorption process at the adsorption sites. The band structure graphs of gas molecules adsorbed Mg-$C_3$B monolayers were also depicted in Figure 8. It is seen from this figure that all gas adsorbed Mg-$C_3$B systems exhibit metallic behavior. The electronic band states induced by the adsorbed gas molecules enhanced the metallicity in Mg-$C_3$B systems.

It is worth mentioning that the adsorption of odd-electron molecules (NO and $NO_2$) can lead to spin-polarization into the system. Thus, we have carried out the spin-polarized calculations for NO and $NO_2$ adsorption on the Mg-adsorbed $C_3$B monolayers. In the case of pristine graphene nanosheet, the results suggested that the adsorption of paramagnetic molecules such as NO and $NO_2$ can introduce spin-polarization in the adsorption system [41]. The spin magnetic moments in these systems are mostly attributed to the adsorbed NO and $NO_2$ molecules. We have also presented the distribution of spin density profile for the adsorption of NO and $NO_2$ on the Mg-$C_3$B system (Figure S1). A close examination of this figure shows that the magnetic moments and thus

magnetization are largely localized on the adsorbed NO and $NO_2$ molecules. The projected density of states (PDOS) spectra of the N and Mg atoms for NO and $NH_3$ adsorptions on the Mg-$C_3$B monolayers were displayed in **Figure S2**. The strong overlaps of the metal Mg states with N states of $NO/NH_3$ molecules show the chemical bonding between these atoms.

It can also be mentioned that the gas molecules considered in this study show different adsorption energies with respect to each other. So, one can distinguish between different absorbed molecules based on the adsorption energy results. If one has a device for measuring adsorption energy, he/she can distinguish between different absorbed molecules because each molecule is absorbed with a specific adsorption energy. For example, among all the gas molecules, the highest adsorption energy occurs in $NO_2$ adsorption on the Mg-$C_3$B monolayer. So one can say that the molecule that shows the highest adsorption energy in the system is $NO_2$. In contrast, the molecule that represents the lowest adsorption energy is $NH_3$ (in the case of most stable configurations).

### 4. Conclusions

In this work, we described the results of density functional theory calculations to examine the capability of Mg-adsorbed $C_3B$ monolayers for sensing nitrogen based gas molecules (NO, $N_2O$, $NH_3$ and $NO_2$). The Mg atom was positioned on the hollow site at the center of $C_3B$ monolayer. The electronic structure analysis reveals the semiconductor to metal phase change upon the adsorption of Mg atom. Besides, the Mg metal was strongly chemisorbed on the $C_3B$ monolayer, making transfer of a large amount of charges to the $C_3B$ monolayer. The adsorption energy results confirmed the stability of the adsorption of Mg atom on the $C_3B$ monolayer. The adsorption of gas molecules on Mg-adsorbed $C_3B$ monolayers was also investigated to propose ideal substrates for gas sensing. We found that the gas molecules weakly interact with pristine $C_3B$ monolayer through vdW interaction. Moreover, all the gas molecule adsorbed Mg-$C_3B$ systems exhibited metallic behavior after the adsorption. The nature of covalent bonds between the gas molecules and Mg adatom was also described based on the strong overlaps in the PDOS graphs. Our findings indicated that Mg functionalization of the $C_3B$ monolayer can act as a promising strategy for enhancing the sensing behaviors of $C_3B$ sheets towards gas molecules.

Highlights:

1. Mg-adsorbed $C_3$B system exhibits metallic behavior.

2. The considerable electron depletion on the adsorbed Mg indicates a significant charge transfer from the Mg to the $C_3B$ monolayer.

3. All the gas molecules are chemisorbed on the Mg-$C_3B$ monolayers.
