# Temperature-Dependent Synthetic Routes to and Thermochemical Ranking of α- and β-SrNCN.
— β-SrNCN is prepared by solid state reaction between SrI₂ and ZnNCN at 843 K, from mixtures of SrI₂, CsCN, and CsN₃ in Ta cylinders at the same temperature, and by direct reaction between elemental Sr and H₂NCN in liq. NH₃.
α-SrNCN is obtained by the solid state reactions at 973 K. The polymorphs are characterized by powder XRD, IR spectroscopy, DSC, and DFT calculations. α-SrNCN crystallizes in the space group Pnma with $Z = 4$ and β-SrNCN in the space group R$\overline{3}$m with $Z = 3$. The β-phase is thermochemically more stable, whereas the α-phase is slightly more dense. The β-to-α phase transition shows two distinct endothermic steps.
