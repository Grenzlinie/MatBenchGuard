Abstract—Using data in the literature and our results, we have carried out a detailed analysis of the heat capacity of $Cu_2Se$ and the thermodynamic characteristics of the $\alpha$-$Cu_2Se$ $\leftrightarrow$ $\beta$-$Cu_2Se$ phase transition. The most reliable results have been recommended, which have been used to calculate the thermodynamic func- tions of $Cu_2Se$ in the temperature range 298–1400 K.

DOI: 10.1134/S0020168515110084

## INTRODUCTION

Previously, Glazov et al. [1] analyzed the Cu–Se system; constructed its most reliable $T$–$x$, $p$–$T$, and $\Delta_f G$–$x$ phase diagrams; and presented a review of new data on phase equilibria in this system, which were of fundamental importance for characterization of both the system and the intermediate phases in it. The Cu–Se system has four intermediate phases, but only one of them, $Cu_2Se$, melts congruently and is the most stable compound.

The $Cu_2Se$ compound is of interest in that it pos- sesses semiconducting properties in both the solid and liquid states [2]. Practical application of this com- pound, however, still depends on the ability to prepare it in single-phase form, so phase diagram data for the composition range around $Cu_2Se$ are of key impor- tance. Glazov et al. [1] paid a great deal of attention to the summarizing the thermodynamic characteristics of copper selenides: $\Delta_f H^\circ(298$ K), $S^\circ(298$ K), and $C_p(T)$. These characteristics, however, vary consider- ably from one source to another.

The objectives of this work are to analyze in detail the available heat capacity data for $Cu_2Se$, select the most reliable results, and calculate the thermody- namic functions of $Cu_2Se$ in the temperature range 298–1400 K.

## ANALYSIS OF THE THERMODYNAMIC CHARACTERISTICS OF $Cu_2Se$ AND DISCUSSION OF RESULTS

The melting point of $\beta$-$Cu_2Se$ is $1419 \pm 1$ K. This value was obtained by precisely determining the posi- tion of the liquidus line around the compound [1, 3] and is the most reliable. $Cu_2Se$ was thought of as a sto- ichiometric line compound and the homogeneity range of $Cu_{2 – x}Se$ (3 at % Se) was left out of account. As shown by Glazov et al. [1], this is a quite reasonable approximation. Copper selenide undergoes an $\alpha \leftrightarrow \beta$ polymorphic transformation at a temperature of $396 \pm$ 15 K. To somewhat simplify calculations, we take $T_{tr} =$ 400 K. The heat of this transformation was determined in several studies [4–6]. The values obtained differ lit- tle: $\Delta H_{tr} = 6.48 \pm 0.12$ kJ/mol [4], $6.83 \pm 0.2$ kJ/mol [5], and 6.4 kJ/mol [6]. The average value calculated by us is $\Delta H_{tr}(400$ K)$^1 = 6.57 \pm 0.25$ kJ/mol. The uncer- tainty was evaluated according to Blachnik and Gunia [4] and Kubaschewski and Nolting [5]. Dividing this value (in J/mol) by 400 K, we obtain $\Delta_{tr}S(400$ K)$^1 =$ $16.42 \pm 0.62$ J/(mol K).

The standard entropy of $\alpha$-$Cu_2Se$ is $129.7 \pm$ 4.2 J/(mol K) [7], but in constructing the $\Delta_f G(298$ K)–$x$ phase diagram, to match it with the $T$–$x$ phase dia- gram we had to make slight corrections in the $S_{298}^\circ$ of some compounds, within their uncertainties. For this reason, the $S_{298}^\circ$ of $\alpha$-$Cu_2Se$ was taken to be 125.5 J/(mol K). This value was used in calculations of the thermodynamic functions of $Cu_2Se$ (table).

The heat capacity of $\alpha$-$Cu_2Se$ was determined in the range 193–325 K by adiabatic calorimetry [5]. The temperature dependence of heat capacity for this phase can be represented by the equation [1]

$$C_p = 58.6 + 7.74 \times 10^{-2} T.^1$$

This equation can be extrapolated to 400 K.

The heat capacity of $\beta$-$\mathrm{Cu_2Se}$ was measured in the ranges 400–1390 [3] and 403–773 K [5]. The results of the two studies agree well. The $C_p$ of $\beta$-$\mathrm{Cu_2Se}$ varies little with temperature and even has a small minimum in the range 650–800 K. The average $C_p$ of $\beta$-$\mathrm{Cu_2Se}$ in this temperature range is $82.9\pm1.7$ J/(mol K), which we propose to use in thermochemical and thermodynamic calculations.

The table presents the thermodynamic functions of $\mathrm{Cu_2Se}$ calculated using the recommended values of $S_{298}^\circ$, $C_p(T)$, $T_\mathrm{tr}$, and $\Delta H_\mathrm{tr}(\alpha\leftrightarrow\beta)$. The good agreement between the $\Phi_{400}^\mathrm{xx}$ values of $\alpha$- and $\beta$-$\mathrm{Cu_2Se}$ suggests that adequate values of $\Delta H_\mathrm{tr}(\alpha\leftrightarrow\beta)$ and $T_\mathrm{tr}$ were chosen.

### CONCLUSIONS

We have carried out a detailed analysis of the thermodynamic characteristics of solid $\mathrm{Cu_2Se}$ available in the literature and obtained by us, calculated them in the range 298.15–1400 K, and recommended the most reliable results, which can be used in thermodynamic calculations.
