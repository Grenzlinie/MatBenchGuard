## ABSTRACT

Experimental and theoretical vibrational spectra of β-2-aminopyridinium dihydrogenphosphate (β-2APDP) have been investigated. The FT-IR spectrum of β-2APDP was recorded in the region $4000-400\,\mathrm{cm}^{-1}$. The optimized molecular structure and theoretical vibrational frequencies of β-2APDP have been investigated using ab initio Hartree-Fock (HF) and density functional B3LYP method with 6-311++G(d,p) basis set. The optimized geometric parameters (bond lengths and bond angles) and theoretical frequencies have been compared with the corresponding experimental data and it is found that they agree well with each other. All the assignments of the theoretical frequencies were performed by potential energy distributions using VEDA 4 program. Furthermore, the used scale factors were obtained from the ratio of the frequency values of the strongest peaks in the experimental and theoretical IR spectra. From the results it was concluded that the B3LYP method is superior to the HF method for the vibrational frequencies.

## 1. Introduction

2-Aminopyridine (2AP) (a.k.a. 2-pyridinamine), $(C_5H_4N)NH_2$, is used in the synthesis of pharmaceuticals and polymer industry. 2AP and phosphoric acid system show rich polymorphism depending on crystallization conditions. Three anhydrate phases and one monohydrate form were isolated earlier [1,2]. It is reported that α-form of 2-aminopyridinium dihydrogenphosphate (2APDP) displays ferroelectric phase transition and this phenomenon is important for technologic materials [1,2].

Pyridine derivatives are widely used to synthesize many organic and organo-metallic compounds such as coordination compounds and metal phosphates. Metal phosphates are a member of open-framework materials family; the preparation of phosphate containing materials is attracting much more interest because of their application areas, such as catalysts, ion-exchangers and optic materials [3,4]. Although ammonium phosphates are known to occur as by-products in the synthesis of metal phosphates [5], it is believed that they could act as intermediates in the formation of metal phosphates and they can be used in order to synthesize metal-phosphates under relatively mild conditions [6-8]. They exhibit interesting crystal packing motifs strongly influenced by hydrogen bonds, also [9-13].

Complementary to our earlier works [9-13], herein we present our results on the experimental and theoretical IR study of β-2APDP. The optimized molecular geometry and vibrational frequencies have been calculated using ab initio Hartree-Fock (HF) and density functional theory (B3LYP) methods with 6-311++G(d,p) basis set level. The calculated optimized geometric parameters and vibrational frequencies have been compared with the corresponding experimental data.

## 2. Experimental details

β-2APDP can be synthesized according to the literature [2,14]. We have used the same sample synthesized and characterized in our earlier work [14]. The FT-IR spectra were recorded from KBr pellets in the range $4000-400\,\mathrm{cm}^{-1}$ using a JASCO FT/IR-430 spectrophotometer. Single crystal X-ray data collection and structure solution procedures were explained earlier [14].

## 3. Computational methods

The optimized structure parameters and vibrational frequencies of β-2APDP have been calculated using ab initio Hartree-Fock and density function theory (B3LYP) method at 6-311++G(d,p) basis set level. All computations have been performed using Gaussian 03 program package on high performance computing system [15]. ## 4. Results and discussion

The molecular structure of β-2APDP is given in Fig. 1. It is a molecule having 21 atoms, which belongs to the point group C1. The three Cartesian displacements of the 21 atoms provide 63 normal modes of vibration of which 57 are internal modes. The calcu- lated total energy value (corrected with zero point energy) of the molecule is -943.814028 Hartree for HF and -947.897250 Hartree for B3LYP, respectively.

### 4.1. Molecular structures

The geometric structure of β-2APDP is monoclinic, the space group $P2_1$, with the cell dimensions $a=9.0502\ Å$, $b=4.5260\ Å$, $c=9.9697\ Å$, $\beta=98.576^\circ$, $V=403.80\ Å^3$ [14]. As seen from Fig. 1, the hydrogen bonds have intermolecularly an attractive interaction with the electronegative oxygen atoms of $PO_4$ ion. The geomet- ric parameters (bond lengths and bond angles) of the calculated optimized structure of β-2APDP in accordance with the atom num- bers in Fig. 1 are given in Table 1. The table also compares the calculated structure parameters with those obtained experimen- tally from X-ray data for the geometric structure of β-2APDP [14]. As seen, there is a reasonable agreement between the calculated and experimental geometric parameters. The largest differences of the calculated ground state geometry from the experimental one are $0.222\ Å$ for N2-H8 bond length and $6.6^\circ$ for O3-P1-O1 bond angle. Demir et al. have reported that N1-H7⋯O3 hydrogen bond length and angle are $1.820\ Å$ and $165.0^\circ$ and N2-H8⋯O4 hydro- gen bond length and angle are $2.080\ Å$ and $179.0^\circ$, respectively [14]. In our calculations, these parameters are $1.459\ Å$ and $174.8^\circ$ for N1-H7⋯O3 and $1.653\ Å$ and $177.2^\circ$ for N2-H8⋯O4. Also, the dihedral angle N1-N2-O4-O3 is $5.6^\circ$ for B3LYP calculations.

As seen from Table 2, the RMS and mean vibrational deviations for the B3LYP method is lower than those for the HF method. Also, the correlation value for B3LYP is better than the one for HF.

The O-H stretching modes are calculated at 3501-3499 cm⁻¹ (HF) and 3683-3680 cm⁻¹ (B3LYP). This mode is experimentally observed at 3334 cm⁻¹ in the FT-IR spectrum. The N-H stretching mode of amino group is calculated at 3273 cm⁻¹ (HF) and 3520 cm⁻¹ (B3LYP). This mode is experimentally observed at 3176 cm⁻¹ in the FT-IR spectrum. These differences between the calculated and experimental data for the O-H and N-H stretching modes are due to large anharmonicity of X-H stretching modes in the highest frequency region of the spectrum and strong the intermolecular hydrogen bonds in the crystal structure.

One of the characteristic stretching mode is the hydrogen bonded N-H stretching modes in the pyridinium ring. These modes were calculated at 2785 cm⁻¹ and 2060 cm⁻¹. This N-H stretching modes are experimentally observed at 2785 cm⁻¹ and 1940 cm⁻¹. Another prominent vibration mode is the N-H in plane bending + N-C stretching mode at 1662 cm⁻¹ in the experimental FT-IR spectrum. Jeyakumari et al. have observed this band at 1662 cm⁻¹ for 2-amino-5-nitropyridinium dihydrogenphosphate [21]. This mentioned characteristic mode is observed as a very strong and sharp band in the experimental and calculated spectra.

A free $PO_4^{3-}$ ion which is almost ideal tetrahedron under $T_d$ symmetry has four fundamental vibrations, $\nu_1$, $\nu_2$, $\nu_3$, and $\nu_4$. While all four modes are Raman active, only $\nu_3$ (asymmetric stretching vibration) and $\nu_4$ (asymmetric bending vibration) are active in the IR. Since this symmetry is usually reduced in a crystal lattice the IR inactive $\nu_1$ and $\nu_2$ modes may become active [22]. The P-O stretching, the P-O-H and O-P-O in plane bending + out of plane bending modes are seen at the fingerprint region in the experimental and calculated spectra. $\nu_3$ vibrations of phosphate group are appeared as strong and sharp bands with shoulders at 1080 and 978 cm⁻¹. While the $\nu_4$ vibration is appeared at 530 cm⁻¹, the very weak band at 470 cm⁻¹ is attributed to the $\nu_2$ mode of phosphate group. The P-O stretching modes were calculated in 1170-790 cm⁻¹ (HF) and 1160-770 cm⁻¹ (B3LYP) intervals. Therefore we state that the experimental and calculated vibrational frequencies and assignments show a good agreement with the literature [21-27].

## 5. Conclusion

β-2APDP has been investigated experimentally and theoretically by IR spectroscopy. Its optimized structure parameters, vibrational frequencies and corresponding vibrational assignments have been calculated using ab initio HF and DFT (B3LYP) method with 6-311++G(d,p) basis set level. The calculated vibrational frequencies have been scaled by some scale factors obtained using the ratio of the frequency values of the strongest peaks in the experimental and calculated IR spectra. And such obtained scale factors were seen to cause the calculated vibrational frequencies well in agreement with the experimental data. Also, the detailed assignments with PED% analysis for the vibrational frequencies of the molecule have been presented. The comparison of the experimental and calculated results shows that they are in good agreement with each other. Besides it is also found that the B3LYP method has got higher conformity than the HF method.
