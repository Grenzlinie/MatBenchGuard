# Study of electronic structures of $\mathbf{CeRu_2Si_2}$ by $\mathbf{LDA+U}$ method

Michi-To Suzuki*, Hisatomo Harima

Department of Condensed Matter Physics, Kobe University, 1-1, Rokkodai, Nada, Kobe 657-8501, Japan

Available online 30 October 2006

## Abstract

Electronic structures of $CeRu_2Si_2$ are calculated by $LDA+U$ method extended for magnetic system with strong spin-orbit interaction. The $LDA+U$ method significantly improves the character of the ground state in the nonmagnetic state. The calculated electronic structures explain change of physical properties associated with metamagnetic transition. The Fermi surfaces after metamagnetic transition were reproduced well in this method, although the continuous change of electronic structure by metamagnetic transition is not described.

**Keywords:** f-electron system; Heavy fermion system; Electronic structure; $LDA+U$ method

$CeRu_2Si_2$ is known as a typical heavy fermion system with a nonmagnetic ground state. The heavy fermion state is characterized by a large value of electronic specific-heat coefficient $\gamma$~360 mJ/mol K$^2$ [1] and Pauli susceptibility $\chi=(150-200)\times10^{-9}$ m$^3$/mol [2]. This compound shows a sharp increase of magnetization around the critical magnetic field $H_\mathrm{M}$~7.7 T along the $c$-axis [1]. This magnetization process is referred as metamagnetic transition and has been studied extensively.

$C/T$ exhibits a sharp peak in $H$~$H_\mathrm{M}$ at sufficiently low temperatures, then the peak changes into a double-peak structure at higher temperatures [3]. This double peak of $C/T$ was explained by existence of singular DOS near the Fermi level. Measurements of the dHvA effect were performed for magnetic field ranges below and above $H_\mathrm{M}$ in $CeRu_2Si_2$ [4]. The frequencies above $H_\mathrm{M}$ can be explained well with the localized f-electron model from similarity with the dHvA frequencies of $LaRu_2Si_2$ [5]. Meanwhile, low-temperature magnetization measurements confirmed that $CeRu_2Si_2$ possess a finite crossover temperature to the Fermi-liquid ground state at $H$~$H_\mathrm{M}$ [6], and the recent magnetoresistance measurements also show the absence of a discontinuity, or of hysteresis at the metamagnetic transition [7]. These experimental results have caused ambiguities in the interpretation about change of the electronic structure around the metamagnetic transition.

$CeRu_2Si_2$ crystallizes in the body-centered tetragonal $ThCr_2Si_2$-type structure (Space group I4/mmm). Electronic structure calculation by the relativistic APW method based on the local density approximation (LDA) was carried out for $CeRu_2Si_2$ in paramagnetic state [8]. The calculated Fermi surfaces agree qualitatively with dHvA experiments below $H_\mathrm{M}$, but the quantitative agreement is not so well particularly in small frequency region. There are other studies for electronic structure of $CeRu_2Si_2$ within LDA [9]. But the change of electronic structure under magnetic fields is not well known. Therefore, theoretical study for electronic structures of the magnetic state is highly desired.

An $LDA+U$ method has been applied to many transition metal compounds, and shows remarkable improvement from LDA calculation [10]. This approach can be applied for f-electron systems by considering spin-flip components due to the strong spin-orbit coupling. We have applied $LDA+U$ method to $CeRu_2Si_2$ for a nonmagnetic and a magnetic state under magnetic field. We use FLAPW method to solve the one electron equation given by $LDA+U$ and LDA. In these calculations, crystal structure parameters are taken from experiments [11].

Fig. 1. Band structures and DOS of ${\rm CeRu_2Si_2}$ calculated by LDA $+U$ method for (a) a nonmagnetic state and (b) a magnetic state under $H=5$ T.

In LDA $+U$ calculations, $U=0.4$ [Ry] is used as a parameter and $|j=\frac{5}{2}, j_z=\pm\frac{5}{2}>$ occupied state is assumed for the initial density matrix, then the density matrix are determined self-consistently, as well as charge density.

Calculated band structures and density of states (DOS) are shown in Fig. 1. Fig. 1(a) shows the anomalous DOS structure which Fermi level is located between two peaks of DOS. The number of occupied electrons are $0.124(j_z=\pm\frac{5}{2})$, $0.119(j_z=\pm\frac{3}{2})$ and $0.109(j_z=\pm\frac{1}{2})$ for $j=\frac{5}{2}$ Kramers doublets of f electrons in the LDA, while they are $0.240(j_z=\pm\frac{5}{2})$, $0.020(j_z=\pm\frac{3}{2})$ and $0.018(j_z=\pm\frac{1}{2})$ in the LDA $+U$.

Measurements of magnetic susceptibility show strong magnetic anisotropy of ${\rm CeRu_2Si_2}$ [2]. Moreover, neutron diffraction experiment observe an almost pure $|\frac{5}{2},\frac{5}{2}>$ occupied ground state in Pauli paramagnetic phase and after the metamagnetic transition [11]. The LDA $+U$ method reproduces the ground-state which agrees with these experiments, while LDA calculation brings nearly isotropic occupied orbitals for three Kramers doublets with $j=\frac{5}{2}$. The Fermi surfaces are calculated from the result of the LDA $+U$ calculation and the corresponding angular dependence of dHvA frequencies are shown in Fig. 2(a). The LDA $+U$ calculation reproduces two small Fermi surfaces observed by dHvA experiments [4] whereas these do not appear in LDA calculation, which is not shown in this paper.

![](./images/812045553612357635_2.jpg)

Fig. 2. Calculated angular dependences of dHvA frequencies of ${\rm CeRu_2Si_2}$ for (a) a nonmagnetic state and (b) a magnetic state with magnetic field $H=5$ T.

Electronic structures are calculated under magnetic field by LDA $+U$ method. Applied magnetic field induces a large split for $|\frac{5}{2},\pm\frac{5}{2}>$ Kramers doublets as shown in

Fig. 1(b), then calculated 4f moment is $M_{\mathrm{f}}=1.25 \mu_{\mathrm{B}}$ for magnetic field $H=5 \mathrm{~T}$. Fig. 2(b) shows the angular dependence of dHvA frequencies for $\mathrm{CeRu}_{2} \mathrm{Si}_{2}$ at $H=5 \mathrm{~T}$. Calculated dHvA frequencies agree well with experiments for $\mathrm{LaRu}_{2} \mathrm{Si}_{2}$ except small split of 50th and 51st Fermi surfaces [5], because the 4f orbitals hardly affect the conduction bands.

These results explain the electronic structure before and after the metamagnetic transition of $\mathrm{CeRu}_{2} \mathrm{Si}_{2}$ observed by a experiments [4]. However, the metamagnetic transition in finite magnetic field is not reproduced in this study, since the magnetic state is strongly stabilized by large U, i.e. no solutions with a small moment under a finite magnetic field obtained. This indicates a method beyond Hartree fock treatment for electron-electron interaction is required to reproduce paramagnetic heavy fermion state.

To summarize, the present LDA $+U$ method improves the characteristics of the ground state from LDA calcula- tion. The magnetic state calculation reproduces well the electronic structure after metamagnetic transition as well. An attempt to describe a metamagnetic transition, i.e. continuous change of electronic structure from paramag- netic state to magnetic state with a large 4f magnetic moment, is a remaining issue.
