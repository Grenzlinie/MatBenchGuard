# THERMODYNAMICS OF THE BAINITIC TRANSFORMATION IN A Cu-Zn-Al ALLOY
T. Y. HSU (XU ZUYAO) and X. W. ZHOUT

Department of Materials Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai, P. R. China

(Received 8 October 1990; in revised form 25 April 1991)

**Abstract**—The driving forces of two possible bainitic reactions within the bainitic transformation temperature range have been calculated for a Cu-Zn-Al alloy. The results show that $\Delta G^{\beta' \to \alpha'} > 0$ and $\Delta G^{\beta' \to \beta_1' + \alpha} < 0$ in this transformation temperature range. Accordingly, the bainitic transformation can only proceed by the diffusional reaction $\beta' \to \beta_1' + \alpha$. Furthermore, the driving force of 9R ordering transition of $\alpha$ has also been calculated. On account of $\Delta G^{\alpha \to \alpha'} < 0$, the disordered $\alpha$ phase produced after the diffusional bainitic transformation may further transform into the ordered one with 9R structure.

**Résumé**—On calcule les forces motrices de deux réactions bainitiques possibles dans la gamme de température de la transformation bainitique pour un alliage Cu-Zn-Al. Les résultats montrent que $\Delta G^{\beta' \to \alpha'} > 0$ et $\Delta G^{\beta' \to \beta_1' + \alpha} < 0$ dans cette gamme de température de transformation. En conséquence, la transformation bainitique ne peut se développer que par la réaction de diffusion $\beta' \to \beta_1' + \alpha$. On calcule aussi la force motrice de la transition d'ordre 9R de la phase $\alpha$. En tenant compte que $\Delta G^{\alpha \to \alpha'} < 0$, la phase désordonnée $\alpha$ produite après la transformation bainitique par diffusion peut se transformer ultérieurement en une phase ordonnée de structure 9R.

**Zusammenfassung**—Die treibenden Kräfte zweier möglicher bainitischer Reaktionen innerhalb des Temperaturbereiches der bainitischen Transformation werden für eine Cu-Zn-Al-Legierung berechnet. Die Ergebnisse zeigen, daß in diesem Temperaturbereich $\Delta G^{\beta' \to \alpha'} > 0$ und $\Delta G^{\beta' \to \beta_1' + \alpha} < 0$ gilt. Entsprechend kann die bainitische Transformation nur über die Diffusionsreaktion $\beta' \to \beta_1' + \alpha$ ablaufen. Des weiteren wird die treibende Kraft für die Ordnungstransformation 9R in $\alpha$ berechnet. Wegen $G^{\alpha \to \alpha'} < 0$ kann sich die nach der diffusiven bainitischen Transformation entstandene Phase $\alpha$ sich in die geordnete mit der 9R-Struktur weiter umwandeln.

## 1. INTRODUCTION

Thermodynamic calculation has revealed that the bainitic transformation can only proceed by a diffu- sional mechanism in Cu-Zn alloys [1]. In a recent X-ray microanalytical study by *Wu et al.*, the bainite $(\alpha_1)$ of Cu-Zn-Al alloys was detected to have a different composition from the $\beta'$ phase even at initial stage of its formation, which greatly contradicts the results of TEM observation that the $\alpha_1$ phase inherits the ordering and the anti-phase boundaries of the $\beta'$ phase [2, 3]. By means of analytical electron- microscope studies, Nakata *et al.* [4] also found a noticeable composition difference between the $\alpha_1$ phase and the matrix phase of Cu-Zn-Al alloys when the bainitic transformation was initiated. Accord- ing to the experimental results so far accumulated, Takezawa *et al.* [5, 6] concluded that the bainitic transformation of Cu-Zn-Al alloys have both the characteristics of diffusion and shearing. Based upon the thermodynamic treatment of Cu-Zn [1, 7–8], Cu-Al [9] and Cu-Zn-Al [10] alloys, this paper attempts to conduct a thermodynamic calculation for the bainitic transformation in the Cu-Zn-Al alloys, so that the transformation mechanism and the contradiction in the previous work [2, 3] may be clarified.

## 2. CALCULATIONS AND RESULTS

The bainite in the Cu-Zn-Al alloys forms from the $\beta'$ phase matrix possessing $B_2$ structure in the elev- ated temperature range $250$–$350^\circ$C. It is assumed that the $\alpha'$ phase have a 9R structure after the martensitic shearing, and the $\beta_1'$ phase has a different compo- sition from the $\beta'$ phase in the diffusional reaction owing to the redistribution of solute atoms. The bainite $\alpha_1$ may be approximatedly treated as $\alpha$ phase with non-equilibrium composition [1].

The change in free energy attending the $\beta' \to \alpha'$ shearing can be written as
$$
\Delta G^{\beta' \to \alpha'} = \Delta G^{\beta' \to \beta} + \Delta G^{\beta \to \alpha} + \Delta G^{\alpha \to \alpha'} \tag{1}
$$
where, the sum of the change in free energy equals that for the internal energy for ordering [8], i.e.
$$
\begin{align*}
\Delta G^{\beta' \to \beta} + \Delta G^{\alpha \to \alpha'} &= -\Delta G^{\beta \to \beta'} + \Delta G^{\alpha \to \alpha'} \\
&= -\Delta U^{\beta \to \beta'} + \Delta U^{\alpha \to \alpha'}.
\end{align*}
$$

Treatment with the GBW model [11] yields [10]
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta U^{\beta \rightarrow \beta^{\prime}} & =0.5 N_{0} \eta_{1}^{2}\left[\frac{X_{\mathrm{Zn}}}{X_{\mathrm{Zn}}+X_{\mathrm{Al}}}\left(Z_{2} W_{\mathrm{CuZn}}^{(2)}-Z_{1} W_{\mathrm{CuZn}}^{(1)}\right)\right. \\
& +\frac{X_{\mathrm{Al}}}{X_{\mathrm{Zn}}+X_{\mathrm{Al}}}\left(Z_{2} W_{\mathrm{CuAl}}^{(2)}-Z_{1} W_{\mathrm{CuAl}}^{(1)}\right) \\
& \left.-\frac{X_{\mathrm{Zn}} X_{\mathrm{Al}}}{\left(X_{\mathrm{Zn}}+X_{\mathrm{Al}}\right)^{2}}\left(Z_{2} W_{\mathrm{ZnAl}}^{(2)}-Z_{1} W_{\mathrm{ZnAl}}^{(1)}\right)\right] \text { (2) }
\end{aligned}
$$
and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta U^{\alpha \rightarrow \alpha^{\prime}} & =2 N_{0} \eta_{1}^{2}\left[-\frac{X_{\mathrm{Zn}}}{X_{\mathrm{Zn}}+X_{\mathrm{Al}}} W_{\mathrm{CuZn}}^{\alpha}\right. \\
& \left.-\frac{X_{\mathrm{Al}}}{X_{\mathrm{Zn}}+X_{\mathrm{Al}}} W_{\mathrm{CuAl}}^{\alpha}+\frac{X_{\mathrm{Zn}} X_{\mathrm{Al}}}{\left(X_{\mathrm{Zn}}+X_{\mathrm{Al}}\right)^{2}} W_{\mathrm{ZnAl}}^{(2 \alpha)}\right]. \quad(3)
\end{aligned}
$$

Here, $W_{i r}^{(1)}$ and $W_{i r}^{(2)}$ represent respectively the nearest and next nearest neighbor chemical interchange energies in the $\beta$ phase for each binary alloys, and $W_{i r}^{\alpha}$ represent those values of the $\alpha$ phase, $Z_{1}=8$ and $Z_{2}=6$ are the number of nearest and next nearest neighboring atoms in the $\beta, N_{0}$ is Avogadró's constant, $X_{i}$ is mole fraction, $\eta_{1}=P_{\mathrm{Cu} a}-X_{\mathrm{Cu}}$ stands for the ordering degree, while $P_{\mathrm{Cu} a}$ is the occupation probability of $\mathrm{Cu}$ atoms on the $a$ sublattice [7]. Approximated by the regular solution model, the change in free energy accompanying $\beta \rightarrow \alpha$ should be
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta G^{\beta \rightarrow \alpha} & =X_{\mathrm{Cu}} \Delta G_{\mathrm{Cu}}^{\beta \rightarrow \alpha}+X_{\mathrm{Zn}} \Delta G_{\mathrm{Zn}}^{\beta \rightarrow \alpha} \\
& +X_{\mathrm{Al}} \Delta G_{\mathrm{Al}}^{\beta \rightarrow \alpha}+\left(E_{\mathrm{CuZn}}^{\alpha}-E_{\mathrm{CuZn}}^{\beta}\right) X_{\mathrm{Cu}} X_{\mathrm{Zn}} \\
& +\left(E_{\mathrm{CuAl}}^{\alpha}-E_{\mathrm{CuAl}}^{\beta}\right) X_{\mathrm{Cu}} X_{\mathrm{Al}} \\
& +\left(E_{\mathrm{ZnAl}}^{\alpha}-E_{\mathrm{ZnAl}}^{\beta}\right) X_{\mathrm{Zn}} X_{\mathrm{Al}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here $\Delta G_{i}^{\beta \rightarrow \alpha}$ stands for the lattice stability parameters, $E_{i r}^{\alpha}$ and $E_{i r}^{\beta}$ represent respectively the interaction parameters of components in the $\alpha$ and $\beta$ phases. The so far accumulated data of chemical interchange energies are listed as follows [8,12-16]
$$
\begin{gathered}
W_{\mathrm{CuZn}}^{(1)}=955 \mathrm{~K}, \quad W_{\mathrm{CuZn}}^{(2)}=535 \mathrm{~K}, \quad W_{\mathrm{CuAl}}^{(1)}=1345 \mathrm{~K}, \\
W_{\mathrm{CuAl}}^{(2)}=825 \mathrm{~K}, \quad W_{\mathrm{ZnAl}}^{(1)}=-50 \mathrm{~K}, \quad W_{\mathrm{ZnAl}}^{(2)}=200 \mathrm{~K}, \\
W_{\mathrm{CuZn}}^{\alpha}=582 \mathrm{~K}, \quad W_{\mathrm{CuAl}}^{\mathrm{M}}=1459 \mathrm{~K}, \quad W_{\mathrm{ZnAl}}^{\mathrm{M}}=0, \\
\mathrm{~K}=13.8 \times 10^{-24} \mathrm{~J}.
\end{gathered}
$$

Among the above symbols, the superscript M refers to the martensite and its $W_{i r}^{\mathrm{M}}$ may approximately replace $W_{i r}^{\alpha}$ [9]. The interaction parameters derived from the activity data of $\mathrm{Cu}-\mathrm{Zn}$ system [7] are

## 3. DISCUSSION

As shown in Figs 1-4, the various driving forces
for the bainitic transformation in $Cu-Zu-Al$ ternary
alloys have been calculated with the weighted average
data available on the binary systems according to
their compositions. The calculation should be reliable
because the data used agree strictly with the phase
diagrams of the $Cu-Zn$ and $Cu-Al$ systems. It can
be seen from Figs 1-3 that the ordering transition
raises all of the driving forces and causes them to

Because $\Delta G^{\alpha \to \alpha'}$ calculated in this paper remains negative within or below the bainitic transformation temperature range, the disordered $\alpha$ phase produced in the diffusional $\beta' \to \beta_1'+\alpha$ reaction of the bainitic transformation may further transform into the ordered $\alpha'$ phase during the subsequent holding or cooling process. Analogous to the study on Cu-Zn alloys [1], bainitic $\alpha_1$ was approximated as $\alpha$ or $\alpha'$. A simplified model was used in the calculation of $\Delta G^{\alpha \to \alpha'}$; the actual values of $\Delta G^{\alpha \to \alpha'}$ may be smaller.

## 4. CONCLUSION

Calculations of the various driving forces $\Delta G^{\beta' \to \alpha'}$, $\Delta G^{\beta' \to \beta_1'+\alpha}$ and $\Delta G^{\alpha \to \alpha'}$ etc. in the bainitic transform- ation for Cu-Zn-Al alloys shows that the bainitic transformation can only proceed by a diffusional mechanism $\beta' \to \beta_1'+\alpha$. The disordered $\alpha$ phase resulting from the diffusional bainitic transformation may then further transform into the ordered one with 9R structure.
