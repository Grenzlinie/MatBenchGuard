First-Principles Study on Site Preference of Aluminum Vacancy and Nitrogen Atoms in $\gamma$-Alon

Bingtian Tu, Hao Wang, $^{\dagger}$ Xiao Liu, Weimin Wang, and Zhengyi Fu

State Key Lab of Advanced Technology for Materials Synthesis and Processing, Wuhan University of Technology, Wuhan 430070, China

The disorder in $\gamma$-alon is caused by random arrangement of nitrogen atoms and aluminum vacancies. To understand the properties and electronic structure of $\gamma$-alon by theoretical methods, the most reasonable structure model is needed. We examined the site preference of nitrogen atoms and aluminum vacancies by first-principles density functional theory (DFT) calculations on $\text{Al}_{24}\text{O}_{24}\text{N}_{8}$ and $\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_{5}$. The calculated results for $\text{Al}_{24}\text{O}_{24}\text{N}_{8}$ with the lowest total energy indicated that nitrogen atoms prefer to be far away from each other, rather than in a completely random arrangement. The further investigation on $\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_{5}$ shows that the aluminum vacancies tend to possess octahedral sites and coordinate only with oxygen atoms. Evaluated by lattice variances ($D_{a}$ and $D_{\theta}$) and simulated XRD pattern, the most reasonable structure model of $\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_{5}$ has little deviation from the experimental results. The calculated bulk modulus of 200.9 GPa in $\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_{5}$ is slightly lower than the experimental value. The electronic structure reveals that the bonds of Al-N and Al-O have partially covalent and ionic characterization, while the covalent bond strength of Al-N is stronger than that of Al-O. The calculated band gap is 3.99 eV, which is much closer to the experimental 4.56 eV than previous suggestions.

### I. Introduction
S PINEL aluminum oxynitride ($\gamma$-alon) ceramics are well-known as new armor materials and thermo mechanically stable windows and domes, for its outstanding optical transparent properties in the range from ultraviolet to mid-infrared light and excellent mechanical properties at high temperature. $^{1-3}$ In addition, the rare earth or transition-metal cations-doped $\gamma$-alon has also shown luminescent properties and high chemical stability, which makes it possible for it to find potential application in advanced phosphors for plasma display, fluorescent lamp, and in white LEDs. $^{4,5}$ In fact, all these properties are derived intrinsically from the bond and crystal structure of $\gamma$-alon.

The $\gamma$-alon is a solid solution of $\text{Al}_{2}\text{O}_{3}$ and AlN. $^{3,6}$ The crystal structure has been determined to be cubic with a space group of $Fd3m$ by X-ray and neutron diffraction Rietveld peak profile refinement. $^{7,8}$ The nitrogen and oxygen atoms occupy the $32e$ sites altogether, and yield a pseudocubic close-packed (ccp) spatial arrangement. The aluminum atoms are located at the $16d$ and $8a$ sites. Because it is impossible to refine the positions of nitrogen and oxygen atoms separately, $^{8}$ the determined anionic positions are, in fact, the mean positions of $\text{N}^{3-}$ and $\text{O}^{2-}$. Three different structural models have been proposed to describe the disordered structure of $\gamma$-alon. $^{9,10}$ The constant anionic structural model (with the formula of $\text{Al}_{(64 + x)/3}V_{\text{Al}[(8 - x)/3]}\text{O}_{32 - x}\text{N}_{x}$, where $2 \leq x \leq 5$) has been proven to be the most reasonable one by experiments and theoretical calculations. $^{8,11,12}$ One to two aluminum vacancies are generated to keep the charge balance in the unit cell of $\gamma$-alon. The $\gamma$-alon with "ideal" spinel structure which has the composition of $\text{Al}_{24}\text{O}_{24}\text{N}_{8}$ ($x = 8$) is proved to be non-existent. The disordered structure of nitrogen atoms and vacancies from experimental determination makes it difficult to obtain the reasonable structural model for theoretical investigations. Using neutron diffraction for the crystalline and microstructure study of $\gamma$-alon, $^{8}$ it is demonstrated that the aluminum vacancies are distributed in the octahedral sites only. Subsequently, this suggestion was confirmed by *ab initio* calculations. $^{12}$ So far, there is still lack of investigation on the properties of $\gamma$-alon, because single crystals of this material could be hardly obtained using present processing routes. $^{13}$ A series of first-principles calculations have been successfully developed to study properties on designing and performance predicting of alon ceramics by Gazonas *et al*. $^{14}$ and Batyrev *et al*. $^{15}$ Even so, the details of the local structure of nitrogen atom substitution and aluminum vacancies in $\gamma$-alon are still not clear.

The first-principles calculation study is suggested to be one of the powerful and effective means to tackle structural problems. It was reported that first-principles calculation had been successfully performed to verify the site preference of disordered atoms in some defective crystal structures. $^{16-18}$ In the very recent report on mullites, Ching's *et al*. have accomplished a systematic and exploratory investigation using the first-principles approaches, aiming to probe into the structure and properties of well-known mullite phase with disorder structures in the crystal. $^{19}$ The results not only presented good agreement with experimental studies, but also provided essential investigations on the structure and properties which could be hardly obtained from experimental examinations.

In addition, theoretical calculation has also proven as an effective way to deal with the N atoms substitution and cationic vacancies in the oxynitride systems. Fang *et al*. pointed out that the anions are ordered in melilite-type structure of $\text{Y}_{2}\text{Si}_{3}\text{O}_{3}\text{N}_{4}$ through first-principles calculations. $^{20}$ The N atoms fully occupy the $8c$ site, and the O atoms fully occupy the $8f$ site. Their theoretical study on $\text{Si}_{5}\text{AlON}_{7}$ obtained the preferential distribution of O and N atoms in $\beta$- sialon. $^{21}$ Chen and Wang carried out first-principles study on the intrinsic defects of TaON. $^{22}$ Their results indicated that the $O_{\text{N}}$ antisite is the dominant defect in the binary tantalum oxide and nitride system. For the site preference of cation vacancies in $\gamma$-$\text{Al}_{2}\text{O}_{3}$ using first-principles study, Gutiérrez *et al*. demonstrated that the Al vacancies prefer to occupy the octahedral sites. $^{23}$

In this work, a series of calculations are performed by first-principles calculations based on density functional theory (DFT) $^{24,25}$ using the ultrasoft pseudopotential method. $^{26}$

The main goal is focused on clarifying the site preference of nitrogen atoms and aluminum vacancies, and figuring out the most reasonable crystal structure model of $\gamma$-alon. The ideal aluminum oxynitride phase ($\text{Al}_{24}\text{O}_{24}\text{N}_{8}$) consistent with the stoichiometric spinel ($\text{MgAl}_{2}\text{O}_{4}$) is firstly selected to investigate the site preference of nitrogen atoms in $\gamma$-alon, although this material has not yet been synthesized. The most reasonable distribution of nitrogen atoms would be obtained from the structure model of $\text{Al}_{24}\text{O}_{24}\text{N}_{8}$ with the lowest total energy. Then, the stable phase at room temperature ($\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_{5}$) which contains one vacancy in its unit cell is chosen to study the local structure of the aluminum vacancies in $\gamma$-alon. The lattice variances ($D_{\text{a}}$ and $D_{\theta}$) and simulated XRD pattern of optimized structure are utilized to examine the deviations of theoretical results from experiments. The elastic constants and electronic structure of $\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_{5}$ are calculated using the theoretical method. All results are verified by comparing with the experimental investigation. Finally, the reasonable structure model of $\gamma$-alon for theoretical studies will be put forward.

## II. Details of Calculation

First-principles total energy and structural optimization calculations based on DFT have been performed using CASTEP codes. $^{27}$ Simulations were carried out for a series of possible ordered configurations with one conventional unit cell, which is commonly used for the spinel structure, containing 55 atoms ($\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_{5}$) and 56 atoms ($\text{Al}_{24}\text{O}_{24}\text{N}_{8}$). Ultrasoft pseudopotential is used to describe the interactions between ions and electrons. The generalized gradient approximation (GGA)$^{28}$ and local density approximation (LDA)$^{29,30}$ are used to describe the exchange-correlation energy. The former is known to overestimate the volume of the unit cell, while the latter leads an opposite tendency. With convergence testing, the plane-wave cutoff energy of 380 eV is sufficient for all structural models. A (2, 2, 2) Monkhorst-Pack grid is employed to sample the Brillouin Zone. The geometries of the configurations are optimized using the Broyden-Fletcher-Goldfarb-Shanno minimizer in the *CASTEP* package with the following default convergence tolerances: $5 \times 10^{-6}$ eV for energy change, 0.001 eV/nm for maximum force, and $5 \times 10^{-5}$ nm for maximum displacement.

Because of the disordered occupancy of N and O atoms on $32e$ site and Al vacancies on the $16d$ or $8a$ sites in $\gamma$-alon spinel structure, the crystal structures of calculated models constructed in our research were all converted to $P1$ symmetry. As a result of symmetry changes, the optimized cell parameters diverge from cubic arrangement. The XRD patterns of these optimized structural models simulated by Mercury software package are used to compare with those of experimental refinement (ICSD #70733). $^{7}$ In addition, two parameters of the angle variance ($D_{\text{a}}$) and length variance ($D_{\theta}$) were introduced to describe the divergence of lattice parameters. Parameters $D_{\text{a}}$ and $D_{\theta}$ indicated below are deduced from quadratic elongation$^{31}$ which were employed to measure the polyhedral distortion.

$$
D_{\mathrm{a}}=\sum_{i=1}^{3}\left(a_{\mathrm{i}}-a_{0}\right)^{2} \tag{1}
$$

$$
D_{\theta}=\sum_{i=1}^{3}\left(\theta_{\mathrm{i}}-90\right)^{2} \tag{2}
$$

where, $a_{\mathrm{i}}$ are the basis vectors lengths (equal to $a$, $b$, $c$), $\theta_{\mathrm{i}}$ are the angles (equal to $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$) from the geometry optimization calculations in degree, and $a_{0}$ is the experimental lattice parameter of $\gamma$-alon.

The total energy ($E$) of structural model with different cell volume ($V$) was calculated. The Birch-Murnaghan equation of states (EoS)$^{32,33}$ was chosen to fit the $E(V)$ data:

$$
\begin{aligned}
E(V) & =E_{0}+\frac{9 V_{0} B_{0}}{16}\left\{\left[\left(\frac{V_{0}}{V}\right)^{2 / 3}-1\right]^{3} B^{\prime}\right. \\
& \left.+\left[\left(\frac{V_{0}}{V}\right)^{2 / 3}-1\right]^{2}\left[6-4\left(\frac{V_{0}}{V}\right)^{2 / 3}\right]\right\}
\end{aligned} \tag{3}
$$

The values of equilibrium total energy ($E_{0}$), volume of minimum total energy ($V_{0}$), zero-pressure bulk modulus ($B_{0}$), and bulk modulus pressure derivative ($B'$) could be obtained from fitting process.

## III. Results and Discussion

### (1) The Site Preference of Nitrogen Atoms in $\text{Al}_{24}\text{O}_{24}\text{N}_{8}$

Nine octahedral Al atoms have one N nearest neighbor ($9 \times \text{AlO}_5\text{N}$); three octahedral Al atoms have two N atoms neighbor ($3 \times \text{AlO}_4\text{N}_2$), and remaining four octahedral Al atoms coordinate with O atoms only ($4 \times \text{AlO}_6$). There are five different possible coordination environments for aluminum vacancy ($V$). Concerned with the numbers of the first and second nearest nitrogen neighbors of vacancy, the structural models can be only described as seven configurations (model 1–7), as shown in Table II.

The unit cells of all models were relaxed together with atoms. The calculated results are listed in Table II. The Al vacancy in the most stable configuration of $\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_5$ (model 1) prefers the octahedral site coordinating with only O atoms. The Al vacancy with one or more N atoms as nearest neighbor would lead to the increase in the total energy. The N atoms on second nearest anionic neighbor of vacancies show weak effect on the stability of crystal structure. This result had been discussed in the previous researches$^{3,36}$ and could be understandable for the following reasons. The effective charge of nitrogen atoms ($-q_\text{N}$) is higher than that of oxygen atoms ($-q_\text{O}$) in $\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_5$, i.e. $q_\text{N} > q_\text{O}$. According to Pauling second rule, the effective charge of N atoms at the second nearest neighbor of Al vacancy is balanced by an equal opposite charge of the surrounding four Al atoms. Each Al atoms surrounding N atom afford $q_\text{N}/4$ positive charges. For N atoms in the first nearest neighbor of vacancy, the negative charges are only balanced by three Al atoms. The charge of each Al atom increased to $q_\text{N}/3$, which is $q_\text{N}/12$ higher than the former. Similarly, the charges of Al atoms coordinating with the O atoms increase $q_\text{O}/12$. The increased charge of Al atom would raise the local potential and cause an unstable crystal structure. This effect is more significant for the nitrogen atoms than that for oxygen atoms, because $q_\text{N}/12$ is larger than $q_\text{O}/12$. Therefore, the Al vacancies tend to coordinate only with O atoms, which leads to a more stable structure. The deductions are also supported by the theoretical calculations of $q_\text{O}$ and $q_\text{N}$ from Mulliken population analysis$^{37-39}$ given in paragraph 3 of part 3 in this section.

The electronic band gap is varied from 2.09 to 4.08 eV in different vacancy configurations. The band gap of 3.99 eV for the model 1 is larger than the calculated value reported in previous literature,$^{11,40}$ which range from 2.35 to 2.52 eV. Because the GGA method normally underestimates the band gap value,$^{41}$ comparing with experimental value (4.59 eV), our calculated value is acceptable. As the total energy of model 2 at 0 K with calculated band gap of 4.08 eV is close to that of model 1, it is possible that the model 2 with the tetrahedral Al vacancies exists at a higher experimental temperature.

After geometry optimization, the symmetries of all these structure models diverge from cubic. As shown in Table II, the model 1 with lowest total energy yields a minimal cell deviation ($D_a = 0.008$, $D_\theta = 0.013$). The lattice parameters are similar to being cubic. As illustrated in Fig. 4, the XRD patterns of the structural models and experimental refinement (ICSD #70733) were simulated. For the cell parameters that were overestimated by GGA methods, the diffraction peaks slightly shift to lower $2\theta$ angle. Because the model construction lowers the $Fd3m$ symmetry of $\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_5$, three weak peaks appearing between $20^\circ$ and $30^\circ$ of $2\theta$ correspond to (2 0 0), (2 1 0), and (2 2 1) planes, respectively. While according to systematic absence law, these three peaks disappear in XRD of experimental structure with $Fd3m$ symmetry group. In addition, the obvious splitting of peaks at $2\theta = 31^\circ$, $37^\circ$, $56^\circ$, $60^\circ$, and $66^\circ$ in XRD of model 2 to model 7 is strongly suggesting an significant structural distortion from the experimental structure. Obviously, the simulated XRD pattern of model 1 is coincides better with the pattern from experimental result than the other models. Consequently, it is suggested that the structural model 1 with the longest $d_{\text{N-N}'}$ and $VO_6$ octahedral coordination is suitable for further researches of electronic structure and elastic properties of $\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_5$.

### (3) Electronic Structure and Elastic Constants of $\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_5$
The most reasonable structural model with Al vacancy at (7/8, 7/8, 5/8) site was chosen to investigate the electronic structure and mechanical property of $\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_5$. For this configuration, total energies of cubic $\text{Al}_{23}\text{O}_{27}\text{N}_5$ at different

volumes were calculated. Through fitting the total energy versus volume curve by Birch-Murnaghan equation of states (as shown in Fig. 5), an equilibrium volume of 514.22 and 487.32 $\mathring{A}^3$ were obtained using GGA and LDA method, respectively. All the calculated results are listed in Table III. These simulated volumes slightly deviate from the experimental result (503.67 $\mathring{A}^3$).$^{34}$ It was overestimated $\sim2.09\%$ by GGA, and underestimated $\sim3.25\%$ by LDA, while these deviations were also presented for the bulk modulus calculations. Thus, we suggest that the exchange-correlation energy could be described more accurately using GGA method than LDA method in our calculations. The GGA method is more suitable for further investigation of electronic structure and elastic constants in $\gamma$-alon. The elastic constants were also calculated by energy-displacement method using GGA for $\gamma$-alon. The $C_{11}$, $C_{12}$, and $C_{44}$ are, respectively, 303.77, 149.30, and 168.82 GPa, which is close to the experimental value of 369.24, 122.66, and 123.92 GPa determined by ultra-sound method.$^{42}$ The bulk modulus (200.90 GPa), simulated from the elastic constants, is smaller than the experimental result (215.81 GPa) gained by McCauley *et al.*$^3$

In the electron density of states (DOS), as shown in Fig. 6, the top valence bands (VB) are mainly from N atoms. The lowest bands situated in the range from $-20.30$ or $-16.57$ eV originate from O $2s$ and Al $3p$ state. The N $2s$ state with bandwidth of 1.4 eV means more localization than O $2s$ states (with bandwidth of 4.0 eV). A gap in the width of 2.5 eV exists between O $2s$ and N $2s$ bands. The wide VB, consisting of O $2p$, N $2p$, and Al $2s$ $2p$, denote nonlocalized electrons of all compositions in $\gamma$-alon. The upper VB in calculated total DOS is dominated by N $2p$ bands. The conduction bands (CB) are mostly composed of Al $3p$ states hybridizing with some O $2p$ states. The band gap 3.99 eV means a highly insulation property in $\gamma$-alon materials. It is obvious that the upper VB structure could be changed by varying the ratio of nitrogen atoms in the anionic sublattice. As the optical absorption in ultraviolet range is always corresponding to the VB-to-CB transition, it is suggested that the transparent property of $\gamma$-alon could be affected by the content of nitrogen atoms.

The site-preferences of aluminum vacancies and nitrogen atoms in $\gamma$-alon have been successfully extracted from the first-principles calculations. It could be contributed to adequately model the spinel structure of $\gamma$-alon to account for the partially occupied sites, and then to make the theoretical results more accurate. In $\gamma$-alon, the increased AlN contents, with the associated decrease in aluminum vacancies, affect their bond states, crystal structures, elastic properties, and optical properties. Some experimental investigations had been carried out on this issue, $^{3}$ but more theoretical works are still especially required. The further investigations in our work are proposed to study on electronic structure, mechanical, and optical properties of $\gamma$-alon in different compositions by both experimental and theoretical investigations.

## IV. Conclusion

Using first-principles total energy calculations based on the DFT, a series of studies were performed on $Al_{23}O_{27}N_{5}$ and $Al_{24}O_{24}N_{8}$ to determine the site preference of the N substitution and Al vacancies in $\gamma$-alon. The calculation results demonstrate that the N atoms prefer to distribute far away from each other, and the Al vacancy prefers coordinating with oxygen atoms at the octahedral site in $\gamma$-alon. As for $Al_{23}O_{27}N_{5}$, the Al-O and Al-N bonds present iono-covalence character, and the covalent bond strength of Al-N is stronger than that of Al-O. The band gap is calculated to be 3.99 eV and the simulated bulk modulus from elastic constants calculation is 200.9 GPa, which is slightly lower than the experimental value. It would be anticipated that the further first-principles study on the mechanical and optical properties with different ratio of N/O in $\gamma$-alon would be also significant based on this model.
