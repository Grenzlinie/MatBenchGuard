# A high-pressure study of $\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$ by XRD and DFT

Lun Xiong(熊伦) $^{1*}$, Qiang Li(李强)$^1$, Cheng-Fu Yang(杨成福)$^1$, Qing-Shuang Xie(谢清爽)$^1$, and Jun-Ran Zhang(张俊然)$^{2,3**}$

$^1$School of Intelligent Manufacturing, Sichuan University of Arts and Science, Dazhou 635000, People's Republic of China;
$^2$Multidiscipline Research Center, Institute of High Energy Physics, Chinese Academy of Science, Beijing 100049, People's Republic of China;
$^3$School of Physical Sciences, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100049, People's Republic of China

The equation of state (EOS) of $\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$ at high pressure has been studied by means of synchrotron radiation X-ray diffraction (XRD) in a diamond anvil cell (DAC) at ambient temperature, and density functional theory (DFT). XRD analysis showed that the orthorhombic structure was maintained to the maximum pressure of 44.5 GPa. XRD data showed that bulk modulus is $K_0$=292 (18) GPA with $K_0'$=3.25(0.85). In addition, the high-pressure compression behavior of $\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$ was studied by first principles calculations. The obtained bulk modulus of $\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$ is 323 (1) GPa.

Keywords: Equation of state; $\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$; High pressure; *In-situ* XRD; First principles calculations

PACS: 64.30.Jk, 64.60.-i, 07.35.+k, 61.05.cp

## 1. Introduction

$\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$ is a kind of high melting point material with good wear resistance, corrosion resistance and oxidation resistance in high temperature environment. It can be widely used in aircraft engines and petrochemical mechanical devices, and can greatly improve the service life of machinery.$^{[1-3]}$ So far, $\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$ has aroused great interest of researchers.$^{[4-10]}$ Kuriyama et al. investigated the electrical conductivity of the $\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$ on different sintering temperatures.$^{[4]}$ Ma et al. obtained the hardness with Hv=18 GPa of $\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$.$^{[5]}$ Hirota et al. derived a hardness of Hv=18.9 GPa on $\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$.$^{[6]}$ Min et al. calculated the structure, electronic properties etc. of $\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$ using density functional theory.$^{[7]}$ Li et al. calculated hardness of 20.9 GPa of $\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$ with first principles.$^{[8]}$ Jiang investigated the structure, elastic and electronic properties of

$Cr_3C_2$ using first principles calculations.$^{[9]}$ Jiang et al. obtained the hardness of 20.3 GPa with $Cr_3C_2$.$^{[10]}$

Pressure can reduce the atomic distance, change the electronic shell state and crystal cell structure, and then change its structure, physical and chemical properties, such as high-pressure structural phase transition, high-pressure strength, high-pressure texture.$^{[11]}$ High pressure science is an interdisciplinary science, which has been integrated with physics, material science, geoscience, chemistry and other disciplines, greatly promoting the application and development of itself and its related fields, and is an important field of basic research and applied science research. The physical and chemical properties of $Cr_3C_2$ under high pressure are closely related to its crystal composition. Therefore, a thorough understanding of the crystal composition of $Cr_3C_2$ is helpful to improve the scientific use of $Cr_3C_2$.

Although $Cr_3C_2$ has been studied,$^{[4-10]}$ there is no direct experimental measurement or theoretical calculation for its high-pressure EOS. Therefore, the EOS of $Cr_3C_2$ to 44.5 GPa was studied in DAC with silicon oil as pressure medium using angle dispersive XRD technology, and the EOS of $Cr_3C_2$ to 50 GPa was calculated with DFT in this study.

### 2. Experimental details

$Cr_3C_2$(99.5%) samples was purchased from the company. Under ambient conditions, $Cr_3C_2$ has an orthorhombic structure (the space group is Pnam, as shown in Fig. 1). We used the scanning electron microscope (SEM) to analyze the the sample of $Cr_3C_2$ . SEM test of $Cr_3C_2$ was completed in the analytical and testing center of Sichuan University. The model of SEM instrument is JSM-7500F, resolution is 1.0nm (15kV), and magnification is 25-800000. The SEM image shows that the average cluster size of the black polycrystalline powders is 3$\mu$m (see Fig. 2), and the grain size of $Cr_3C_2$ is homogeneous. The X-ray diffraction experiment of copper target was completed in the analytical and testing center of Sichuan University. The model of XRD instrument is EMPYREAN, $2\theta$ angle range is -110 degrees to 162 degrees, minimum controllable step is 0.0001 degrees, resolution is FWHM=0.028°, and wavelength is $\lambda$=1.5406 $\mathring{A}$. Figure 3 shows the representative XRD patterns of $Cr_3C_2$ powder under ambient conditions, and the derived lattice parameters are a=5.532(0.001) $\mathring{A}$, b=11.500(0.002) $\mathring{A}$, c=2.8318(0.0003) $\mathring{A}$, respectively. In the high-pressure XRD experiment, a modified Mao bell DAC with a diameter of 300 $\mu$m was used. We pre-indented a T301

gasket to $\sim35\ \mu\text{m}$, and made sample hole with diameter of $120\ \mu\text{m}$ by laser. We used Silicone oil as a pressure medium. A ruby chip with a diameter of $10\ \mu\text{m}$ was placed on the top of the sample center, and used as a pressure sensor$^{[12]}$.

Each XRD pattern was given 5-8 minutes for exposure. We used fit2d software$^{[13]}$ to process and analyze.

### 3. Theoretical calculation details

DFT calculations were carried out using Vienna Ab-initio Simulation Package (VASP)$^{[14]}$. In addition, DFT calculations include structural optimization and enthalpy, which uses Perdew-Burke-Ernzerho$^{[15]}$ for exchange-correlation functions. The interaction between the real electron and the valence electron of the ion is calculated by the method of projection affixed plane wave (PAW). The valence electrons of C and Cr are $2s^22p^2$ and $3d^54s^1$, respectively. Monkhorst pack method is used for Brillouin region integration of $Cr_3C_2$ system, and the integration grid is 8×8×8. The truncation energy of plane wave basis function is measured, and its convergence accuracy is less than $2×10^{-3}$ eV, and the value of 600 eV is given. $Cr_3C_2$ has a orthorhombic structure (space group Pnam) with lattice parameters $a$=5.4767 Å, $b$=11.4621 Å, $c$=2.7882 Å, $\alpha$=$\beta$=$\gamma$=$90^o$ at ambient conditions. Using the third-order Birch-Murnaghan equation to fit the vloume-pressure data, the bulk modulus($K_0$) and its first derivative ($K_0'$) are obtained.

### 4. Results and discussion

The diffraction patterns obtained are analyzed with fit2d software$^{[13]}$. The maximum pressure of the experiment is 44.5 GPa, and the pressure value is given by ruby sensor$^{[12]}$. The diffraction patterns of $Cr_3C_2$ at different pressures are shown in Fig. 4. Several peaks of (011), (101), (121), (230), (151) can be seen in the whole pressure range. The orthorhombic

structure is maintained to a maximum pressure of 44.5 GPa.

![](./images/812645390842068994_4.jpg)

Fig. 4. Representative XRD patterns of Cr₃C₂ under different pressures.

The lattice parameters and the equivalent unit cell volume of Cr₃C₂ under different pressures were fitted by $d$-spacings of $d$(011), $d$(101), $d$(121), $d$(230), $d$(151). The compressibility of $a/a_0$, $b/b_0$, $c/c_0$ in orthorhombic structure of Cr₃C₂ is shown in Fig. 5, respectively. The $a$, $b$ and $c$ axes of the orthorhombic system decrease with the increase of pressure.

![](./images/812645390842068994_6.jpg)

Fig. 5. The compressibility of the lattice parameters of $Cr_3C_2$ and the generalized-dgradient approximation (GGA) calculation result.

The unit cell volume of the compression curve of experimental results and GGA calculations results are shown in Figure 6. The results show that the orthorhombic structure of $Cr_3C_2$ is stable to the highest pressure of 44.5 GPa, and there is no phase transition.

![](./images/812645390842068994_7.jpg)

Fig. 6. Compression curve of $Cr_3C_2$ derived from lattice parameters. The line is a Birch-Murnaghan fitting line based on the experimental datae. The dotted line is Birch-Murnaghan equation fitting to the theoretical calculation result.

The third-order Birch-Murnaghan equation was used to fit the change of cell volume with pressure. And we can obtain the $K_0$ and $K_0'$ by the way. The third-order Birch-Murnaghan equation is expressed as follows$^{[16]}$

$$
P=1.5 K_{0}\left[\left(\frac{V_{0}}{V}\right)^{\frac{7}{3}}-\left(\frac{V_{0}}{V}\right)^{\frac{5}{3}}\right]\left\{1+\frac{3}{4}\left(K_{0}^{\prime}-4\right)\left[\left(\frac{V_{0}}{V}\right)^{\frac{2}{3}}-1\right]\right\}, \tag{1}
$$

$K_0$, and $K_0'$ are the bulk modulus, its pressure derivate at ambient conditions, respectively. $V_0$ is the volume at ambient conditions.

The bulk modulus and its first derivative of the orthorhombic structure are 292(18) GPa and 3.25(0.85), respectively. The theoretical calculation result is 323 (1) GPa. It can be seen that the experimental bulk modulus of present work is 10% lower than the GGA calculation result of present work. As DFT transforms the real multi-electron problem into a single-electron problem to calculate single-electron effective potential. The total energy of electron gas is only a function of electron density, and the corresponding density is the ground state density of a single particle. The exchange correlation energy in the GGA approximation is related to density and the density gradient. In addition, the temperature of electronics in real materials at ambient conditions is not absolute zero compared to GGA approximation. Thus, the bulk modulus of $Cr_3C_2$ needs to be further studied. A summary of the bulk modulus ($K_0$), its pressure derivative ($K_0'$) of carbides obtained with various methods is shown in Table 1. It can be seen that, the elastic modulus of $Cr_3C_2$ is higher than those of TiC, ZrC and HfC.

We calculated the electronic structure of Cr₃C₂ at high pressure. Figure 7 depicts the electron band structure of Cr₃C₂ in the selected highly symmetric direction, where the Fermi level is set to zero. It can be seen from the band structure that Cr₃C₂ is a semimetallic compound, as its electron orbit occupies the Fermi energy level. In addition, the electronic structure of Cr₃C₂ has no change with the increase of pressure. With the aid of the electronic density of state (DOS), the structure of Cr₃C₂ can be well understood (Figure 8). It can be seen that, the DOS in the spin up and down from ambient pressure to 30 GPa in Figure 8 is equal, indicating that Cr₃C₂ is not magnetic in the whole pressure range.

### 5. Conclusion

In conclusion, we have studied the EOS of $\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$ to 44.5 GPa in the modified Mao-bell DAC under quasi-static compression by XRD at room temperature. The orthorhombic structure of $\text{Cr}_3\text{C}_2$ was stable to the highest pressure of 44.5 GPa without phase transition. The bulk modulus of XRD was 292 (18) GPa and its first derivative3.25(0.85). The calculated bulk modulus is 323(1) GPa. The results show that the bulk modulus calculated by GGA is

slightly higher than the experimental results.
