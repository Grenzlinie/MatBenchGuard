# Grain boundary structural and diffusive properties in ice from MD simulation

## Problem background
Grain boundaries, triple junctions, and the ice/vapor interface in polycrystalline ice influence its mechanical, diffusive, and electrical properties. Characterizing the local molecular structure and water mobility at these defects is essential for interpreting experimental measurements and modelling natural ice. Classical molecular dynamics simulations with the TIP4P/Ice water model can probe the hydrogen-bond network and diffusion at the nanoscale, making it possible to quantify the fraction of liquid-like bonds and molecules, and the corresponding diffusion coefficients.

## Approach
Generate polycrystalline ice configurations by placing spherical ice I<sub>h</sub> seeds (created with GenIce) into a slab of supercooled water in a simulation box that also contains a vapour region. Grow the seeds via constant-volume molecular dynamics at 250 K using GROMACS and the TIP4P/Ice force field until the system freezes and the potential energy stabilizes; use both two-grain and four-grain replicas. From the production trajectories, classify every hydrogen bond as ice-like or liquid-like using the real part of the twist order parameter (Re χ)² with a public threshold of 0.5, and classify every molecule as orientationally ordered or disordered using the orientational order parameter Δθ_i² with a public threshold of 150. Identify the grain-boundary, triple-junction, and ice/vapour-interface regions using the number-density profile of liquid-like bonds. Compute the grain-boundary thickness as the width where the liquid-like bond density exceeds half its peak value. In each region, calculate three fractions: the fraction of liquid-like hydrogen bonds (f_liq_bond), the fraction of orientationally disordered molecules (f_liq_mol), and the fraction of molecules with hydrogen-bond coordination different from four (f_NHBneq4). Compute mean-squared displacement (MSD) of water molecules initially located in each region, using only the displacement component parallel to the defect. Correct the MSD for the liquid-like fraction and fit the diffusive regime to a line to extract diffusion coefficients D_gb, D_tj, and D_surf.

## Reproduction target
Produce the structural classification fractions f_liq_bond, f_liq_mol and f_NHBneq4 for the grain boundary, triple junction and ice/vapour interface regions, the diffusion coefficients D_gb, D_tj and D_surf (in m²/s), and the grain-boundary thickness (in nm). The values must be obtained from molecular dynamics simulations of polycrystalline ice with the TIP4P/Ice model at 250 K, following the procedure described in the workflow steps. Aggregate all results into the structured JSON file /app/outputs/results.json.

## Workflow steps

### Step 1: Generate polycrystalline ice configurations
- Role: process
- Action: Using GROMACS and the TIP4P/Ice force field, prepare a water slab in a simulation box with a vapor region, insert spherical ice Ih seeds generated by GenIce, and perform constant-volume molecular dynamics at 250 K to grow the ice particles until the potential energy stabilizes. Run production trajectories for 2-grain and 4-grain replicas.
- Evidence: `/app/outputs/potential_energy_vs_time.png`

### Step 2: Measure grain-boundary thickness
- Role: process
- Action: From the production trajectories, use the bond-level twist order parameter with a publicly stated threshold to classify bonds as ice-like or liquid-like; compute number-density profiles across grain boundaries and define the grain-boundary thickness as the width where the liquid-like bond density exceeds half its peak value. Record the thickness.
- Evidence: `/app/outputs/boundary_thickness.txt`

### Step 3: Compute structural classification fractions
- Role: process
- Action: For grain-boundary, triple-junction and ice/vapor-interface regions (delineated by the thickness), compute the fraction of liquid-like hydrogen bonds using the twist parameter threshold, the fraction of orientationally disordered molecules using a molecule-level orientational order parameter threshold, and the fraction of molecules with hydrogen-bond coordination different from four. Store the fractions.
- Evidence: `/app/outputs/fractions.json`

### Step 4: Compute diffusion coefficients
- Role: process
- Action: Calculate mean-squared displacement (MSD) of water molecules initially located in the same three regions; only the displacement component parallel to the defect is used. Correct for the liquid-like fraction. Fit the parallel MSD in the diffusive regime to a linear function to extract the diffusion coefficients for the grain boundary, triple junction, and ice/vapor interface. Store the coefficients.
- Evidence: `/app/outputs/diffusion_coefficients.json`

### Step 5: Compile final reproduction results
- Role: scored (load-bearing)
- Action: Aggregate the grain-boundary thickness, the structural fractions for each region, and the diffusion coefficients into a single structured JSON file.
- Output file: `/app/outputs/results.json`
- Format: json
- Contract: {"grain_boundary_thickness": <float>, "grain_boundary": {"f_liq_bond": <float>, "f_liq_mol": <float>, "f_NHBneq4": <float>, "D": <float>}, "triple_junction": {"f_liq_bond": <float>, "f_liq_mol": <float>, "f_NHBneq4": <float>, "D": <float>}, "ice_vapor_interface": {"f_liq_bond": <float>, "f_liq_mol": <float>, "f_NHBneq4": <float>, "D": <float>}}
- Scoring: scored by hidden verifier

## How you are scored
A hidden verifier will read /app/outputs/results.json and compare each fraction, diffusion coefficient, and the thickness to reference values. Comparisons use tolerances appropriate for the numerical spread of an independently re-run MD simulation. The verifier may also evaluate structural consistency relationships among the reported quantities (e.g., ordering of diffusion coefficients). The final reward is a weighted sum of accuracy and consistency scores. Submitting the artifact file without having executed the full MD pipeline will not yield a high score, because the verifier expects values that reflect a genuine re-run of the protocol rather than trivial guesses or reproduced literature numbers.
