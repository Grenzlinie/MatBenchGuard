# Crystal Field Analyses of $\mathbf{Co^{2+}}$ Doped in $\mathbf{MgF_2}$ Crystal

Ramona Nistora $^{\text{a}}$, Laura Andreici $^{\text{b}}$ and Nicolae M. Avram $^{\text{a,b}}$

$^{\text{a}}$Department of Physics, West University of Timisoara, 300223-Timisoara, Romania.
$^{\text{b}}$Academy of Romanian Scientists, Independentei 54, 050094-Bucharest,Romania

**Abstract.** The aim of this paper is the crystal field analyses and the energy level calculation of $\text{Co}^{2+}$ ($3\text{d}^{7}$ electrons) doped in $\text{MgF}_{2}$ crystal. We have obtained energy levels scheme of $\text{Co}^{2+}$:$\text{MgF}_{2}$ in the frame of exchange charge model, taken into account the effect of the charge of ligands and covalence bonding between Co and fluorine anions. The fine structure of the energy levels due the spin-orbit interaction is also analyzed and possible Jahn-Teller effect is predicted. The obtained results are in satisfactory agreement with experimental data.

**Keywords:** crystal field theory, transition metals, spin-orbit coupling, Jahn-Teller effect.
**PACS:** 75.10.Dg, 71.20.Be, 71.70.Ej

## INTRODUCTION

Crystals of $\text{MgF}_{2}$ were used as a standard host material and its optical absorption and emission for the 3d transition-metal doped in this crystal are well known [1]. $\text{MgF}_{2}$ has a rutile-type structure, with space group P42/mnm, two formula units in a unit cell and lattice constant $\text{a=4.6213Å}$, $\text{c=3.0159Å}$ [2]. $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ ion is surrounded by six fluorines occupying the tops of distorted octahedra of $\text{D}_{2\text{h}}$ site symmetry [3]. By doping $\text{Co}^{2+}$ substitutes the $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ in $\text{MgF}_{2}$. Despite the optical properties of 3d ions in $\text{MgF}_{2}$ crystal have been widely investigated [1] and it should be point out that reports on the consistent crystal field analysis of $\text{Co}^{2+}$ doped $\text{MgF}_{2}$ crystal with calculation of crystal field parameters (CFP) from structural data are scarce. In this paper we present the results of application of the exchange charge model (ECM) of crystal field [4] to the calculation of the crystal field parameters and energy levels of $\text{Co}^{2+}$ ion in $\text{MgF}_{2}$.

## METHOD OF CALCULATIONS

In order to analysis the crystal field for $\text{Co}^{2+}$ doped in $\text{MgF}_{2}$ crystal we follow paper [5]. In the framework of the exchange charge of crystal field the energy levels of an impurity ion are considered as the eigenvalues of the following Hamiltonian:

$$
H = \sum_{p=2,4} \sum_{k=-p}^{p} \left( B_{p,q}^{k} + B_{p,S}^{k} \right) O_{p}^{k} \tag{1}

## CONCLUSIONS

The crystal field parameters and energy for lowest levels of Co²⁺ doped in MgF₂ crystal have been calculated in ECM. The obtained results are in satisfactory agreement with experimental data. For the calculation of the fine structure of these energy levels we diagonalized the Hamiltonian of the system taking into account spin-orbit interaction. The result indicates that total spin-orbit splitting of $^4$T₁g and $^4$T₂g excited states of Co²⁺:MgF₂ are larger than experimental one (Ham effect).This suggests the existence of the Jahn-Teller effect in these triplet states.
