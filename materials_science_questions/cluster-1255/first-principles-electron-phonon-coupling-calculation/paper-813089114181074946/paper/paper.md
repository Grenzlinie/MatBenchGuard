## ABSTRACT

This paper reports a theoretical study on the thermal red-shift of $R_1$-line (one component of $^2E_2 \rightarrow ^4A_2$ transition) for $LiAl_5O_8$: $Cr^{3+}$ crystal by using a full expression including both the static effect derived from the lattice thermal expansion and the vibrational effect coming from the electron-phonon interaction. The results indicate that the vibrational and static effects result in the red-shift and blue-shift of $R_1$-line, respectively, and the static effect in magnitude is about 19% of the vibrational effect. The true electron-phonon coupling parameter is nearly 1.23 times as large as the apparent one which was obtained by considering only the vibrational effect in the previous paper. So for explaining reasonably the thermal-shift of a spectral line in crystals and giving its true electron-phonon coupling parameter, one should take into account both the static and vibrational effects.

## 1. Introduction

$LiAl_5O_8$ has the inverse spinel structure. It can be applied as a host for luminescence (including upconversion) and phosphor materials when doped with transition-metal and rare-earth ions [1–5]. So the spectroscopic properties of these doped $LiAl_5O_8$ crystals have been considerably concerned [1–7]. Recently, the temperature dependences of luminescence properties for $LiAl_5O_8$: $Cr^{3+}$ crystal, a potential material for temperature sensor, were measured [7]. The measurement [7] gave the thermal red-shift of $R_1$-line (one component of $^2E_2 \rightarrow ^4A_2$ transition) at the temperature range about 20–780 K for $Cr^{3+}$ at the approximately trigonal octahedral $Al^{3+}$ site. Since the thermal shift is related to the application of temperature sensing, the reasonable explanation of the thermal red-shift of $R_1$-line is of significance. Li et al. [7] found that the thermal red-shift of $R_1$-line for $LiAl_5O_8$: $Cr^{3+}$ can be described and explained by using the expression

$$
\Delta E(R_1)=-\alpha\left(\frac{T}{T_D}\right)^4 \int_{0}^{T_D/T} \frac{x^3}{e^x - 1}dx \tag{1}
$$

with the electron-phonon coupling parameter $\alpha \approx 510\mathrm{cm}^{-1}$ and Debye temperature $T_D \approx 700 K$. This expression is obtained by the Raman two-phonon process related to the Debye model where only the vibrational effect stemming from electron-phonon interaction is taken into account [8]. In fact, the thermal-shift of a spectral line in crystals originates from two effects, the above vibrational effect and the static effect caused by the lattice thermal expansion [9–11]. So, the above explanation of thermal shift of $R_1$-line for $LiAl_5O_8$: $Cr^{3+}$ crystal by considering only the vibrational effect is not reasonable

The results are discussed.

## 2. Calculation

The reasonable expression consisting of both the static and vibrational effects for the thermal-shift of a spectral line in crystals is derived as [11]

$$
\Delta E(T)=\left(A-\alpha^{\prime}\right)\left(\frac{T}{T_{D}}\right)^{4} \int_{0}^{T_{D} / T} \frac{x^{3}}{e^{x}-1} d x
\tag{2}
$$

where $A$ is a parameter characterizing the static effect and $\alpha'$ denotes the true electron-phonon coupling parameters in the vibrational effect. Eq. (2) is almost the same as Eq. (1) except the coefficient change from $(A-\alpha')$ to $-\alpha$. So, Eq. (2) can also applied to describe and explain the thermal red-shift of $R_1$-line for $LiAl_5O_8$: $Cr^{3+}$ crystal with $T_D \approx 700$ K and

$$
\left(A-\alpha^{\prime}\right) \approx-\alpha \approx-510 \mathrm{cm}^{-1}
\tag{3}
$$

According to the above definitions, the parameters $A$ and $\alpha'$ defer to the correlation

$$
\frac{A}{-\alpha}=\frac{\Delta E_{s t.}(T)}{\Delta E_{v i b.}(T)}=\frac{\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{s t.}}{\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{v i b.}}
\tag{4}
$$

where $\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{s t.}$ and $\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{v i b.}$ stand for the static and vibrational parts in the temperature dependence of $R_1$-line near and above room temperature. Obviously, the ratio $t=\frac{A}{-\alpha}$ implies the relative importance of static effect.

As has been said before, the static part of thermal-shift of $R_1$-line is due to the lattice thermal expansion. So, we have

$$
\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{s t.}=\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d \ln R}\right)\left(\frac{d \ln R}{d T}\right)
\tag{5}
$$

in which $\frac{d \ln R}{d T}=\frac{1}{R} \frac{d R}{d T}$ is the linear thermal expansion coefficient near and above room temperature. For the $LiAl_5O_8$ under study, we find $\frac{d \ln R}{d T} \approx 6.7 \times 10^{-6} / K$ [12]. $\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d \ln R}$ presents the bond length dependence of $R_1$-line. From the pressure-induced $R_1$-line shift, the parameter [11]

$$
\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d \ln R} \approx 7100 \mathrm{~cm}^{-1}
\tag{6}
$$

was achieved for the $(CrO_6)^{9-}$ trigonal octahedral cluster in YAG: $Cr^{3+}$ crystal. This value can also be rationally used to similar approximately trigonal $(CrO_6)^{9-}$ cluster in the $LiAl_5O_8$: $Cr^{3+}$ crystal studied. Thus, we get

$$
\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{s t.} \approx 4.8 \times 10^{-2} \mathrm{~cm}^{-1} / K
\tag{7}
$$

The observed value of $\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{o b s.} \approx-21 \times 10^{-2} \mathrm{~cm}^{-1} / K$ for $LiAl_5O_8$: $Cr^{3+}$ near and above room temperature was found from the observed temperature dependence of $R_1$-line in Ref. [7]. Since the observed (or total) temperature dependence of $R_1$-line is the sum of static part and vibrational part, we therefore have

$$
\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{v i b.}=\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{o b s.}-\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{s t.} \approx-25.8 \times 10^{-2} \mathrm{~cm}^{-1} / K
\tag{8}
$$

Thus, from Eq. (4), we have

$$
t=\frac{A}{-\alpha^{\prime}} \approx-0.186
\tag{9}
$$

Substituting $A=-\alpha't$ into Eq. (3), we yield

$$
-\alpha^{\prime} t-\alpha^{\prime} \approx-510 \mathrm{cm}^{-1}
\tag{10}
$$

and so

$$
\alpha^{\prime} \approx 627 \mathrm{~cm}^{-1}, A=-\alpha^{\prime} t \approx 117 \mathrm{~cm}^{-1}
\tag{11}
$$

## 3. Discussion

The negative value of $\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{v i b.}$ implies that the vibrational effect results in the red-shift of $R_1$-line, whereas the positive value of $\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{s t.}$ indicates that the static effect leads to the blue-shift of $R_1$-line. Since the $\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{v i b.}$ in magnitude is larger than the $\left(\frac{d E\left(R_{1}\right)}{d T}\right)_{s t.}$, the observed thermal-shift of $R_1$-line for $LiAl_5O_8$: $Cr^{3+}$ crystal is of red-shift. Even so, since the

relative importance $t$ of static effect is about 19%, the static effect cannot be omitted in the studies of thermal-shift of $R_1$-line for $LiAl_5O_8$: $Cr^{3+}$ crystal. When the static effect is also considered, the true electron-phonon coupling parameter $\alpha'$ for $LiAl_5O_8$: $Cr^{3+}$ crystal is about 1.29 times as large as the corresponding apparent parameter $\alpha$ obtained by considering only the vibrational effect. So, for analyzing rationally the thermal shift of a spectral line in crystals and gaining its true electron-phonon coupling parameter, both the static and vibrational effects should be taken into account.
