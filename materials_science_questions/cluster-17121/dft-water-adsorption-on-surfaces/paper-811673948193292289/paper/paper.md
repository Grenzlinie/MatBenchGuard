## ABSTRACT

The structure optimizations of all configurations of $\ce{H_{2}O}$ and $\ce{NH_{3}}$ adsorbed on ZnO nanoclusters (ZnONCs), aromatic-like (AL-ZnONC), naphthalene-like (NLL-ZnONC), pyrene-like (PRL-ZnONC) and ZnO graphene-like nanosheets (ZnOGLNSs), coronene-like (CNL-ZnONS) and circumcoronene-like (CCL-ZnONS) were carried out using the B3LYP/LanL2DZ calculations. Adsorption energies of $\ce{H_{2}O}$ and $\ce{NH_{3}}$ on AL-ZnONC, NLL-ZnONC, PRL-ZnONC, CNL-ZnONS and CCL-ZnONS are reported. It was found that the $\ce{H_{2}O}$ adsorptions on the nanoclusters (ZnONCs) and nanosheets (ZnOGLNSs) are caused by interactions between (1) water oxygen and Zn surface atom, (2) water hydrogen and hydride hydrogen of the surface and (3) water hydrogen and oxygen surface atom. For the $\ce{NH_{3}}$ adsorptions occur by pointing its nitrogen toward Zn surface atom and this orientation is somewhat perpendicular to the surface planes of ZnONCs and ZnOGLNSs.

### 1. Introduction

The zinc oxide crystal faces have been studied by numerous experimental and theoretical approaches on adsorptions of $\ce{H_{2}O}$ [1-4], $\ce{NH_{3}}$ [5]. The ZnO materials were founded to be excellent performance in optics, electronics, piezoelectricity [1] and photo-electronics [6]. Due to the strong piezoelectricity of these ZnO materials, they can be used as transducers and sensors. Different surfaces of the planar graphite-like [7] and the wurtzite ZnO [8] structures have been studied. A great number of different shapes of ZnO nanostructures such as nanorods [9], nanowires [10-12], nanocombs [13], nanorings [14] and nanotubes [15,16] have been prepared and investigated their properties. It was found that ZnO nanotube, or nanorods, or nanowires ZnO have shown much higher sensitivity than polycrystalline ZnO at room temperature because of their higher surface-to-volume ratio and stronger dependence of electrical conductance on the amount of adsorbates [17-24]. Nevertheless, since ultrawide ZnO nanosheets which have high specific surface area were synthesized, [25] they are expected to have the highest surface-to-volume ratio and can be used as gas sensors. All the synthesized ZnO nanosheets have been found to be wurtzite or planar graphite-like structures [7,8] but they have never been expected to be graphene-like structures. Recently, the single ZnO monolayer with graphene-like structure and its elastic, piezoelectric, electronic and optical properties were theoretically studied [26]. However, electronic properties of ZnO graphene-like structures have not been found and their adsorptions of fundamental gases have never been investigated.

In the present paper, the adsorptions of $\ce{H_{2}O}$ and $\ce{NH_{3}}$ on various surface areas of ZnO nanoclusters (ZnONCs) i.e. aromatic-like (AL-ZnONC, $\ce{Zn_{3}O_{3}H_{6}}$), naphthalene-like (NLL-ZnONC, $\ce{Zn_{5}O_{5}H_{8}}$), pyrene-like (PRL-ZnONC, $\ce{Zn_{8}O_{8}H_{10}}$) and ZnO graphene-like nanosheets (ZnOGLNSs) i.e. coronene-like (CNL-ZnONS, $\ce{Zn_{12}O_{12}H_{12}}$) and circumcoronene-like (CCL-ZnONS, $\ce{Zn_{27}O_{27}H_{18}}$) have been studied using the B3LYP/LanL2DZ method.

### 2. Computational details

Structure optimizations of $\ce{H_{2}O}$ and $\ce{NH_{3}}$ adsorption configurations on the various sizes of hydrogen-terminated ZnONCs and ZnOGLNSs namely aromatic-like (AL-ZnONC, $\ce{Zn_{3}O_{3}H_{6}}$), naphthalene-like (NLL-ZnONC, $\ce{Zn_{5}O_{5}H_{8}}$), pyrene-like (PRL-ZnONC, $\ce{Zn_{8}O_{8}H_{10}}$), coronene-like (CNL-ZnONS, $\ce{Zn_{12}O_{12}H_{12}}$) and circumcoronene-like (CCL-ZnONS, $\ce{Zn_{27}O_{27}H_{18}}$) were carried out using density functional theory (DFT) method. The calculations have been performed with hybrid density functional B3LYP, the Becke three-parameter hybrid functional [27] combined with the Lee-Yang-Parr correlation functional [28], using the Los Alamos LanL2DZ split-valence basis set [29-31]. All calculations were performed with the Gaussian 03 program [32].

Initial adsorption configurations for all possible positions and orientations of H₂O adsorbed on the studied ZnO nanosheets were set by pointing oxygen and hydrogen atoms of H₂O molecule toward the Zn and O atoms on ZnO nanosheets, respectively. In the same manner, initial adsorption configurations for all possible positions and orientations of NH₃ adsorbed on the ZnO nanosheets were set by pointing nitrogen and hydrogen atoms of NH₃ molecule toward the Zn and O atoms on ZnO nanosheets, respectively.

The adsorption energies of H₂O, $\Delta E_{\text{ads}}(\text{H}_2\text{O})$ and NH₃, $\Delta E_{\text{ads}}(\text{NH}_3)$ adsorbed on the clean surface of ZnOGLNS have been computed by the following equations.

$$
\Delta E_{\text{ads}}(\text{H}_2\text{O}) = E_{\text{H}_2\text{O/ZnOGLNS}} - \left(E_{\text{H}_2\text{O}} + E_{\text{ZnOGLNS}}\right) \tag{1}
$$

$$
\Delta E_{\text{ads}}(\text{NH}_3) = E_{\text{NH}_3\text{/ZnOGLNS}} - \left(E_{\text{NH}_3} + E_{\text{ZnOGLNS}}\right) \tag{2}
$$

where $E_{\text{H}_2\text{O/ZnOGLNS}}$ and $E_{\text{NH}_3\text{/ZnOGLNS}}$ are total energies of H₂O and NH₃ adsorbed on the ZnOGLNS surface, respectively. $E_{\text{H}_2\text{O}}$, $E_{\text{NH}_3}$ and $E_{\text{ZnOGLNS}}$ are the total energies of the isolated H₂O, NH₃ and clean surface of ZnOGLNS, respectively.

## 3. Results and discussion

### 3.1. Adsorption energies of H₂O

The B3LYP/LanL2DZ-optimized structures of adsorption configurations of H₂O adsorbed on the rigid structures of the AL-ZnONC, NLL-ZnONC, PRL-ZnONC, CNL-ZnONS and CCL-ZnONS are shown in Fig. 1. These adsorption structures are symmetrically representative of all possible water adsorptions on the whole nanosheets of AL-ZnONC, NLL-ZnONC, PRL-ZnONC, CNL-ZnONS and CCL-ZnONS. Due to the adsorption configurations of water adsorbed on the ZnONCs and ZnOGLNSs and their molecular symmetries, all water molecules as minimum energy structures of their adsorptions can be plotted as shown in Fig. 2.

There is one type (called type I) of adsorption configuration of H₂O on the AL-ZnONC. The water adsorption, type I occurs by the water pointing its oxygen and hydrogen toward zinc and hydride hydrogen atoms of the surface, see Fig. 1a. The number of energy minima of H₂O adsorptions on the AL-ZnONC of three configurations was found for each side of its molecular planes. These energy minima were obtained from the structure optimizations of interaction configurations within one third of each side of the AL-ZnONC molecular area and H₂O adsorptions over the whole AL-ZnONC molecular area were generated using $C_{3\text{h}}$ symmetrical operation. The adsorption energy of H₂O on the AL-ZnONC is $-11.05$ kcal/mol as shown in Fig. 1a and Table 1. The adsorption energies of H₂O on the AL-ZnONC computed using the B3LYP/LanL2DZ method with BSSE correction, the B3LYP/SDD without and with BSSE correction and PBEPBE/LanL2DZ method were also listed in Table 1. It shows that the adsorption energy ($\Delta E_{\text{ads,BSSE}}$) computed at the B3LYP/LanL2DZ with BSSE correction for H₂O adsorption on the AL-ZnONC ($\Delta E_{\text{ads,BSSE}} = -7.76$ kcal/mol) is smaller than the adsorption energy without BSSE correction by 3.29 kcal/mol.

The adsorption configurations of H₂O on NLL-ZnONC of two types, type I and type II were found. The type II is the water adsorption by pointing its oxygen and hydrogen toward zinc and inner oxygen atoms of the surface, see Fig. 1b. The water adsorptions of type I are composed of adsorption position H₂O #1 ($-10.08$ kcal/mol, type I) and H₂O #2 ($-11.45$ kcal/mol, type I) and type II is H₂O #3 ($-13.78$ kcal/mol, type II).

### 3.2. Adsorption energies of $NH_3$

The B3LYP/LanL2DZ-optimized structures of adsorption configurations of $NH_3$ adsorbed on the rigid structures of the AL-ZnONC, NLL-ZnONC, PRL-ZnONC, CNL-ZnONS and CCL-ZnONS are shown in Fig. 4. These adsorption structures are symmetrically representatives of all possible water adsorptions on the whole nanosheets of AL-ZnONC, NLL-ZnONC, PRL-ZnONC, CNL-ZnONS and CCL-ZnONS.

Due to the adsorption configurations of ammonia adsorbed on the ZnONCs and ZnOGLNSs and their molecular symmetries, all ammonia molecules as minimum energy structures of their adsorptions can be plotted as shown in Fig. 5.

The adsorption bonds of $NH_3$ adsorbed on the AL-ZnONC and NLL-ZnONC take place as one type called type I. The adsorption energies of $NH_3$ adsorbed on the AL-ZnONC is $-10.84$ kcal/mol and on the NLL-ZnONC are $-9.26$, $-10.92$ and $-13.65$ kcal/mol as shown in Fig. 4a and b and Table 3.

The ammonia adsorption of type I occurs by the ammonia pointing its nitrogen towards zinc surface atom and all three hydrogen atoms align on three [Zn$\cdots$O] bonds of ZnONCs as shown in Fig. 5a and b.

## 4. Conclusions

The adsorption energies of $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ and $\text{NH}_3$ on the AL-ZnONC, NLL-ZnONC, PRL-ZnONC, CNL-ZnONS and CCL-ZnONS for all possible configurations were obtained at the B3LYP/LanL2DZ level of theory. It was concluded that the $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ adsorptions on the ZnONCs and ZnOGLNSs caused by interactions between (1) water oxygen and Zn surface atom, (2) water hydrogen and hydride hydrogen of the surface and (3) water hydrogen and oxygen surface atom. For the $\text{NH}_3$ adsorptions occur by pointing its nitrogen toward Zn surface atom and this orientation is somewhat perpendicular to the surface planes of the ZnONCs and ZnOGLNSs. All the geometries of adsorption configurations for $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ and $\text{NH}_3$ adsorbed on the ZnONCs and ZnOGLNSs and plots of all the $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ and $\text{NH}_3$ adsorption positions are presented. It was also found that all the $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ and $\text{NH}_3$ adsorptions on the AL-ZnONC, NLL-ZnONC, PRL-ZnONC, CNL-ZnONS and CCL-ZnONS hardly affect their energy gaps.
