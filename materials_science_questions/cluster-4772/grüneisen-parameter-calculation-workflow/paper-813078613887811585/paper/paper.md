# Pressure dependence of the Raman frequency calculated from the volume data close to the ferroelectric-paraelectric transition in $PbTiO_3$

Hamit Yurtseven$^{a}$ and Ali Kiraci$^{b}$

$^{a}$Department of Physics, Middle East Technical University, Ankara, Turkey; $^{b}$Inter-Curricular Courses Department, Cankaya University, Ankara, Turkey

## ABSTRACT
We calculate the pressure dependence of the Raman frequencies of some Raman modes by using the observed volume data through the mode Grüneisen parameters for the ferroelectri-paraelectric transition in $PbTiO_3$. The mode Grüneisen parameters which we have determined using the observed Raman frequencies for the soft modes, increase considerably with increasing pressure toward the transition pressure ($P_C$ $\sim$ 11 GPa) from the tetragonal (ferroelectric) to the cubic (paraelectric) phase in $PbTiO_3$. Variation of the mode Grüneisen parameter with the pressure is rather smooth for the other Raman modes studied as compared to the drastic change at $P_C$ for the soft modes in this ferroelectric material. Raman frequencies (energy shifts) of the modes which we have calculated, decrease from the ferroelectric to the paraelectric phase with the exception of the optical modes of E(3LO) and E(3TO) whose Raman frequencies increase with increasing pressure.

This decrease and/or increase in the energy shifts for the Raman modes which we have calculated as also observed experimentally, shows a continuous manner, indicating a second order character rather that the first order transition from the tetragonal to the cubic phase in $PbTiO_3$.

## 1. Introduction
$PbTiO_3$ undergoes a first order transition of a displacive type at $T_C = 493^\circ$C, which is associated with the soft modes [1]. This transition temperature decreases under hydrostatic pressure down to 300 K and at $P = 12.1$ GPa it changes to a second order type [2]. As the first order changes to a second order transition, a tricritical point (TCP) occurs in the high pressure region of $PbTiO_3$ [3].

The crystal structure of ferroelectric ($T < T_C$) $PbTiO_3$ is tetragonal with the lattice parameters of $a = 3.9046(1)$ Å and $c = 4.1440(2)$ Å (space group P4 mm) [4,5], whereas the cubic phase ($T{>}T_C$) has the space group Pm$\overline{3}$m [5]. Since the pressure effects on the behaviour of the tetragonal-cubic transition in $PbTiO_3$, experimental studies such as Raman scattering [7], optical absorption [8] and X-ray diffraction [5] have been performed. Pressure dependence of $PbTiO_3$ has also been studied theoretically mainly by Monte Carlo simulations [9, 10] and density-functional theory [11-13], as also indicated prevously [13].

Lattice dynamics in the displacive ferroelectric phase transition [14] has been investi- gated experimentally for $PbTiO_{3}$, in particular the possibility of observing critical effects near $T_{C}$ using the soft mode data for the $E(1 TO)$ and $A_{1}(1 TO)$ modes [1]. It has been statedthat critical effects have not been seen in the temperature dependence of the soft $A_{1}(1 TO)$  mode in $PbTiO_{3}$ [1]. In the case of $PbTiO_{3}$ thin films deposited on Pt/Si substrates, however, the soft $E(1 TO)$ and $A_{1}(1 TO)$ modes do not appear to soften further on heating toward the ferroelectric-paraelectric transition and since their damping factors are larger, they do not diverge near the phase transition [15]. The soft $A_{1}(1 TO)$ mode which appears on bulk single crystal of $PbTiO_{3}$ as expected theoretically to directly relate to the ferroelectricity on the basis of the mean-field and self-consistent phonon model calculations [16-18], has not been observed in $PbTiO_{3}$ thin films [15]. Raman studies on single crystal bulk $PbTiO_{3}$ show[19] that the soft mode frequency decreases non-linearly as $(T-T_{C})^{\alpha}$ where $\alpha \leq 0.5$ over a large temperature range as a function of temperature and vanishes at the phase transition, whereas in thin film superlattices it has been found only a linear decrease of soft mode E(1TO) frequency with temperature with no phase transition taking place in the tempera- ture region studied [20]. Although the driving force to the ferroelectric-paraelectric transi- tion in $PbTiO_{3}$ is the temperature rather than the pressure, the ferroelectric distortion is so sensitive to the external pressure that the Curie temperature of this compound can be reduced at higher pressures [13]. It has been observed that the volume of the unit cell, the order parameters $P_{S}$ (spontaneous polarization) and $\eta$ measuring the tetragonality of PbTiO, considering the lattice constants, decrease with the increasing of pressure, con- firming that the pressure suppresses the ferroelectricity of this compound [13]. It has also been observed that this decrease in volume is continuous (second order) whereas decrease in $P_{S}$ and $\eta$ shows a first order jump in the vicinity of the tetragonal-cubic transition in $PbTiO_{3}$ [13]. It was found that the phase transition temperature $T_{C}=747 ~K$ decreasesupon applying the high pressure with the coefficient $d T_{C} / d P=-65 ~K / GPa$ in $PbTiO_{3}$ [6]. It has also been found tetragonal (T)- to- monoclinic (M)- to- rhombohedral (R)- to- cubic (C) phase transition sequence induced by pressure in $PbTiO_{3}$ [11]. From the compu tations based on the density functional theory (DFT) it has been indicated that the linearityof the square mode frequency $\omega^{2}$ for phases of $T[E(1 TO), A_{1}(1 TO)], R[E(1 TO), A_{1}(1 TO)]$  and $C[F_{1 u}(1 TO)]$ with pressure is in accordance with the Curie-Weiss pressure law close to the phase transition [11].

In this study, we calculate the pressure dependence of the Raman frequencies of the soft modes $E(1 TO)$ and $A_{1}(1 TO)$ using the observed volume data [13] by means of the mode Grüneisen parameters for the tetragonal-cubic phase transition in $PbTiO_{3}$ . We also perform this calculation for those modes of $A_{1}(2 TO), E(2 TO), A_{1}(3 TO), B_{1}+E, E(3 LO)$ and $E(3 TO)$ in $PbTiO_{3}$ for its tetragonal-cubic transition. By using the observed Raman frequen cies [21] of those modes, their Grüneisen parameters are determined as a function of pres- sure in $PbTiO_{3}$ .

Below, in section 2 we give our calculations and results. Sections 3 and 4 give our discus- sion and conclusion, respectively.

## 2. Calculation and results

We first calculated the Raman frequencies of the soft modes of $A_1(\text{TO}_1)$ and $\text{E(1TO)}$ as a function of pressure using the volume data [13] by determining the mode Grüneisen parameter of those modes in $\text{PbTiO}_3$. The mode Grüneisen parameters were determined using the observed Raman frequencies [22] of those phonon modes according to

$$
\gamma_{T}(P)=\partial ln v_{T}(P) / \partial ln V_{T}(P) \tag{1}
$$

as a function of pressure, as plotted in Fig. 1. Since the Raman frequencies of the $A_1(\text{TO}_1)$ and $\text{E(1TO)}$ modes decrease abruptly as $T_C$ is approached from the ferroelectric phase as observed experimentally [22], we normalized the isothermal mode Grüneisen parameter $(\gamma_{T}/\gamma_{Tmax})$ with respect to the maximum value (Fig. 1). This was done by analyzing the observed volume data [13] and the observed Raman frequencies of the $A_1(\text{TO}_1)$ and $\text{E(1TO)}$ soft modes at various pressures using the expressions, respectively, for the ferroelectric-paraelectric transition in $\text{PbTiO}_3$,

$$
V=a_{0}+a_{1} P+a_{2} P^{2} \tag{2}
$$

and

$$
v=b_{0}+b_{1} P+b_{2} P^{2} \tag{3}
$$

where $a_0$, $a_1$, $a_2$ (Eq. 2) and $b_0$, $b_1$, $b_2$ (Eq. 3) are constants. Their values are given in Table 1 (Eq. 2) and Table 2 (Eq. 3). Using the values of the ratio $\gamma_{T}/\gamma_{Tmax}$ as determined and the observed volume data [13], the Raman frequencies were calculated for the soft modes of $A_1(\text{TO}_1)$ and $\text{E(1TO)}$ by

$$
v_{T}(P)=v_{0} \exp \left[-\gamma_{T}(P) \ln \left(V(P) / V_{0}\right)\right] \tag{4}
$$

where $v_0$ and $V_0$ are the values of the Raman frequency and volume at ambient conditions ($P=0$, $T=300$ K). Eq. (4) was obtained from the definition of the isothermal mode

It is of interest to calculate the Raman frequencies of the soft modes in particular and the other modes studied here close to those suggested tricritical points in the monoclinic phase among the tetragonal, rhombohedral and cubic phases in $PbTiO_3$ [11] using the volume data by means of determining the mode Grüneisen parameters of the Raman modes studied as we calculated in this study. Continuous and discontinuous variations of the Raman frequencies of those modes with the pressure and/or temperature would then locate the tricritical (TCP) points in the T-P phase diagram of $PbTiO_3$.

### 4. Conclusions

The Raman frequencies were calculated as a function of pressure for various modes including the two soft modes using the observed volume data by determining the mode Grüneisen parameter close to the tetragonal-cubic transition in $PbTiO_3$. The mode Grüneisen parameters were determined at various pressures using the observed Raman frequencies. Mostly, they vary considerably and the energy shifts (Raman frequencies) decrease with increasing pressure toward the tetragonal-cubic transition in this ferroelectric material. One exception is the E(3TO) mode whose mode Grüneisen parameter ($\gamma \cong 2.5$) is almost independent of pressure within the pressure range of 0 to 11 GPa and its Raman frequency increases with increasing pressure, also partly for the E(3LO) mode, as also observed experimentally.

Our calculation of the Raman frequencies which decrease with pressure continuously as the volume of unit cell, indicates a second order transition rather than a first order from the tetragonal to cubic phase in $PbTiO_3$. The tricritical point(s) can be located from our calculations of the Raman frequencies between the tetragonal and cubic phases in $PbTiO_3$.
