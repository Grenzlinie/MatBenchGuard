# Computational study of hydrogen-induced conversion of peroxy linkage defects in amorphous silica

## Problem background
Silica optical fibers can develop peroxy linkage (POL, Si-O-O-Si) defects during manufacturing or under irradiation, degrading fiber performance. Hydrogen passivation is used to mitigate these defects, but the reaction can proceed along different paths, converting POL into either silanol (Si-OH) groups or a Si-OOH+Si-H configuration in certain electronic states. Understanding the conversion mechanisms and the resulting defect states is critical for predicting and improving laser-induced damage resistance in silica-based fiber optics.

## Approach
We use a fully computational pipeline. A 96-atom amorphous silica model is generated by classical molecular dynamics quenching from a crystalline supercell. Peroxy linkage (POL) and its hydrogen-induced defect configurations (silanol and Si-OOH+Si-H) are constructed. Density functional theory (DFT) with the GGA-PBE functional is used to optimize the geometries in both singlet and triplet states. Formation energies of the hydrogen-induced defects are computed from total energies relative to POL and H₂. Reaction pathways and the energy barriers for O-O and Si-O bond cleavage are mapped via the climbing-image nudged elastic band (CI-NEB) method. Finally, many-body perturbation theory (GW/BSE) is employed to obtain the quasiparticle electronic structure and the optical absorption spectra for all defect configurations. This allows a direct comparison of the two conversion paths and their impact on optical absorption in the 6–8 eV range.

## Reproduction target
Compute and report the following quantities for the hydrogen-passivation of the peroxy linkage defect in a 96-atom amorphous silica model:
- The formation energies (eV) of the silanol and Si-OOH+Si-H configurations relative to POL and H₂.
- The energy barriers (eV) for the initial O-O bond cleavage (leading to silanol) and Si-O bond cleavage (leading to Si-OOH+Si-H) obtained from CI-NEB.
- The optical absorption spectra in the range 0–10 eV for the POL, silanol, and Si-OOH+Si-H defects, allowing identification of characteristic absorption peak positions.

## Workflow steps

### Step 1: Generate amorphous silica model
- Role: process
- Action: Generate a 96-atom amorphous silica (a-SiO2) model by classical molecular dynamics quenching from a 2x2x2 crystal silica supercell using LAMMPS with the Tersoff potential and a Langevin thermostat, targeting a final density of ~2.20 g/cm^3.
- Evidence: `/app/outputs/amorphous_silica.xyz`

### Step 2: Construct defect configurations
- Role: process
- Action: From the amorphous model, build the initial POL defect by inserting an O-O bridge, then construct the silanol (Si-OH + Si-OH) and Si-OOH+Si-H defect configurations by modifying bonds as described. Also create a model of an isolated H2 molecule for energy reference.
- Evidence: none

### Step 3: DFT geometry optimization
- Role: process
- Action: Perform DFT structure relaxation of POL (singlet S0 and triplet T1), silanol, Si-OOH+Si-H, and an isolated H2 molecule using Quantum Espresso with the PBE functional. Converge forces to <0.01 eV/Å. Record total energies and optimized geometries.
- Evidence: `/app/outputs/dft_optimized_structures.xyz`

### Step 4: Calculate formation energies of hydrogen-induced defects
- Role: scored
- Action: Using the total energies from DFT optimization, compute formation energies of silanol and Si-OOH+Si-H relative to POL (S0) and H2: E_f = E(POL_S0) + E(H2) - E(defect). Output the two energies.
- Output file: `/app/outputs/formation_energies.json`
- Format: json
- Contract: {"silanol": <float>, "Si-OOH+Si-H": <float>}
- Scoring: scored by hidden verifier

### Step 5: Calculate CI-NEB reaction barriers
- Role: scored
- Action: Using ASE with Quantum Espresso as calculator, perform climbing-image nudged elastic band (CI-NEB) calculations for the two conversion paths: (a) POL + H2 → silanol (O-O bond cleavage), (b) POL + H2 → Si-OOH+Si-H (Si-O bond cleavage). Determine the energy barriers for the initial bond-breaking steps (O-O cleavage barrier, Si-O cleavage barrier).
- Output file: `/app/outputs/barriers.json`
- Format: json
- Contract: {"O-O_cleavage": <float>, "Si-O_cleavage": <float>}
- Scoring: scored by hidden verifier

### Step 6: GW-BSE optical absorption spectra
- Role: scored (load-bearing)
- Action: On the optimized structures of POL, silanol, and Si-OOH+Si-H from DFT optimization, run GW (G0W0) quasiparticle calculations followed by Bethe-Salpeter Equation (BSE) optical absorption calculations using Yambo. Compute absorption spectra in the range 0–10 eV and output the spectra with energy and absorption intensity for each defect.
- Output file: `/app/outputs/absorption_spectra.csv`
- Format: csv
- Contract: Columns: energy_eV, absorption_POL, absorption_silanol, absorption_SiOOH_SiH
- Scoring: scored by hidden verifier
