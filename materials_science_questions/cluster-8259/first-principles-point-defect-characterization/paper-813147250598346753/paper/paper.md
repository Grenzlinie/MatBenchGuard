# Effect of the $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect doping on properties of the semi-insulating GaAs

Deming Ma, $^{a)}$ Hongbo Qiao, Wei Shi, and Enling Li
Deparment of Applied Physics, Xi' an University of Technology, Xi' an 710054, China

(Received 21 December 2013; accepted 4 April 2014; published online 16 April 2014)

The different position $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ cluster defect doping in semi-insulating (SI) GaAs has been studied by first-principles calculation based on hybrid density functional theory. Our calculated results show that EL6 level is formed due to the $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect, which is very close to the experimental result. It provides the explanation of the absorption of laser with the wavelength beyond in semi-insulating GaAs. The formation energy of $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect is found to decrease from surface to interior gradually. The conduction band minima and valence band maxima of GaAs (001) surface with the $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect are all located at $\Gamma$ point, and some defect levels are produced in the forbidden band. In contrast, the conduction band minima and valence band maxima of GaAs with the interior $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect are not located at the same k-point, so it might involve the change of momentum in the electron transition process. The research will help strengthen the understanding of photoelectronic properties and effectively guide the preparation of the SI-GaAs materials. © 2014 AIP Publishing LLC. [http://dx.doi.org/10.1063/1.4871536]

## I. INTRODUCTION

The deep level defects are considered as traps and recombination centers in semi-insulating (SI) GaAs. They play a key role in the absorption and recombination properties of SI-GaAs materials and directly affect the photoelectronic characteristics of the electronic devices. $^{1,2}$ Some defects register as metastable behaviors and form peculiar low-temperature transient phenomenas, such as continuous photocurrent, photoconduction, and photosensitive effect. $^{3-6}$ The electrical characteristics of the SI-GaAs materials are sensitive to surface defects. Furthermore, its electrical properties can be controlled by defect levels' electrical compensation mechanism. In recent years, by a variety of theoretical calculations and technical means, the possible microstructures of the Energy Level (EL) level are studied, and the common deep-level defects are EL2 (Ec-0.82 eV) and EL6 (Ec-0.38 eV) levels. $^{7}$ Up to present, many stable defect models have been proposed, and these defects might exist in the form of clusters. The possible structure models of the EL2 level are $As_{Ga}V_{As}V_{Ga}, As_{Ga}As_{i}, As_{Ga}$ , and so on, whereas the possible structure models of the EL6 level are $V_{As}V_{Ga}, As_{Ga}V_{As}$ , and so on. $^{8-10}$ Based on preliminary studies of GaAs clusters structures and defects, $^{11,12}$ we systematically studied the effect of the $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect doping on properties of the semi-insulating GaAs by using first-principles density functional theory (DFT). Our study would be significant to the study of the photoelectric process, the output properties of optoelectronic devices, the photoconductive switches, and the other ultrafast optoelectronic devices of GaAs materials.

## II. COMPUTATIONAL METHODS

Our calculations are based on the DFT as implemented in the Vienna ab-initio simulation package (VASP) $^{13,14}$ code with modified Heyd-Scuseria-Ernzerhof (HSE) hybrid exchange-correlation functional. $^{16}$ In the HSE approach, a screening parameter of $0.2\mathring{A}^{-1}$ and a combination of 25% nonlocal Hartree-Fock exchange with 75% semilocal Generalized-Gradient-Approximation (GGA) of Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE) $^{15}$ exchange are used in our calculations, and we also apply the Projector-Augmented-Wave (PAW) $^{17-19}$ pseudopotentials. The special k-point sampling is Monkhorst-Pack scheme in the Brillouin-zone. A supercell approach is used to model the perfect GaAs and the different position $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect doping. We use a supercell structure consisting of 64 atoms, which is a reasonable compromise between accuracy and efficiency. After removing two adjacent Ga atom and As atom from the perfect supercell, we build the $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ cluster defect model. The Fermi level of the perfect GaAs is regarded as energy reference zero point. We study the effect of the surface and internal $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect on the band structure, electron density of states (DOS), formation energy and electron transition levels of the GaAs materials.

## B. Surface doping $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ defect
Fig. 2(a) shows the supercell structure model of GaAs (001) surface doping $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect. The crystal thickness and vacuum thickness are $8.24\mathring{A}$ and $20.00\mathring{A}$, respectively. $^{20}$ We set a thicker vacuum layer so that it will be helpful to the dipole correction. The length of the base vectors A and B are both $15.99\mathring{A}$, whereas the length of the base vector C is $28.24\mathring{A}$. Furthermore, the vector angles $\alpha$, $\beta$, and $\gamma$ are all $90^\circ$. Fig. 2(b) shows the band structure and the total DOS of the GaAs(001) surface $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect model, and the formation energy of the surface doping $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect is 6.28 eV.

Compared with Figs. 1(b) and 2(b), three acceptor defect levels (the red lines) appear on the top of valence band, and four donor defect levels emerge below the bottom of conduction band, due to the surface doping $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect, corresponding to the new defect peaks (the red lines) of the total DOS. These defect levels will generate lots of electron traps and increase the carrier recombination rate. Furthermore, the conduction band minima and valence band maxima of the surface $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ cluster defect level are all located at $\Gamma$ point; therefore, it might not involve the change of momentum in the electron transition process.

The surface is reconstructed owing to the dangling bonds of lattice surface, and the electrons of the surface states can exchange with the internal electrons and holes. Consequently, this will directly affect optoelectronic properties of the GaAs materials. Moreover, the lowest donor level of the surface $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect is observed to locate at 0.39 eV below conduction band from Fig. 2(b). This value is very close to the experimental result of the EL6 deep level (Ec-0.38 eV). $^{21}$ The GaAs (001) surface with the $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect might generate the EL6 defect level in the forbidden band.

## IV. CONCLUSIONS

In this study, we have carried out the different position $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect doping of the semi-insulating GaAs by first-principles calculation based on hybrid density functional theory. Our calculated results show that EL6 level is formed by the formation of $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect, which is in agreement with the experimental result. The formation energy of $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ cluster defect is gradually reduced from surface to interior. The conduction band minima and valence band maxima of GaAs (001) surface with the $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect are all located at $\Gamma$ point, and some defect levels are produced in the forbidden band. In contrast, the conduction band minima and valence band maxima of GaAs with the interior $V_{As}V_{Ga}$ complex defect are not located at the same $k$-point, so it might involve the change of momentum in the electron transition process. The research will help strengthen the understanding of photoelectronic properties and effectively guide the preparation of the SI-GaAs materials.
