SHORT COMMUNICATION

# Electronic energy calculation in three models of (La,Li) $\mathbf{Ti_2O_6}$ ionic conductor with vacancies

A. Ando · K. Nomoto · H. Nakagawa · N. Inoue

Received: 25 December 2008 / Revised: 25 February 2009 / Accepted: 29 March 2009 / Published online: 5 May 2009
© Springer-Verlag 2009

**Abstract** The electronic energy calculation using the linear-muffin-tin-orbital method is performed to study the property of Li ion diffusion in $La_{4/3-y}Li_{3y}Ti_2O_6$ (y=0.21) with vacancies. The total electronic energy of three models, $LiTi_2O_6$, $LaLiTi_2O_6$, and $La_2LiTi_4O_{12}$ shows the stable position of Li ions strongly dependent on each model, suggesting that the stable position and the activation energy change with the existence of vacancy.

**Keywords** Lithium ionic conductors ·
Electronic energy · Vacancies

## Introduction

The solid state Li ionic conductors $La_{4/3-y}Li_{3y}Ti_2O_6$ (LLTO) have been given considerable attention because of high ionic conductivity of $10^{-3}$ S/cm at room temperature [1–6]. Solid-state Li ionic conductors are useful materials for applications in solid-state, high-energy rechargeable batteries and sensors et al. Many studies were performed on the crystal structure by means of X-ray diffraction and neutron diffraction [2–12]. Inaguma et al. [12] showed the possible diffusion pathways and the bottleneck of 2d site in the $ab$ plane for SG $Cmmm$ in $La_{0.55}Li_{0.35}TiO_3$ and $La_{0.62}Li_{0.16}TiO_3$. The review on chemical composition and crystal structure data of LLTO was successfully summarized by Stramare et al. [13]. Ono et al. calculated the total electronic energy with the movement of Li ions and discussed the off-center position and the diffusion path of Li ions [14]. Catti [15] performed a first-principle calculation of LLTO models and showed the Li diffusion path. Inoue et al. showed the total BOP between the moving Li ion and the others, and also between the moving Na ion and the other ions on the $ab$ plane at $z=1/2$ by DV-X$\alpha$ cluster method using the model $La_8M_2Ti_2O_{11}$ (M = Li, Na) [16]. The diffusion path of Li ion and the trajectory of the Li ion path were very similar to the results by MD simulation in the literature [17–19]. However, until now, there has been no discussion about the diffusion of Li ions in LLTO with vacancy.

In the present study, we calculate the total electronic energy with the movement of Li ions in three models of LLTO with vacancies and discuss the stable position and the activation energy.

## Calculation method

The total electronic energy was calculated with the movement of Li ions along the $x$ axis. The total electronic energy for LLTO was calculated by the full-potential-linear-muffin-tin-orbital (LMTO) method [20]. We used three models for calculation. Figure 1 shows the three models. Model A is $LaLiTi_2O_6$ without vacancy, model B is $LiTi_2O_6$ with vacancies at 1a site, and model C is $La_2LiTi_4O_{12}$ with vacancies at 1b site. Each model was adopted from the tetragonal structure SG $P4/mmm$ of LLTO at $y=0.21$. A first principle calculation should be included in a periodic structure, so that the model C with vacancies was presented by twice cell to models A and B. The lattice parameters and the atomic positions used in the calculation were taken from the data discussed in the literature [6]. ## Results and discussion

Figure 2 shows the variation of the total electronic energy on the $ab$ plane along the $x$ axis in three models. The zero value in the total electronic energy was used as a standard. The coordinate of the Li ion indicates a relative scale of the lattice parameter $a$. The value of zero at $x$ means the position of 1b site in SG $P4/mmm$ and the value of 0.5 corresponds to the half position of lattice parameter $a$. The total electronic energy in model A firstly decreases, showing a minimum at about $x = 0.25$, and increases with increasing the position $x$. The maximum at $x = 0.5$ corresponds to the bottleneck by the oxygen ion at the atomic positions O4i (0.5, 0, 0.2393) and O1d (0.5, 0.5, 0.5). The total electronic energy in the model B first decreases, then increases with an increase in position $x$, showing a minimum at about $x = 0.15$. Compared with model A, the potential barrier in the model B becomes lower at $x = 0.0$, and becomes higher at $x = 0.5$. The total electronic energy in model C firstly shows a minimum at $x = 0.0$, then increases with an increase in position $x$. In this case, the stable position of Li ions is at $x = 0.0$, and the

Figure 3 shows the variation of the total electronic energy along the x axis on the ab plane in the model A as three parameters of the position z of oxygen O4i (0.5, 0, z). This suggests that the equilibrium position at $z=0.2393$ obtained by the X-ray diffraction is the most stable and easily activated position for the movement of the Li ion. ## Conclusion

The total electronic energy was calculated on the ab plane at $z=1/2$ by using the LMTO method in three models of (La, Li) $Ti_2O_6$ with vacancies. These results suggest that the stable position of Li ions and the potential barriers strongly depend on the existence of vacancy. Present results give us the reason why the Li ions can migrate along lattice axis and the off-site.
