
# Band structure of new superconducting AlB_{2}-like ternary silicides M(Al_{0.5}Si_{0.5})_{2} and M(Ga_{0.5}Si_{0.5})_{2} (M= Ca, Sr and Ba).

I.R. Shein \( ^{*} \) , N.I. Medvedeva and A.L. Ivanovskii

Institute of Solid State Chemistry, Ural Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences 620219, Ekaterinburg, Russia (Dated: 28 November 2002)

The electronic band structures of the new superconducting (with  \( T_{C} \)  up to 7.7K) ternary silicides  \( \mathrm{M}(\mathrm{A}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  (M= Ca, Sr, Ba; A= Al, Ga) in the  \( AlB_{2} \) -type structure have been investigated using the full-potential LMTO method. The calculations showed that the trend in transition temperatures doesn’t follow the changes in the density d-states at the Fermi level and probably is associated with phonon-mode frequencies.

* E-mail: shein@ihim.uran.ru

PACS numbers: 74.70.-b, 71.20.-b

The discovery of superconductivity (SC) in hexagonal AlB_{2}-like MgB_{2} (T_{c} \sim 39K)^{1} and creation of promising materials based thereon^{2-4} have attracted a great deal of interest in related compounds isostructural with magnesium diboride because of their potential as a new superconductors. One of the remarkable results is the synthesis by a floating zone and a Ar arc melting methods the series of the new ternary layered silicides Sr(Ga_{x}Si_{1-x})_{2}^{6}, Ca(Al_{0.5}Si_{0.5})_{2}^{7} and (Ca,Sr,Ba)(Ga_{x}Si_{1-x})_{2}^{7,8} with transition temperatures T_{c} ranging 3.3 - 7.7 K, except for Ba(Al_{x}Si_{1-x})_{2}. They have the AlB_{2}-type structure in which Si and Al,Ga atoms are arranged in honeycomb sheets and alkaline-earth metals are intercalated between them. Furthemore, a series of the compounds (Ca,Sr,Ba)(Al_{x}Si_{1-x})_{2} were synthesized by varying Al/Si (0.6<x<1.2)^{9} and the maximum T_{c} for these phases appears at the 1:1:1 composition.

Electrical resistivity and dc magnetization results \( ^{5-8} \)  revealed that these layered silicides are a type-II superconductors. The observed different  \( T_{c} \)  of these phases would be qualitatively attributed to the change in densities of states at  \( E_{F} \) ,  \( \mathrm{N}(E_{F})^{8} \) . The Seebeck coefficient measurements for  \( (\mathrm{Ca},\mathrm{Sr},\mathrm{Ba})(\mathrm{Al}_{x}\mathrm{Si}_{1-x})_{2}^{9} \)  indicate that their carriers are predominantly electrons, in contrast to the holes in magnesium diboride \( ^{1-4} \) .

Recently, the first band structure study of the layered silicides  \( \mathrm{Sr}(\mathrm{Ga}_{x}\mathrm{Si}_{1-x})_{2} \) , x = 0.375, 0.5, 0.625 and  \( \mathrm{Ca}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  has been performed \( ^{10} \) . It has been shown that the SC properties may be due to the high density of  \( (\mathrm{Ca},\mathrm{Sr}) \) -states at the Fermi level. In the present work we report the results of first-principles calculations for all known 1:1:1 ternary compounds  \( \mathrm{M}(\mathrm{A}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  (M=Ca, Sr, Ba; A=Al, Ga) and analyze the band structure parameters in association of SC properties for the isostructural and isoelectronic compounds:  \( \mathrm{Ca}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) ,  \( \mathrm{Sr}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) ,  \( \mathrm{Ba}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  and  \( \mathrm{Ca}(\mathrm{Ga}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) ,  \( \mathrm{Sr}(\mathrm{Ga}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) ,  \( \mathrm{Ba}(\mathrm{Ga}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) . The band structures of the above silicides were calculated by the scalar relativistic full-potential LMTO method \( ^{11} \) . The lattice parameters used are listed in the Table 1. Energy bands, total and site projected  \( \ell \) -decomposed densities of states (DOS, LDOS) of  \( \mathrm{M}(\mathrm{A}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  are presented in Figs. 1-7. Let us discuss the band structures of  \( \mathrm{M}(\mathrm{A}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  for example  \( \mathrm{Ca}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) . The valence band (VB) for  \( \mathrm{Ca}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  includes four fully occupied bands and has a width of about 10 eV. The quasi-core s-like band is located in the interval from 10.0 to 7.8 eV below the Fermi level and separated by a gap ( \( \sim 1.45 \)  eV) from the hybrid (Al,Si)sp-states which form four  \( \sigma(2p_{x,y}) \)  and two  \( \pi(p_{z}) \)  bands, Fig. 1. The E(k) dependence for  \( p_{x,y} \)  and  \( p_{z} \)  bands differs considerably. For  \( 2p_{x,y} \)  like bands the most pronounced dispersion of E(k) is observed along the direction  \( k_{x,y} \)  ( \( \Gamma \) -K of the Brillouin zone (BZ)). These bands are of the quasi two dimensional (2D) type. They form a quasi-flat zone along  \( k_{z} \)  ( \( \Gamma \) -A). The (Al,Si) \( p_{x,y} \)  orbitals participate strong covalent  \( \sigma \) -states to form 2D honeycomb network bonds of  \( sp^{2} \)  type with the s states. The (Al,Si) \( p_{z} \) -like bands are responsible for weaker  \( \pi(p_{z}) \)  interactions. These 3D-type bands have the maximum dispersion in the direction  \( k_{z} \)  ( \( \Gamma \) -A). The Cas,p,d-states are admixed to p-like bands. The  \( \sigma(p_{x,y}) \)  and  \( \pi(p_{z}) \)  bands intersect at the  \( \Gamma \)  point of the BZ. It is important that the (Al,Si)p-bands are located below  \( E_{F} \)  and do not contain hole states as well as those in non-superconducting  \( AlB_{2}^{2-4} \) , which is isoelectronic to the  \( \mathrm{Ca}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) . The main contribution to the  \( \mathrm{Ca}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  DOS in the vicinity of the Fermi level is made by the Ca3d-states: their contribution in  \( \mathrm{N}(\mathrm{E}_{F}) \)  is about 59 % compared with 9 % and 10% for Alp- and Sip-states, respectively. Let us compare the band structures of  \( \mathrm{Ca}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) ,  \( \mathrm{Sr}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  and  \( \mathrm{Ba}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) . The most obvious consequence of the alkaline-earth metal variation ( \( \mathrm{Ca} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Sr} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Ba} \) ) is the decreasing of VB width from 10.0 ( \( \mathrm{Ca}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) ) to  \( \sim 9.1 \)  eV ( \( \mathrm{Ba}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) ) caused by the increased cell volume. The location and dispersion of lowest d-bands depend from the alkaline-earth metals. As is seen from Fig.1, (Sr,Ba)d-states form the nearly flat bands in the direction L-H, close to  \( E_{F} \) . As a result for  \( \mathrm{Sr}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) ,  \( \mathrm{Ba}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  the sharp peaks in LDOS of (Sr,Ba)d-states hybridized with (Al,Si)-orbitals, appear which are separated by a pseudogap from the bonding p-bands (Figs. 2-4). It is important to note that the increase in  \( N(\mathrm{E}_{F}) \)  is due to simultaneously growth in LDOS of valence states for all atoms in silicates, see Table 1.

The band structures of  \( M(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  and  \( M(\mathrm{Ga}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  are similar, Fig.1. Their differences are revealed in an increase in the dispersion of  \( \sigma- \) ,  \( \pi \) -bands in the A-L-H directions an decrease in band gap (at  \( \sim1.0-0.9 \)  eV) between s- and p-like bands for  \( M(\mathrm{Ga}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  compared with  \( M(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) . The VB width of  \( M(\mathrm{Ga}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  increases by  \( \sim1.3-1.0 \)  eV. The change of the alkaline-earth metal in the sequence  \( Ca\longrightarrow Sr\longrightarrow Ba \)  causes the increase of the  \( N(\mathrm{E}_{F}) \) , the M d-states make the main contribution to near-Fermi DOS, Figs. 5-7, Table 1.

Thus, the band structure of the ternary  \( AlB_{2} \) -like silicides is quite different from SC  \( MgB_{2} \) . As compared by magnesium diboride, for  \( M(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  were found (i) the filling of the bonding  \( p_{x,y} \) -bands and the absence of  \( \sigma \) -holes, (ii) the increase of covalent interactions (due to p-d-hybridization) between graphene-like (Al,Si) or (Ga,Si) sheets and metal hexagonal layers and (iii) the principal change in the composition of  \( N(\mathrm{E}_{F}) \) , where the alkaline-earth metal d-states make the main contributions (55-60%, Table 1).

According to the experimental data \( ^{5-9} \) : (i) in the silicides  \( M(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  the  \( T_{c} \)  decreases monotonically when the metal M is changed from Ca to Ba; (ii) in the silicides  \( M(\mathrm{Ga}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  the  \( T_{c} \)  changes slightly (within range 3.9-5.1) with the maximum (5.1K) for  \( \mathrm{Sr}(\mathrm{Ga}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) , Table 1.

In framework of BCS theory, the  \( T_{c} \)  can be estimated by the McMillan equation  \( T_{c} \approx < \omega > \exp(\lambda) \) , where  \( \langle \omega \rangle \)  is the averaged phonon frequency (inversely proportional to the atomic masses),  \( \lambda \)  is the electron-phonon coupling constant ( \( \lambda = N(E_{F}) < I^{2} > / < M^{2} >, < I^{2} \)  is the electron-phonon matrix element, the value of  \( < M^{2} > \)  does not depend on mass and is determined by force constants).

Thus, the direct relationship  \( T_{c} \sim N(\mathrm{E}_{F}) \) , suggested in \( ^{7} \)  for the "strong stoichiometric" compositions of silicides  \( M(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  are impossible.

It may be supposed that the main factor, determining the variation in  \( T_{c} \)  in a number of isostructural and isoelectronic compounds  \( \mathrm{M}(\mathrm{Al}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \) , is the change of phonon frequencies depending from the atomic masses. Additionally, the shape of DOS (and the value of  \( \mathrm{N}(\mathrm{E}_{F}) \) ) may be changed owing to disorder in the distribution of  \( (\mathrm{Al},\mathrm{Ge})/\mathrm{Si} \)  atoms in honeycomb layers. As a result alkaline-earth metals will be in different trigonal-prismatic positions, that may lead to the splitting of near-Fermi bands and to a decrease in  \( \mathrm{N}(\mathrm{E}_{F}) \) . This effect will be more strong for Sr, Ba-containing silicides, where for "ideal ordering" state the  \( \mathrm{N}(\mathrm{E}_{F}) \)  is determined by narrow intensive peaks in DOS, Figs. 3,4,6,7. The possibility of chemical disordering and the inhomogeneity in the arc melted samples of silicides were noted by authors of \( ^{7} \) .

In summary, our calculations of the band structure of all known ternary silicides with the stoichiometry  \( \mathrm{M}(\mathrm{A}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  showed that the Fermi level is located in the region of a sharp DOS peak originated mainly from alkaline-earth metal d-states with some contributions of  \( (\mathrm{Al},\mathrm{Ge},\mathrm{Si}) \) p-orbitals. The  \( T_{c} \)  behavior for the isostructural and isoelectronic  \( \mathrm{M}(\mathrm{A}_{0.5}\mathrm{Si}_{0.5})_{2} \)  cannot be identified with the variation of  \( \mathrm{N}(\mathrm{E}_{F}) \) , and probably connected with phonon-mode frequencies determined by atomic masses.
