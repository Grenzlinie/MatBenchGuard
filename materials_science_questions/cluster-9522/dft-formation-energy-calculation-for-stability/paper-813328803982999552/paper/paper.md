First-principles Calculation of Carbides in Niobium Microalloyed Medium and High Carbon Steels

Dong Wang$^\text{a}$, Jianchun Cao$^\text{b}$, Xiaolong Zhou$^\text{c}$, Dongwei Zhao$^\text{d}$, Lei Deng$^\text{e}$

School of Material Science and Engineering, Kunming University of Science & Technology, Kunming Yunnan, 650093, China

**Keywords:** Carbide, Niobium steel, First-principle, binding energy, formation energy, density of state

**Abstract:** The binding energies, formation energies and DOS (density of state) of several carbides ($\text{Fe}_3\text{C}$, $\text{Fe}_2\text{C}$, $\text{Fe}_5\text{C}_2$, $\text{NbC}$) in Nb micro-alloyed steels are investigated using the first-principle pseudo potential plane-wave method. The results show that the structure type with the strongest alloying ability and the highest structure stability is NbC with fcc structure. After compared the DOS of these different structure types, the results show that the discrepancy in structural stability of carbides can be attributed to the difference in the bonding electron numbers at Feimi level. The less the valence electrons at Feimi level are, the better the structural stability of carbides. Therefore, the theory predicts that the existence form of Nb in medium and high carbon steels are the same as low carbon steels. Nb is still the effective alloying element in medium and high carbon steels.

## Introduction

The effect of niobium in steel is realized by grain refinement and precipitation strengthening for strengthening and toughening, at the same time the dissolved niobium can enhance hardenability, and delays phase transition from austenite to ferrite. After 30 years of research, China's steel output not only ranks the first in the world, but also niobium containing kinds are amounted to 200, are widely used in almost all varieties of steels .They are petroleum pipeline steel, automotive steel, construction steel, machinery, shipbuilding, steel for bridge , pressure vessel steel, stainless steel and special steels[1,2]. Because the carbon content can be reduced to improve the solubility of niobium in austenite, allowed to add more Nb. Therefore, the niobium microalloying technology is used in the research of low carbon steel, ultra low carbon steel [3, 4].

In recent years, the role of the Nb in medium high carbon steel is paid more attention by more people. Some studies have shown that, the undissolved niobium carbide in medium and high carbon steels can prevent grain growth ,and the dissolved niobium of austenite can make the pearliites transformation significantly delay, significantly increase the strength and toughness of steel[6,7,8]. Niobium in steel is mainly combinated with carbon, and the main form of carbon in steel is carbides. Four carbides $\text{Fe}_3\text{C}$, $\text{Fe}_2\text{C}$, $\text{Fe}_5\text{C}_2$, $\text{NbC}$ in niobium steel are studied in this paper using the first-principle pseudo potential plane-wave method. This research can enlarge a range of application for the niobium microalloying in steel, especially for the application of niobium in medium and high carbon steels such as non-quenched and tempered steel, spring steel, hard wire steel, die steel, has the theory guiding significance.

## Calculation Method

The buildup of model The possible existing carbides in niobium steel have orthogonal lattice $\theta\text{-Fe}_3\text{C}$, $\eta\text{-Fe}_2\text{C}$, monoclinic lattice $\text{x-Fe}_5\text{C}_2$, and face-centered cubic lattice NbC respectively belong to the space group Pnma, Pnnm, C12/C1, Fm-3m. In $\theta\text{-Fe}_3\text{C}$ crystal, each crystal cell contains 12 Fe atoms and 4 C atoms, the initial crystal package parameters are $\text{a=5.107Å,b=6.778Å,c=4.540Å}$；in $\eta\text{-Fe}_2\text{C}$ crystal, each cell contains 4 Fe atoms and 2 C atoms, the initial crystal packet parameters are

$\text{a=4.295Å,b=4.704Å,c=2.830Å}$；in $\text{x-Fe}_5\text{C}_2$ crystal, each crystal cell contains 10 Fe atoms and 4 C atoms, the initial crystal package parameter are $\text{a=b=6.2167Å,c=5.0595Å}$；in NbC crystal, each crystal cell contains 4 Nb atoms and 4 C atoms, the initial crystal package parameter are $\text{a=b=c=4.4647Å}$. Using MS software core module Visuallzer constructs four carbide structure models.

Calculation Methods and Basic Parameters Calculation work is achieved by CASTEP (Cambridge Serial Total Energy Package) module in MS (Material Studio) software. Exchange correlation energy is used by the PBE function under GGA, and the interaction pseudopotentialand between electron and ion select the ultrasoft pseudopotential of reciprocal space representations with the plane wave truncation energy is 300eV. The Brillouin zone integral calculation is using 8 x 8 x 8 Monkors-park special K-point to total Brillouin summation and the total energy is calculated by the self-consistent iteration (SCF) method, convergence precision in the iterative process for 1.0×10-6eV/atom. In order to get a stable and accurate result, first, we optimize the structure using the BFGS method to all kinds of models. the calculation accuracy is adopted 1.0×10-5eV/atom,in each atom force no greater than 0.3eV/nm,the maximum strain convergence accuracy for 0.05GPa, the maximum displacement convergence accuracy for 0.01nm.

After optimization of four carbides, the lattice constant $\text{A}_0$ and the unit cell volume $\text{V}_0$ are listed in table 1. The achieved theory lattice parameters and experimental value by geometry optimization are compared, considering the lattice thermal expansion effect, the calculation results agree with the experimental results. It shows that the selected calculation method and calculation parameters are suitable, and the result is reliable.

Binding Energy and Formation Energy Crystal binding strength and structure stability are in close relationship withbinding energies. Crystal binding energy is that crystal with the free atoms formed releases energy. It means the energy is that the crystal is decomposed into individual atoms to need to do work outside, so the larger the binding energy, the more stable crystal forms. Therefore, it works out the single atomic binding energy $E_{\text{coh}}$[10] of four carbide cells (or primitive cells) with the formula(1).

$$
E_{\mathrm{coh}}=\frac{1}{\mathrm{x}+\mathrm{y}}\left(E_{\mathrm{tot}}-\mathrm{x} E_{\mathrm{atom}}^{\mathrm{A}}-\mathrm{y} E_{\mathrm{atom}}^{\mathrm{B}}\right) \tag{1}
$$

Among them, $E_{\mathrm{atom}}^{\mathrm{A}}$ and $E_{\mathrm{atom}}^{\mathrm{B}}$ are the energies of A and B free atomic, x and y are the atomic number that A and B atoms are in the structure model of the crystal cells (or primitive cells). With the calculation conditions as the same as the carbide total energy, it works out the free atomic energies of

Fe, C and Nb are respectively -856.6552eV,-146.4217eV and -1541.2353eV. By the formula (1) calculated from four kinds of carbides, the single atomic binding energy $E_{coh}$ of the cells (or primitive cells) is shown in table 1. Comparing with the date in Table 1 it shows: the binding energies of $Fe_3C$ and $Fe_5C_2$ are -9.3835eV/atom and -9.3807eV/atom, the difference between the two is very small. The $Fe_2C$ structure $E_{coh}$=-8.9385eV/atom, is the smallest in four carbides, and the NbC structure $E_{coh}$=-10.7053eV/atom, is much larger than the other three carbides. It shows that NbC is the most stable in four carbides' structure.

For a further study about the nature characteristic difference of $Fe_3C, Fe_2C, Fe_5C_2, NbC$, using the formula (2), we calculate the formation energy $E_{form}$ [11] of the four carbides:

$$
E_{\text{form}}=\frac{1}{\mathrm{x}+\mathrm{y}}\ \left(E_{\text{tot}} \text{-x}E_{\text{solid}}^{\text{A}} \text{-y}E_{\text{solid}}^{\text{B}} \right) \tag{2}
$$

Among them, $E_{tot}$ is the total energy of the crystal cells (or primitive cells), $E_{\text{solid}}^{\text{A}}$ and $E_{\text{solid}}^{\text{B}}$ are average atomic energy of solid A and B, x and y are the atomic number that A and B atoms are in the structure model of the crystal cells (or primitive cells). When amorphous single atomic energy is calculated, the calculation way is the same pseudopotential as the total energy of the intermetallic compound crystal cells (or primitive cells). The single atomic energy calculated values of Fe, C and Nb are -865.5281eV,-155.8170eV and -1541.2353eV. By the formula (2) calculation of the formation energy $E_{form}$ about four carbides, the calculated results are shown in table 1. From the results in Table 1, we can get formation energy of $Fe_2C$ is positive; the formation energies of the three carbides $Fe_3C$, $Fe_5C_2$, NbC are negative. It suggests that the three carbides can form a stable structure, but relatively speaking, the formation energy of NbC is the largest. Comparing with other types of carbides, NbC has the strongest forming ability.

**density of state** In order to analyze electronic mechanism generated by the structural stability of four carbides, we have calculated the total density of state of all crystal cells (or primitive cells) about $Fe_3C, Fe_2C, Fe_5C_2$ and NbC and the partial density of state of the corresponding atomic. Figure 1a, 1b, 1c and 1d are the total density of state and the partial density of state of the average each atom about $Fe_3C, Fe_2C, Fe_5C_2$ and NbC structure. Figure 1 shows that bonding electron distribution of $Fe_3C$ and $Fe_5C_2$ mainly lie in: -15eV~-11eV, -8.5eV~3eV, and the valence electron contribution is basically: C（2s）, Fe（4s）, Fe（3d）; C（2p）, Fe（3d）, Fe（3p）, Fe（4s）. the bonding electron distribution of $Fe_2C$ mainly lies in: -14.5eV~-11eV, -8.5eV~12eV, the valence electron contribution is basically: C（2s）, Fe（4s）, Fe（3d）; C（2p）, Fe（3d）, Fe（3p）, Fe（4s）. The bonding electron distribution of NbC is mainly distributed in -58eV~-55eV, -35eV~-32eV, -17eV~-11eV, -10eV~6eV,the valence electron contribution are basically: Nb(5s); C（2s）, Nb(4p); C(2p), Nb(4d).

Through the electronic number $N(E_F)$ at the Fermi level $E_F$ to the total density of state about the average each atom of different structure to judge their valence electron structure activity. Namely the less $E_F$ electron number is, when external conditions change, the smaller crystal electronic structure probability change, so the higher the corresponding phase structure stability[12]. By the figure 1, we can see that the bonding electron number $N(E_F)$(electrons / eV) of average each atom in the Fermi level about $Fe_3C$, $Fe_2C$, $Fe_5C_2$ and NbC are: 26.0,4.1,20.4 and 5.6 , namely $Fe_2C$ and NbC are relatively smaller.

## Conclusions
In medium and high carbon steel containing niobium, there may be four carbides .They are $\text{Fe}_3\text{C}$, $\text{Fe}_2\text{C}$, $\text{Fe}_5\text{C}_2$ and NbC. Through the comparison of their binding energy, the formation energy and the states density, it is seen NbC has the highest structure stability and the strongest forming ability. The reason is that there are less C (2p), Nb (4d) bonding electrons at the Fermi level. Therefore, in medium and high carbon steel, NbC is more easily precipitate than Iron carbides, still has grain refinement and precipitation strengthening effect.
