# Soft-mode-driven structural transition and phonon stability analysis

## Problem background
TaReSi is a noncentrosymmetric compound that has been reported to crystallize in the orthorhombic Ima2 structure. Because the crystal symmetry can influence topological properties, it is important to know whether this structure is dynamically stable at low temperature. First‑principles phonon calculations can reveal whether the structure possesses unstable vibrational modes (soft modes), and if so, following those soft‑mode distortions can indicate a more stable alternative structure. This task investigates the dynamical stability of the Ima2 polymorph of TaReSi and, if instabilities exist, determines the atomic displacements along the soft‑mode eigenvectors and the relaxed low‑energy structure that results from the most relevant distortion.

## Approach
The stability analysis uses density‑functional‑theory (DFT) calculations with projector augmented‑wave (PAW) pseudopotentials and the generalized‑gradient approximation (GGA‑PBE). First the reported Ima2 structure is relaxed. Then the phonon dispersion is computed via the direct (Parlinski–Li–Kawazoe) method using a supercell to obtain the interatomic force constants; any imaginary‑frequency modes indicate dynamical instability. For the soft modes with the largest imaginary frequencies, a series of structures is generated by displacing the atoms along the corresponding eigenvectors at varying amplitudes, and the total energy per formula unit is evaluated. The energy‑minimising amplitude for each soft mode gives the atomic displacement magnitudes. The displaced structure corresponding to the S‑point soft mode is fully relaxed (lattice parameters and atomic positions) and its space‑group symmetry is determined. Finally, a phonon calculation on this relaxed structure checks whether it is dynamically stable (i.e., free of imaginary modes).

## Reproduction target
Starting from the reported Ima2 crystal structure of TaReSi, the goal is to:

1. Determine whether the Ima2 structure exhibits imaginary phonon modes at the Γ and S points.
2. Calculate the energy‑minimising atomic displacement magnitudes (in Å) for the soft‑mode distortions at Γ and S (reported separately for Ta, Re, and Si).
3. Produce the fully relaxed crystal structure obtained from the S‑point soft‑mode distortion and identify its space group.
4. Confirm that the relaxed structure is dynamically stable by computing its phonon dispersion and verifying that it has no imaginary modes.

## Workflow steps

### Step 1: DFT optimization of Ima2 TaReSi
- Role: process
- Action: Perform DFT structural optimization of the Ima2 unit cell using PAW/GGA-PBE. Relax lattice parameters and atomic positions until convergence. Produce a relaxed structure file as evidence.
- Evidence: `/app/outputs/ima2_relaxed_structure.cif`

### Step 2: Phonon dispersion and soft-mode identification for Ima2
- Role: scored
- Action: Build a 2×2×20 supercell (the conventional cell repeated 2×2×20, yielding 48 formula units) from the relaxed Ima2 structure, compute forces for displaced atoms using the reduced k‑grid of 3×3×5, and calculate the phonon dispersion via the direct method. Identify soft modes (imaginary frequencies) and report the instability summary, highlighting the Γ and S points.
- Output file: `/app/outputs/ima2_instability_summary.json`
- Format: json
- Contract: object with keys: has_imaginary_modes (boolean), imaginary_frequencies_at_qpoints (array of objects with qpoint [3 floats] and frequencies [array of floats]), minimum_frequency_cm (float).
- Scoring: scored by hidden verifier

### Step 3: Energy-vs-amplitude scan for soft modes
- Role: process
- Action: For each soft-mode eigenvector at Γ and S, generate a series of structures by displacing atoms with varying amplitudes. Compute total energy per formula unit for each amplitude to map the energy curve. Store the scan data.
- Evidence: `/app/outputs/energy_scan_data.json`

### Step 4: Extract energy-minimizing atomic displacements
- Role: scored (load-bearing)
- Action: From the energy-vs-amplitude curves, determine the atomic displacement magnitudes (in Å) for each element at the energy minima for the Γ-mode and S-mode distortions. Report these shifts from the original Ima2 positions.
- Output file: `/app/outputs/energy_minima.json`
- Format: json
- Contract: object with keys: gamma_mode (object with Ta, Re, Si displacement in Å), S_mode (object with Ta, Re, Si displacement in Å).
- Scoring: scored by hidden verifier

### Step 5: Relax S-mode-displaced structure and identify symmetry
- Role: scored
- Action: Fully relax the structure obtained by applying the S-mode energy-minimizing displacement. Optimize lattice parameters and atomic positions. Perform symmetry analysis to determine the space group. Output the final CIF file.
- Output file: `/app/outputs/S_mode_relaxed_structure.cif`
- Format: other
- Contract: Standard CIF file containing the relaxed structure.
- Scoring: scored by hidden verifier

### Step 6: Phonon calculation for S-mode-derived Cm structure
- Role: process
- Action: Compute the phonon dispersion for the relaxed Cm structure using the direct method. Save the dispersion data for verification.
- Evidence: `/app/outputs/cm_phonon_dispersion.dat`

### Step 7: Phonon stability report for Cm
- Role: scored
- Action: Analyze the phonon dispersion of the Cm structure and report whether any imaginary modes are present. Confirm dynamical stability.
- Output file: `/app/outputs/Cm_phonon_stability.json`
- Format: json
- Contract: object with keys: all_frequencies_positive (boolean), minimum_phonon_frequency_cm (float), no_imaginary_modes_confirmed (boolean).
- Scoring: scored by hidden verifier
